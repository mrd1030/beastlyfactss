READ THIS FIRST. AR-ar-addenda-mammals.txt stops mid-sentence in R1.03's *** footnote; the
registration deadline it cuts off is 30 June 2025, and the full footnote is below. R1.01 has no
amphibian section, but R1.02(A)(1) and R1.03(A)(1) both do. The axolotl is on R1.03 by name, and its
*** footnote restricts possession itself, not only new permits, to animals owned before 16 May 2024
and registered by 30 June 2025. R1.01(C) says R1.02 includes native species, which is R1.02(B)(1)
(in AR-ar-codebook-reptiles-alligator.txt): every native species on neither R1.01 nor R1.03 is a
permit-list species, so "prohibited until evaluated" reaches only a non-native animal on no list.

==== Addendum R1.02(A)(1), amphibians ====
      R1.02 Permit Required Captive Wildlife
      Species List
      A. Permits for breeding, sale, and importation may be issued for the
         following species in accordance with Codes 09.07 (Wildlife Breeder/
         Dealer Permit Required) and 09.10 (Wildlife Importation Permit
         Required):
          1. Amphibians
              ▪ African Bullfrogs (Genus Pyxicephalus)
              ▪ African Dwarf Aquatic Frog (Genus Hymenochirus)
              ▪ Australasian Treefrogs (Genera: Litoria, Nyctimystes,
                Ranoidea)
              ▪ False Brook Salamanders (Genus Isthmura)
              ▪ Fire-bellied Toads (Genus Bombina)
              ▪ Horned or Pacman Frog (Genus Ceratophrys spp.)
              ▪ Milk Treefrogs (Genus Trachycephalus)
              ▪ Poison Dart Frogs (Genera: Dendrobates, Epipedobates,
                Phyllobates, Oophaga, Ranitomeya)
              ▪ Tomato Frogs (Genus Dyscophus)

==== Addendum R1.03(A), opening and amphibians ====
R1.03 Prohibited Captive Wildlife Species List
A. The Commission will not issue permits for breeding, sale, or
   importation of the following species other than in accordance with the
   exceptions listed in Codes 09.07, 09.10, 09.11, and Addenda F1.03,
   F1.04 (possession of some species may be allowed in accordance with
   Codes 09.02, 09.05, 09.08, and 09.14):
    1. Amphibians
        ▪ Axolotl (Ambystoma mexicanum)***
        ▪ Newts, fire salamanders, etc. (Family Salamandridae) **

==== Addendum R1.03(B) and footnotes ====
      B. Species not listed in Addenda R1.01, R1.02, or R1.03 are prohibited
         until evaluated.

      * Issuance of Wildlife Importation Permits and Wildlife Breeder/Dealer
      Permits for these species is restricted to only those permittees permitted for
      these species prior to February 20, 2019.

      ** Issuance of Wildlife Importation Permits and Wildlife Breeder/Dealer
      Permits for these species is restricted to only those permittees permitted for
      these species prior to May 21, 2020.

      *** Legal possession of these species is restricted to only those owned prior
      to May 16, 2024, that register the animal with the Commission by June 30,
      2025.
