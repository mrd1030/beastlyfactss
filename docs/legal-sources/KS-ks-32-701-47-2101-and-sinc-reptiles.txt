==== K.S.A. 32-701(a), (e), (g), (r) (kslegislature.gov) ====
32-701. Wildlife and parks laws, definitions. As used in the wildlife and parks laws of this state,
unless the context otherwise requires or specifically defined otherwise: (a) "Big game animal" means
any antelope, deer or elk.
(e) "Furbearing animal" means any badger, beaver, bobcat, grey fox, lynx, marten, mink, muskrat,
opossum, otter, raccoon, red fox, spotted skunk, striped skunk, swift fox or weasel.
(g) "Game animal" means any big game animal, wild turkey or small game animal.
(r) "Small game" means any game bird, hare, rabbit or squirrel.

==== K.S.A. 47-2101(a) and definitions (kslegislature.gov) ====
47-2101. Raising domesticated deer; unlawful acts; criminal penalty; permit; identification of deer;
inspection of premises; communications with and assistance from the department of wildlife and
parks; definitions. (a) It shall be unlawful for any person to possess domesticated deer unless such
person has obtained from the animal health commissioner a domesticated deer permit. Application for
such permit shall be made in writing on a form provided by the commissioner. The permit period shall
be for the permit year ending on September 30 following the issuance date.
As used in this section: (1) "Deer" means any member of the family cervidae. (2) "Domesticated deer"
means any member of the family cervidae that was legally obtained and is being sold or raised in a
confined area for: (A) Breeding stock; (B) any carcass, skin or part of such animal; (C) exhibition;
or (D) companionship.
