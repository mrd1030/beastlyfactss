READ THIS FIRST. The Fis 800 chapter was READOPTED IN FULL by Document #14558, effective 21 April
2026, expiring 21 April 2036. Anything written about New Hampshire before that date is describing
a different rule.

New Hampshire runs the same three-way split twice, once for importation (Fis 803 against Table
800.1) and once for possession (Fis 804 against Table 800.2), and then closes it:

  Fis 804.04(b): "All species not specifically listed under the categories of non-controlled,
  prohibited, or controlled shall be designated as prohibited."

Fis 804.03(a) then says no permit shall be issued for anything prohibited under 804.04(b), and Fis
802.05(c), the waiver for unlisted species, is closed to permittee categories 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 7,
which leaves only exhibitors (category 5). So an unlisted species is a flat no for a private keeper.

The numbers in the tables are permittee categories from Fis 802.01: 1 individual, 2 propagator,
3 individual training and shooting, 4 regulated shooting area, 5 exhibitor, 6 aquaculturist,
7 field trial. A species marked 5 alone cannot be held privately at all.

What this closure does to invertebrates is the striking part. RSA 207:1, XXXV defines wildlife to
include INVERTEBRATES by name. Fis 804.02, the non-controlled possession list, has no invertebrate
in it. Table 800.2 runs amphibians, reptiles, fish, birds and mammals and stops. Fis 804.03(b)(1)
does have an invertebrates heading and it names five aquatic nuisance species, which shows the
Department writes invertebrate entries when it means to. So a pet tarantula, millipede or hissing
cockroach is prohibited in New Hampshire with no route for an individual.

Reptiles are the opposite: Fis 804.02(b)(4) clears exotic reptiles as a CLASS, excepting only
alligators, crocodiles and venomous species other than the western hognose. Indigenous reptiles come
off that clearance and go to Table 800.2, where two specimens of a category 1 species may be held
with no permit under 804.02(b)(5). The eastern milksnake, Lampropeltis triangulum triangulum, is
listed "1,5" in both tables.


==== RSA 207:1, XXXIII to XXXV ====
XXXIII. Wild Animals: All animals other than domestic animals.
XXXIV. Wild Birds: All birds other than domestic birds.
XXXV. Wildlife: Refers to all species of mammals, birds, fish, mollusks, crustaceans, amphibians,
invertebrates, reptiles or their progeny or eggs which, whether raised in captivity or not, are
normally found in a wild state.
