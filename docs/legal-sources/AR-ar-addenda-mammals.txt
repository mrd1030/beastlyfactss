READ THIS FIRST. AR-ar-agfc-codebook.txt promises "Addenda R1.01/R1.02/R1.03 in full" and carries only
their three headings, so the mammal rows are here. R1.01 clears exactly one canid entry, "Wolf/Dog
hybrids". R1.02 is the permit list and names the arctic fox, Canada lynx, kinkajou, ring-tailed coati,
white-nosed coati, serval and wolf. R1.03 refuses breeding, sale and importation permits for raccoons,
skunks, large carnivores (which Code 01.00 defines to include every bear) and red and gray foxes from
listed states. Its closing line makes anything on none of the three lists "prohibited until
evaluated", which is what decides an unlisted animal such as the Asian small-clawed otter. Native
pets are Code 09.14 (in AR-ar-agfc-codebook.txt): six per household, hand-captured raccoons allowed,
and hand-captured bobcats and foxes only if taken before 1 July 2022.

==== Code 01.00, selected definitions ====
     FURBEARERS - Badger, beaver, bobcat, coyote, gray fox, red fox, mink,
     muskrat, nutria, opossum, raccoon, river otter, spotted skunk, striped
     skunk, and weasel.
     GAFF - To take or attempt to take fish with a hand-held or handled hook.
     GAME ANIMALS - Alligator, black bear, bobcat, coyote, deer, elk, gray fox,
     red fox, mink, opossum, eastern cottontail rabbit, swamp rabbit, raccoon,
     gray squirrel, and fox squirrel.

     LARGE CARNIVORE - Mountain Lion (Puma concolor), Tigers (Panthera
     tigris), African Lions (Panthera leo), or any hybrid thereof, and all species of
     bears (family Ursuidae).
NONGAME WILDLIFE - All wildlife other than furbearing or game
animals, game birds, and game fish.
NON-NATIVE WILDLIFE - Any wildlife not defined as native wildlife.
