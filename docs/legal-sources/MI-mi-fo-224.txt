VERBATIM, from the official guide:

  "A fishing license is required to take amphibians and reptiles for personal use."

  "Reptiles and amphibians may not be bought, sold or offered for sale."

  Protected, may not be taken or possessed except as authorized by the DNR Director:
  "Blanding's, spotted, wood, and eastern box turtles; black rat snake, Butler's garter
   snake, eastern fox snake, copper-bellied water snake, Kirtland's snake, queen snake,
   smooth green snake, and eastern massasauga rattlesnake; boreal chorus frog,
   Blanchard's cricket frog, pickerel frog, and Fowler's toad; mudpuppy; small-mouthed
   salamander, marbled salamander, six-lined racerunner, western lesser siren, northern
   two-lined salamander, northern dusky salamander, and mink frog; all reptile eggs"

  Possession limits:
    Frogs, toads and salamanders: 10 combined daily and total
    Snapping turtles, season July 15 to September 15: 2 daily, 4 total
    Other turtles, snakes and lizards: 3 daily, 6 total, max 2 turtles daily and 4 total

  Scope of the order, which is what keeps non-native pets outside it:
  "It shall be unlawful to kill, take, trap, possess, buy, sell, offer to buy or sell,
   barter, or attempt to take, trap, possess or barter any state reptile or amphibian
   from the wild...except as provided within this order."

OTHER MICHIGAN REGIMES, verified this session:

  Large Carnivore Act, MCL 287.1102(f). A closed list:
  "any of the following cats of the Felidae family, whether wild or captive bred,
   including a hybrid cross with such a cat: a lion, a leopard (including a snow leopard
   or clouded leopard), a jaguar, a tiger, a cougar, a panther, or a cheetah; or a bear
   of a species that is native or nonnative to Michigan, whether wild or captive bred."
  Servals and Bengal cats are NOT on it.

  Permit to Hold Game in Captivity, NREPA Part 427, MCL 324.42701 et seq. Closed list,
  captive-bred game only:
  "badger, bobcat, red and gray fox, raccoon, coyote, beaver, otter, muskrat, mink,
   squirrels, striped skunk, woodchuck, opossum, ring-necked pheasant, bobwhite quail,
   Hungarian partridge, ducks, geese and wild turkeys"

  Animal Industry Act, MCL 287.731, with the MDARD import requirements:
  "Certificate of Veterinary Inspection is required for the import of exotic, circus and
   zoo animals, native wild animals, domestic rabbits, nonnative rodents, nonnative
   reptiles and amphibians, and non-poultry birds"
  MDARD does not issue entry permits for exotic, circus and zoo animals.
