Header of the document: "Species below are exceptions to Unrestricted Wild Animal List
status and require an importation and possession permit".

Read this together with the Unrestricted list. Several families are unrestricted as a
whole and then clawed back species by species here, which is how Vermont allows a ball
python but not a Burmese, a California kingsnake but not an eastern milk snake, and
every gecko but no Ambystoma salamander.

Regulation 881 is what makes restricted bite: the Commissioner shall not issue a permit
for keeping or possessing a wild animal in captivity except for bona fide scientific or
educational purposes. Restricted therefore means no pet, not extra paperwork.
