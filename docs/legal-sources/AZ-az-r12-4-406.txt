STRUCTURE

  R12-4-406(D)  Domestic animals, as defined under R12-4-401, are not subject to
                restrictions under A.R.S. Title 17, 12 A.A.C. 4, or Commission Orders.
  R12-4-401(8)  "Domestic" means an animal species that does not exist in the wild, and
                includes animal species that have only become feral after they were
                released by humans who held them in captivity or individuals or
                populations that escaped from human captivity.
  R12-4-406(E)  Hybrid wildlife that result from the interbreeding of at least one parent
                species of wildlife listed under this Section are regulated by this
                Section. No filial generation cutoff. This is what catches the Bengal.
  R12-4-407     Exemptions. Transit within 72 hours, fairs and circuses, photography,
                advertising, veterinary care, USDA-registered research facilities,
                pre-1989 desert tortoises. None of them is a pet route.

CURRENT RESTRICTED LISTS

  (F) Mammals: Afrosoricida; Artiodactyla families Antilocapridae, Bovidae, Cervidae,
      Tayassuidae; ALL of Carnivora; Chiroptera; genus Didelphis; Erinaceomorpha EXCEPT
      genus Atelerix, which are not restricted; Lagomorpha EXCEPT genus Oryctolagus,
      which are not wildlife and are not restricted; all nonhuman primates; Rodentia
      families Capromyidae, Castoridae, Dipodidae, Echimyidae, Erethizontidae,
      Geomyidae, Sciuridae; Soricomorpha; Xenarthra.
      NOTE the rodent families NOT listed: Caviidae (guinea pig, capybara), Cricetidae
      (hamster), Muridae (gerbil), Chinchillidae, Octodontidae (degu). All clear.

  (G) Birds: named Phasianidae species, plus all species listed under the Migratory Bird
      Treaty Act at 50 CFR 10.13. No psittacine appears. The only parrot Arizona names
      anywhere is its native thick-billed parrot.

  (H) Reptiles: order Crocodilia; family Chelydridae (snapping turtles); genus Gopherus
      (gopher and desert tortoises); Squamata families Atractaspidae, Elapidae,
      Helodermatidae, Hydrophiidae, Viperidae; and four colubrids by name, the brown tree
      snake, boomslang, keelback and twig snake.
      NOTE the absences: Varanidae, Teiidae, Pythonidae, Boidae, Trachemys, Terrapene.

  (I) Amphibians: Bufo horribilis, Bufo marinus, Bufo schneideri; genus Rana; genus
      Xenopus. Ambystoma is absent, so axolotl and tiger salamander are clear.

OUTER LIMIT

  A.R.S. 17-101(24): "Wildlife" means all wild mammals, wild birds and the nests or eggs
  thereof, reptiles, amphibians, mollusks, crustaceans and fish, including their eggs or
  spawn. Insects and arachnids are outside Title 17 entirely.

  Taking a native species alive from the wild is a separate regime: R12-4-404, R12-4-405
  and Commission Order 43, all requiring a hunting licence.
