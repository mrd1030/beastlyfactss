    ALABAMA DEPARTMENT OF CONSERVATION AND NATURAL RESOURCES
                       ADMINISTRATIVE CODE

                             CHAPTER 220-2
                        GAME AND FISH DIVISION


220-2-.06       Game Animals Designated.

The following animals are hereby designated as game animals: Bear,
Beaver, Coyote, Deer, Opossum, Eastern Cottontail and Swamp
Rabbit, Raccoon, Squirrel, Nutria, Fox, Mountain Lion (Cougar),
Groundhog, Bobcat, Red Wolf, Feral Swine (Wild Hog or Wild Pig),
and Alligator.
Author: N. Gunter Guy, Jr.
