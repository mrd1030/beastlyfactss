THIS IS THE DOCUMENT THAT DECIDES COLORADO. Chapter W-7's Appendix A turns on whether a species
is "native only to the tropical and subtropical region", which is a research question per
species, not a lookup. These lists are CPW answering that question itself, row by row, and they
are the agency applying its own rule rather than a secondary summary. Each row gives the
species and the regulation that decides it. The four verdicts are Domestic, Unregulated,
Appendix A/B, Appendix C, Regulated, and Prohibited; the regulation text in the last column
states which, so the tick-box columns do not need to be recovered from the PDF layout.

The list is expressly NOT exhaustive. CPW takes questions on unlisted species by email at
Wildlife.SpecialLicensing@state.co.us. Species absent from it that this map needed: axolotl
(Appendix A on the order Urodela listing), ackie monitor (recorded unclear on the map, because
Varanus acanthurus sits on the 26 S line the rule draws and CPW has published determinations
for five other monitors but not this one).

The two sentences from the page that do the most work:
  "Any species of wildlife that is not specifically listed in Chapter W-11 as Domestic or
   Unregulated or in Chapter W-07 as Appendix A or Appendix B, the species is regulated
   wildlife and a CPW license is required. CPW will not issue a license for private possession
   (eg pet) of regulated wildlife."
  "Monk Parakeet (aka Quaker Parrot) is a prohibited species and not allowed in Colorado."

Rows below are "<species type> <scientific name> <common name> <other common names>" then the
determination on the following line.
