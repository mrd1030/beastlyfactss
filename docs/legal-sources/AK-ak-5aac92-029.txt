READ THIS FIRST. Alaska is the strictest live-animal state in the country and the two things that
decide a cell here are the definition and the clean list, in that order.

AS 16.05.940(19) makes game "any species of bird, reptile, and mammal". Amphibians, fish and
invertebrates are outside it, so 5 AAC 92.029 never engages for them and they are free by default.
That is why the axolotl, the tarantula and the hissing cockroach are legal in the state where the
sugar glider is banned.

For a bird, reptile or mammal, § 92.029(a) bars possessing or importing live game without a permit
and § 92.029(c) then says the department "may not issue a permit ... for use as a pet". So the clean
list at § 92.029(b) is the whole answer, and absence from it is a flat no rather than a permit. The
route onto the list is § 92.029(h), clear and convincing evidence to the board.

The clean list is a roll call of individual species with one class-wide entry, "Any nonvenomous
reptile - Class Reptilia", which is what makes Alaska easy for a lizard or snake keeper and hard for
everyone else. Domesticated varieties are marked "Var.": European rabbit, white rat, white mice,
pigeon, chicken, canary, reindeer, ass.
