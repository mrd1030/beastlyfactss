Columns are animal group, order, suborder, family, genus, species, common name,
though not every row fills every column.

The rule that makes this list decisive: a species not found on the Restricted,
Unrestricted or Domestic species lists is considered Restricted. Vermont is an
inverted-list state like Hawaii, so absence from this list is a restriction.
