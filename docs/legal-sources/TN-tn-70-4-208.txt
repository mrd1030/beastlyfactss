WHY THIS MATTERS: § 70-4-208 sits in Part 2 of Chapter 4 (hunting and trapping), not in the
  Exotic Animals part (Part 4) that the five-class scheme lives in. The skunk cell was read as
  Class II (native furbearer, $10 permit) from 1660-01-18-.03(3)(b). This section bars the
  animal outright regardless of class: no import, no possession, no sale or transfer, with
  zoos and research institutions as the only exemption and a rehabilitation-and-release
  route for permitted rehabilitators. A specific statutory ban on the animal controls over a
  general rule-made class, so the skunk cell is banned, not permit.

Tennessee Code Title 70. Wildlife Resources § 70-4-208

(a) It is unlawful for any person to import, possess, or cause to be imported into this state any type of live skunk, or to sell, barter, exchange or otherwise transfer any live skunk, except that the prohibitions of this section shall not apply to bona fide zoological parks and research institutions.

(b) Notwithstanding subsection (a), a person who possesses a valid wildlife rehabilitation permit issued by the agency may receive skunks from the wild for the purposes of rehabilitation and release only.

(c) A violation of this section is a Class C misdemeanor.
