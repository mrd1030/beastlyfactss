### ND North Dakota (9 unread)

Sources already cited for this jurisdiction:

- `nd-36-01` North Dakota Century Code Chapter 36-01, Board of Animal Health <https://ndlegis.gov/cencode/t36c01.pdf>
  The chapter names only three restrictions relevant to pets: skunks and raccoons may not be kept at all, mountain lions and wolves need a tattooed identification number, and the board is to make rules on primates. § 36-01-08.1 says the board may require a licence for nontraditional livestock, meaning any wildlife held in confinement, but the wording is permissive.
- `nd-48-1` North Dakota Administrative Code Title 48.1, State Board of Animal Health <https://ndlegis.gov/information/acdata/pdf/48.1-13-02.pdf>
  The old title 48, which carried the nontraditional livestock category system, is repealed. What replaced it is a set of importation health chapters. Chapter 48.1-13-02 covers everything else and asks only for a certificate of veterinary inspection and an importation permit number from the state veterinarian.
