**2. Hub and encyclopedia**

Both earn their place. The hub is a real index of numbers sourced from the deep dives rather than a rewrite, and it says outright where sources disagree (humidity, feeding frequency) instead of flattening the disagreement into one false number. The encyclopedia's only overlap with the hub is size and difficulty; the morph-breeding history is unique to it.

No hard numeric conflict between hub and any deep dive that I could find; the hub's figures (cage size, temperatures, humidity, cost, lifespan) all match their source articles closely, including carrying over the caveat language ("some sources say 80 to 85°F is acceptable").

**3. The set as a whole**

Yes. Between these nine pages you could buy, set up, feed, handle, and watch for illness in a corn snake with real confidence, which is rare for a beginner-pet guide set. What's thinner: nothing tells you what to actually do the day you bring the snake home besides quarantine (the quarantine guide itself isn't in this set, just referenced). Nowhere in this set does an article give the actual quarantine setup steps, only the hub's one-line summary of duration.

One place a question is raised without a same-page answer: the vs-hognose guide states the corn snake's humidity range as a flat "40-60%" with no mention of the tank setup guide's "genuine disagreement" (some sources say 65-75%). A reader who lands on the comparison page first gets a simpler, slightly different number than the tank setup guide gives, with no link back explaining the discrepancy exists.

**4. Overlap and conflict across the deep dives**

Repeated across several pages: the thermostat rule, the 40-gallon breeder minimum, and the humidity range all appear near-identically in the hub, cost guide, tank setup guide, and vs-hognose guide. That's expected repetition for numbers a buyer needs more than once, not padding.

One real numeric conflict: the tank setup guide and vs-hognose guide both give corn snake humidity as "40-60%, or 65-75% per some sources," properly flagged as disputed in the tank setup guide. But the vs-hognose guide drops that caveat and just states "40-60%" as if settled, next to hognose's "30-50%." Quoting both: tank setup guide, "some specialist husbandry guides recommend a higher baseline around 65 to 75%... Many other sources and vets cite a lower 40 to 60% baseline," versus vs-hognose guide, "Humidity needs | 40-60% | 30-50%." Not a contradiction so much as one page quietly dropping the nuance the other page took care to flag.

The feeding guide is the one page that explicitly cites competing sources on both feeding frequency and "when to worry" and tells the reader which one it trusts and why, which is unusually transparent for this kind of content.

**5. Trust**

The math shown in the cost guide (mouse price per unit from a named supplier, kWh-to-dollars conversion at a stated national rate) reads like someone who actually priced it out, not boilerplate. The sentence that most convinced me a real keeper wrote this: "A corn snake in a large bare enclosure will use two hides and nothing else, which looks like proof it wanted a small enclosure and is actually proof it wanted cover." That's a specific, correct rebuttal to a real myth, not a generic enrichment tip.

What gave slight pause: the vs-hognose guide flattening the humidity disagreement that the tank setup guide takes pains to preserve.

**6. Grade: A-.** Exceptionally specific and honest about where sources disagree, held back only by the comparison guide simplifying a number the main guide correctly treats as unsettled. First two changes: make the vs-hognose guide's humidity row match the tank setup guide's caveat, and add the actual quarantine steps (not just duration) somewhere in this species' own set rather than only cross-linking to the shared quarantine guide.
