## quaker-parakeet

1. Page by page

Hub: Finish it, unusually thorough for a hub, legal status leads before any care detail. Act on: state list, cage size, diet floor, nest-box rule. Grade A.
Encyclopedia: Finish it, the invasion history (65,000 birds 1968-72, the JFK crate legend) is genuinely good. Act on: little directly. Grade B+.
Cost guide: Finish it. Act on: real itemized setup prices, vet cost table, the legal-check-first framing. Grade A.
Legal/handling guide (03): Finish it. Act on: the New Jersey permit trap, nest-building instinct, first-timer mistakes. Grade A.
Health issues guide: Finish it. Act on: fatty liver signs, feather-destructive behavior distinction, PBFD/psittacosis flags. Grade A-.
Cage setup guide: Finish it. Act on: cage size, bar spacing, the nest-box warning repeated a third time. Grade A- (some repetition with the handling guide, bordering on filler on the nest-box point specifically, though the warning is important enough to justify repeating once more).
Feeding guide: Finish it. Act on: 70% pellet floor, safe/unsafe foods, why seed causes fatty liver. Grade A.
Enrichment guide: Finish it, upfront about having no species-specific research. Act on: weaving material list, priority order. Grade A.

2. Hub and encyclopedia

Both earn their place. The hub leads with the legal question before any husbandry content, which is the right call for a species where ownership itself may be illegal, and it is not just a rehash: the state list, the emergency card, and the FAQ all add value beyond the deep dives. The encyclopedia's colony-nest and invasion history does not appear elsewhere. No numeric disagreement found between hub and linked articles: cage size (24x24x36 minimum, 30-36 better), quarantine (30-45 days), and the 70% pellet floor all match exactly across pages.

3. The set as a whole

Mostly yes, with one real gap: legality itself. Every article in this set defers the actual state-by-state legal detail to "Are Quaker Parrots Legal? The Bird Banned for Nest Building," a title that appears in every single sidebar Deep Dive list in this set but was not included among the files given here. The result is that a reader following only these eight files gets the headline (13 states ban it) repeated four times but never the full 52-jurisdiction breakdown the hub explicitly promises ("The legal guide works through all 52 against the state codes themselves"). That is the single biggest thing "still missing" after reading the whole set: a reader in a state not named in the 13-ban list (say, Ohio or Texas) has no way to confirm their own state's status from this set alone. Beyond that, a new owner can cage, feed, and enrich a quaker competently by the end of this set, and knows to check state law before buying, just not how to finish that check.

4. Overlap and conflict across deep dives

The nest-box warning ("never provide a permanent nest box") appears near-identically in the handling guide, cage setup guide, and enrichment guide, each time with the same reasoning (triggers hormonal aggression). That is deliberate reinforcement of the single most important behavioral rule for this species, not sloppy padding, though three full repetitions is on the edge of it. The 13-states-ban figure and the state list itself repeat word-for-word across the hub, cost guide, and legal/handling guide with no drift. No numeric conflict surfaced anywhere in this set.

5. Trust

The New Jersey detail in the legal/handling guide, citing the specific regulation numbers (N.J.A.C. 7:25-4.8(a), 4.9(a)3) and working through why a "permit" state is functionally a ban, is the sentence that most convinced me a real keeper or someone who had actually read the statute wrote this: "For a keeper, New Jersey is a ban." That is a level of specificity generic content does not reach. Nothing in the set raised doubt.

6. Grade and two changes

Grade: B+. Strong, consistent, unusually honest about legal complexity, held back only by leaving out the one article (the full legal guide) that every page in the set treats as essential reading. Two changes: (1) include the full 52-jurisdiction legal guide in this set, or at minimum give the complete state list somewhere in it rather than only the 13-state headline; (2) trim the nest-box warning from three full repetitions to one clear statement plus a cross-reference.

7. Not covered anywhere

- Legal status for any of the 34+ states not on the 13-ban list, the 3-permit list, Virginia, or Tennessee: the "legal guide" that promises this is referenced everywhere but not included in this set, so this remains unanswered even after checking the Health and More list, which only ever links out to the same missing article.
- None beyond that.
