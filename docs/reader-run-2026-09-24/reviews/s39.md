## platy

**1. Page by page**
- 00 hub: A. A tight digest with real numbers and a genuinely useful emergency card; nothing here reads padded.
- 01 encyclopedia: A-. The Gordon-Kosswig melanoma research history (1928-31, traced to the Xmrk gene in 1989) is substantial, non-repeated content that earns the separate page.
- 02 cost guide: A. Clean tables, dated price sources, and correctly flags "more platies than you started with" as the real hidden cost.
- 03 handling guide (breeding plan): A. Retitles "handling" honestly as a breeding-management page and delivers exactly that, with a real genetic detail (the sex-linked maturation gene) most care sites skip.
- 04 health issues guide: A. Covers the everyday ailments plus a careful, well-sourced (2024 Disease Models & Mechanisms review) debunking of the platy-tumor myth without either dismissing or overselling it.
- 05 tank setup guide: A. The footprint detail (a 10-gallon's 20x10 base misses the 24x12 minimum, a 20-long meets it) is the kind of specific, checkable fact a generic care site wouldn't bother including.
- 06 feeding guide: A. Correctly narrows in on overfeeding as the real risk rather than restating generic feeding advice, with a concrete visual tell (stringy waste).
- 07 enrichment guide: A-. Honest that there's no platy-specific research, borrows the guppy/molly harassment literature explicitly, and its priority list adds real structure rather than repeating the tank setup guide.

**2. Hub and encyclopedia**
The hub is a genuine digest, not a duplicate. Every number I checked (10 vs. 20-gallon footprint, 68-79°F with mid-70s target, pH 7.0-8.2/tolerating 6.8-8.5, 10-30 dGH, 2-4 year lifespan, $2-6 per fish, brood size 20-50 up to 80, sex ratio 2-3 females per male) matches its source deep dive exactly, including the more unusual specifics like the 24x12 inch footprint rule and the sex-linked maturation window (2 to 6.5 months). The encyclopedia doesn't repeat the hub at all; it earns its place with the melanoma research history, which the health guide then references without re-explaining, a good division of labor. I found no numeric disagreement between the hub and any deep dive.

**3. The set as a whole**
Yes. This is the most complete and consistent set I read: cycle the tank, hit the water chemistry, feed correctly, get the sex ratio right, and recognize the five or six things that can go wrong. What's genuinely missing from this set specifically: the tank setup and enrichment guides both tell you to cycle the tank "2 to 4 weeks fishless" but the actual cycling procedure (ammonia dosing, what to test for) is only in the linked "How to Cycle a Fish Tank" page, which is in the Health and More list, not this set, so a first-time owner following only these pages knows to cycle but not exactly how. Similarly, the breeding article raises "rehoming or selling fry" as a population option with no guidance on how, and nothing in this set explains water testing itself (what a liquid test kit result should read), though "Freshwater pH, GH, and KH" in the Health and More list likely covers that.

**4. Overlap and conflict across the deep dives**
Overlap is heavy but deliberate and non-repetitive in substance: tank size, temperature, sex ratio, and brood numbers appear in the hub, cost guide, breeding guide, tank setup, and enrichment articles, and every instance I checked was numerically identical. I did not find a contradiction anywhere in this set, which stands out against the other species reviewed alongside it.

**5. Trust**
The set's precision is what builds trust: a stated sex-linked maturation gene with actual measurements (21mm at 8 weeks vs. 38mm at 28 weeks), a named 2024 peer-reviewed review debunking the tumor myth, and dated, named retail sources for the cost tables. The sentence that most convinced me a real keeper wrote this: "The platy is the fish that survives the beginner mistakes, which is why it is the fish that gets them." That's an observation from someone who has actually watched a hardy fish get put in bad conditions precisely because it tolerated the first round of bad conditions.

**6. Grade: A.** Consistent numbers, no contradictions, and content that adds up rather than restating itself. The two changes I'd make: link the actual tank-cycling steps (ammonia dosing, what "done" looks like) directly into the tank setup guide rather than only naming the 2-4 week window and pointing elsewhere, and add a short "what to actually do with fry" note (rehoming venues, when to separate) to the breeding guide instead of leaving it as an unexplained option.

**7. Not covered anywhere**
- Step-by-step tank cycling instructions (ammonia dosing, when it's safe to add fish) are referenced but not present in this set.
- Practical guidance for what to do with unwanted fry (where to rehome or sell them) beyond naming it as an option.
