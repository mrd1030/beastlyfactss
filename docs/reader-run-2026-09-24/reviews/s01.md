# dog-medium-breed

1. Page-by-page

2. Hub vs encyclopedia
There is no encyclopedia page in this set, because "medium breed" is a size class, not a species. Nothing to compare or repeat. The hub earns its place on its own terms: it is a size-class summary, not a stand-in for a breed page.

3. Could I act on this?
Yes for the basics: bowl, crate, food type, exercise band (45 to 90 minutes), twice-daily feeding, and the health risks to watch (hip/elbow dysplasia, bloat in deep-chested types). What's missing is anything breed-specific: this page tells me my 55 lb Border Collie and 65 lb Labrador need "the same" care, but two sentences later it names Labs and Border Collies as having different bloat and dysplasia risk. It never resolves that tension by pointing me to an actual breed page, because none exists in this set.

4. Overlap and conflict across deep dives
No deep dives in this set. Nothing to compare.

5. Trust
Nothing here reads as fabricated, but the FAQ section is close to verbatim restatement of the body sections, which feels like padding to hit a Q&A quota rather than adding anything. The line that sounds most like it came from someone who actually owns one of these dogs: "Lack of exercise and enrichment is the leading cause of destructive behavior in medium breeds" — specific and it matches what every trainer says about this size class.

6. Grade: B. Solid, honest, generic-by-design page that does what a size-class hub should do. Two changes: link out to at least one specific medium-breed page (Border Collie, Golden) so the reader isn't left holding a range with no anchor, and cut the FAQ redundancy with the body text.

This is one of the few pieces of small-breed gear advice that is genuinely about anatomy rather than preference." That kind of qualifier, admitting most other advice isn't this well-grounded, reads like an honest writer rather than a content farm.

6. Grade: B+. Good anatomy-first reasoning, undercut by a size-class page pulling in one breed's health content without integration. Two changes: link explicitly to the Dachshund page from the enrichment section, and drop or explain the IVDD/Dachshund-Enrichment entries in the sidebar rather than mixing breed-specific into size-class.
