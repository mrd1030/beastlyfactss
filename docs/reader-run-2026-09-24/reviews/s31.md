## cardinal-tetra

**1. Page by page**
- 00 Hub: A. Finish it. Gives first-week numbers, buy list, emergency card, all sourced from the deep dives.
- 01 Encyclopedia: B+. Finish it. Short, but the naming-priority history (Schultz beating Myers and Weitzman into print) is real content found nowhere else.
- 02 Cost guide: A. Finish it. Real dollar ranges, a lifespan comparison, and it explains the wild-vs-farmed price gap instead of just stating it.
- 03 Handling guide: B. Finish it, but the group-size number (10, or 8-10) gets stated three separate times in a short article. Useful ID section (red stripe length) keeps it from being filler.
- 04 Health issues: A. Finish it. Four conditions, clear treatable/not-treatable split, ties back to the nitrite finding with a reason.
- 05 Tank setup: B (filler). Finish it, but two consecutive paragraphs both say "a 10-gallon tank is the bare minimum for 6" almost verbatim, which pads the tank-size section.
- 06 Feeding guide: A. Finish it. Concrete portions, a real overfeeding-to-nitrite link, and the dead-fish warning explained rather than just asserted.
- 07 Enrichment: A-. Finish it. Unusually honest that there's no cardinal-specific study and the group-size number is borrowed from neon tetra research.

**2. Hub and encyclopedia**
Both earn their place. The hub condenses numbers with sourcing per row rather than re-explaining them, and the encyclopedia's etymology story is unique. No numeric disagreement found: hub's pH 5.0-7.0, temp 77-82°F (73-84°F tolerated), lifespan "about five, some to ten," and 20-gallon-long-for-8-10 all match the deep dives word for word. The hub does simplify Seriously Fish's wider pH 3.5-7.5 comfort range down to 5.0-7.0, but that's compression, not contradiction, since the tank setup article itself recommends aiming for 5.0-7.0 too.

**3. Could I keep this animal after reading the set**
Yes, close to fully. Cycle the tank, hit 20-gallon-long with soft acidic water and gentle flow, stock 8-10, feed micro-pellet twice a day, quarantine new arrivals, and remove any dead fish immediately. Two gaps: the emergency card and health article both point to quarantine and a hospital tank but the actual quarantine duration and hospital-tank rules live in a different guide not in this set (Fish Quarantine and Treatment), so "how long" is answered outside this set even though the health article name-drops it directly ("settled by the hospital tank rules"). Second, water-change math (25-30% weekly, nitrate thresholds) is entirely on the hub's Health and More link, not restated or explained anywhere in the species set itself, so a reader who skips that one link is missing the only water-change instructions available.

**4. Overlap and conflict across deep dives**
Read twice or more: the Rio Negro wild-harvest conservation story (hub, encyclopedia, cost guide, enrichment all give a version of it), the 2008 Acta Amazonica ammonia/nitrite tolerance study (hub, cost guide, handling, health issues, tank setup, feeding all cite it), and the neon tetra disease description (health issues and feeding guide both give the same parasite name, symptom list, and "no cure, remove the body" instruction almost sentence for sentence). No numeric conflicts turned up. Tank setup does contain an internal near-duplicate: "A 10-gallon tank is workable for the bare minimum school of 6" appears, then two lines later, "A 10-gallon tank covers a school of 6 at minimum" restates the identical fact.

**5. Trust**
Nothing here reads as fabricated, and the sourcing (Seriously Fish, Practical Fishkeeping, Animal Diversity Web, Merck Veterinary Manual, a named 2008 Acta Amazonica paper) is specific enough to check. What builds trust most is the enrichment article's admission, in its own FAQ: "Is there enrichment research on cardinal tetras? A: Not on the species," followed by explaining it borrowed the neon tetra number instead of pretending the evidence was direct. A site padding for SEO does not volunteer that its central recommendation is extrapolated from a different species.

**6. Grade**
A-. The set is accurate, consistent across pages, and honest about the limits of its evidence; the only real drags are the redundant Rio Negro/2008-study retelling and the duplicated sentence in tank setup. Two changes: cut the tank setup article's repeated "10-gallon covers 6" sentence, and add the quarantine duration (30 days, already known to the health issues article) directly into this set's hub or health page instead of only cross-linking to an external guide.

**7. Not covered anywhere**
- Full water-change routine (percentage, frequency, nitrate-based adjustment) beyond the hub's one summary line, since the detail lives only in the external Aquarium Water Changes guide, not in this set. Covered on the Health and More list, so: covered, just outside this set.
- Exact quarantine duration and hospital-tank water rules: named as "settled by the hospital tank rules" but that guide is external to this set. Covered on the Health and More list.
- None left uncovered after checking the Health and More list; both gaps above resolve to guides that list names.
