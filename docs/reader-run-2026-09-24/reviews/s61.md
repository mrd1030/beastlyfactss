## sulcata-tortoise

1. Page by page:
- Hub: A. Everything a first week needs, with links, in one place. Would finish it.
- Encyclopedia: A. Short but not filler, the export history and 54.3 year longevity figure earn it a separate page from the hub.
- Cost guide: A. Real dollar figures with a check date and named retailers, can act on it immediately.
- Handling guide: A. Sexing cues, the two timing/size mistakes, tells you plainly this is not a pick-up pet as an adult.
- Health issues guide: A. Names two conflicting studies by author and year on pyramiding instead of picking a side, which is rare and welcome.
- Tank setup guide: A. Every number (size by age, temp, humidity, substrate depth) is here and matches the hub.
- Feeding guide: A. Schedule by age, supplement cadence, and the eight reasons for appetite loss, ranked by likelihood.
- Enrichment guide: A- (thin on native research, honestly flagged). Admits no study exists on this species and borrows from red-footed tortoises, which is more trustworthy than faking it.
- Vs. Russian tortoise: A. Clean comparison table, no filler.

2. The hub and encyclopedia don't repeat the deep dives; the hub compresses their numbers into a first-week checklist and the encyclopedia adds taxonomy, origin, and export history that no deep dive covers. One soft tension: the hub says "Lifespan: Commonly 70 to 100 years," while the encyclopedia says "Animal Diversity Web puts average captive longevity at 54.3 years, and 70 years or more is achievable with good care." Both can be true, but the hub's "commonly" oversells what the encyclopedia's own cited average says.

3. After this set you could legally acquire, house, feed, and handle a sulcata through adulthood, and you'd know the outdoor-space deadline is real and near. What's missing: the actual state legal rules and the tick certificate for interstate transport (the hub links "Is It Legal to Own a Sulcata Tortoise" but that page isn't in this set), and the UVB lighting deep dive referenced from the tank setup guide ("our complete guide to UVB lighting") isn't here either and isn't in the Health and More list. The six-month quarantine number appears on the hub with no explanation of why, but the Health and More list shows a Tortoise Herpesvirus guide that answers it, just not in front of you.

4. Overlap: shell pyramiding's cause is explained (with the same two studies) in the health guide, referenced again in the cost guide and feeding guide, and asked again in three separate FAQs. No numeric conflict, though the feeding guide's FAQ adds a detail the health guide doesn't have, that Wiesner & Iben's methodology "has itself been criticized," which reads like an unevenly distributed fact rather than a contradiction. Outdoor fencing depth (12 inches), basking temp (95-105°F), and the 80-100 sq ft outdoor minimum are repeated identically across the hub, tank setup, and enrichment guides. No numbers disagree.

5. Trust: the site cites Wiesner & Iben (2003) and Heinrich & Heinrich (2016) by name on a genuinely contested question rather than presenting one answer as settled, and the enrichment guide flatly states "Nothing on Centrochelys sulcata" rather than dressing up borrowed research as species-specific. The sentence that most reads like an actual keeper: "Almost nothing else sold as a pocket-sized pet grows into something so large, or outlives its owner so routinely," paired with the specific, checkable detail that transporting an 80-plus pound adult to a vet is "its own logistical challenge worth thinking through well before you actually need to do it."

6. Grade: A. Consistent numbers, honest about unresolved science, and structured so a buyer would actually plan for the outdoor transition instead of being surprised by it. Two changes: add the linked legal guide and UVB lighting guide to this species' own directory instead of leaving them as dead ends from this set's perspective, and soften the hub's "commonly 70 to 100 years" to match the encyclopedia's cited 54.3-year average so the two pages don't imply different numbers.

7. Not covered anywhere:
- State-by-state legal detail and the interstate tick certificate requirement.
- UVB bulb specifics and replacement schedule (the "complete guide to UVB lighting" is linked in text but isn't a Health/More item here).
