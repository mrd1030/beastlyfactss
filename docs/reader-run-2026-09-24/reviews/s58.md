## goldfish

1. Page by page

Hub: Finish it, dense and well organized. Act on: tank size by variety, filtration turnover, feeding portion. Grade A.
Encyclopedia: Finish it, the domestication history (Song dynasty pond, Ming-era indoor breeding) is genuinely interesting. Act on: little directly. Grade B.
Cost guide: Finish it. Act on: the real setup number ($150-400) versus the myth of a cheap pet. Grade A.
Handling guide: Finish it, short and to the point, but thin on its own (it restates the slime-coat point three times across sections and FAQ without adding new material). Act on: net-and-container method. Grade B (thin).
Health issues guide: Finish it. Act on: full symptom list per condition, with sourced dosing detail on flukes and anchor worm. Grade A.
Tank setup guide: Finish it. Act on: tank size by variety, filtration turnover, water parameters. Grade A.
Feeding guide: Finish it. Act on: schedule by life stage, pellet vs. flake vs. gel, safe-food tiers. Grade A.
Enrichment guide: Finish it, cites actual studies rather than folklore. Act on: substrate choice, plant attachment method. Grade A.
Bowl myth guide: Finish it. Act on: the real space-requirement table, the debunking of both myths. Grade A.

2. Hub and encyclopedia

Both earn their place rather than repeating the deep dives. The hub compresses seven articles into one usable checklist, and the encyclopedia's history (Jin dynasty color mutations, the 1162 imperial pond, Henry Bishop's Baltimore operation) appears nowhere else in the set. No numeric disagreement between the hub and its linked articles turned up: tank sizes, temperature range, and feeding portions all match exactly.

3. The set as a whole

Yes. This is the most complete of the four sets: a reader can size a tank correctly by variety, choose and turn over a filter, feed by life stage and format, spot every named disease, and enrich a tank with evidence-backed substrate and plants. The one gap the set itself creates and never closes: the feeding guide's own FAQ admits "sources genuinely diverge" on adult feeding frequency, one vet source says once daily, another says two to three, and the guide settles on "1 to 2 small meals" as a compromise rather than a resolved answer. That is an honest admission, not a hidden problem, but it does mean a new owner is told upfront there is no settled number. Nowhere in this set is the actual nitrogen cycle or fishless-cycling timeline spelled out; the tank setup guide says "cycle the tank before adding any fish" and points to a cycling guide that isn't part of this set, so a first-time owner following only these pages knows to cycle but not how.

4. Overlap and conflict across deep dives

The slime-coat mechanism (24-48 hour regeneration, infection risk) is repeated near-verbatim across the handling guide and the health guide, consistent rather than conflicting. Tank size numbers repeat across the hub, tank setup guide, and bowl myth guide without drift: 20+ gallons for one fancy, 55 for one common/comet, 40 for a shubunkin, matching exactly each time, down to the "+10 gallons per additional fish" rule. No numeric conflict found. The one place two articles lean different directions without contradicting: the tank setup guide calls a heater merely optional, while the cost guide lists one in its cost table as "optional, mainly to stabilize temperature," consistent framing, just phrased twice.

5. Trust

Nothing raised doubt. The line that most convinced me a real keeper wrote it: "A single goldfish can produce a bio-load comparable to several tropical fish combined, which is exactly why experienced keepers often run two separate filters on a goldfish tank as standard practice, not as backup equipment for when something breaks," from the tank setup guide, because it corrects a specific, common beginner assumption rather than repeating generic advice.

6. Grade and two changes

Grade: A. Comprehensive, internally consistent, and unusually well-sourced (Merck, UF/IFAS, and named studies on plant and substrate preference). Two changes: (1) settle or better flag the adult feeding-frequency disagreement instead of splitting the difference across two vet sources; (2) put the actual cycling numbers and timeline into this set rather than only linking out to a guide that is not included here.

7. Not covered anywhere

- The nitrogen cycle timeline and ammonia-dosing numbers for a new goldfish tank: referenced but not given in this set.
- A single settled number for adult feeding frequency: explicitly flagged as unresolved rather than answered.
- None beyond those two.
