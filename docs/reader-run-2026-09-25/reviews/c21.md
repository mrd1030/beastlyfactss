## box-turtle

**1. Pages**
- Care guide hub: every number sourced to its own article, plus a buy list and emergency card. Would finish. A
- Encyclopedia: short but carries real history, the 30,000-turtles-in-41-months Louisiana figure and the 1994 CITES push. A-
- Cost guide: dated, sourced price tables, a clear $345-453 setup and $40-70/month. A
- Handling guide: real technique, sexing cues, and the homing-instinct escape data. A
- Health issues guide: ties MBD, vitamin A deficiency, and respiratory infection together instead of listing them flat. A
- Tank setup guide: specific numbers for every variable, sourced to VCA and Zoo Med. A
- Feeding guide: clear age-based ratio and schedule, explains the oxalate vs goitrogen distinction properly. A
- Enrichment guide: builds around one real controlled study with numbers, not vibes. A-

**2. Conflicts**
None found. Brumation numbers in the hub, the health guide, and the sidebar brumation guide all agree: 10-14 day fast, 45-50°F, 10% weight-loss ceiling for this species specifically. Quarantine's "minimum six months" matches the sidebar herpesvirus guide exactly.

**3. Gaps**
- No thermostat appears anywhere in "what to buy," despite a basking dome being recommended and the shared heating/thermostat guide sitting in Health and More specifically to prevent burns. A new owner following only the buy list could run an unregulated heat lamp.
- Egg-laying and nest-site needs for a female box turtle are never mentioned on any of these pages. It's covered only in the shared Tortoise Sexing, Eggs, and Egg Binding guide, which nothing here links to or even references.
- Outdoor pen build detail beyond "bury the fencing 6-12 inches" lives only in the sidebar Outdoor Reptile Housing guide; the species pages recommend an outdoor pen twice without saying more.
- "Is It Legal to Own a Box Turtle?" is linked from every single page here but wasn't part of this review set.

**4. Trust**
Nothing overstated or ad-like. One small imprecision: the tank setup guide calls a "6% strength" T5 HO bulb "equivalent" to a "Zoo Med ReptiSun 5.0," which mixes two different manufacturers' rating scales as if they were the same number. Not dangerous, but a buyer comparing bulbs by the percentage on the box could get confused.

**5. Set grade: A-**
Every number I checked against the sidebar held up, and each page gives something a new owner can actually act on. It loses half a grade for the missing thermostat callout and the orphaned egg-laying topic. First two fixes: add a thermostat to the buy list and heating section, and link the egg/nest-site guide from the feeding or health page instead of leaving it stranded in Health and More only.

## red-eared-slider

**1. Pages**
- Care guide hub: thorough numbers, and the FAQ directly kills the "4-inch rule bans ownership" myth. A
- Encyclopedia: solid history, the 280,000-salmonella-infections figure and the 1975 FDA rule land well. A-
- Cost guide: detailed, honestly flags the "buy small, upgrade later" trap. A
- Handling guide: the best page in the set, gives a full escalation order for adding a second slider with real warning signs. A
- Health issues guide: clearly separates MBD from pyramiding, a distinction most care writing blurs, and cites the study that tested it. A
- Tank setup guide: specific sizes, temps, filtration, and a real nesting-tub protocol for a gravid female. A
- Feeding guide: the "why they stop eating" list is genuinely diagnostic, seven causes ranked by likelihood. A
- Enrichment guide: good research and a clear priority order, but see Trust below. B+

**2. Conflicts**
None in the numbers. One integrity issue: the enrichment guide's "chelonian enrichment study" sentence describes the Case, Lewbart and Doerr eastern box turtle study by name, but its link points to /blog/red-footed-tortoise-enrichment-guide/, not the box turtle article that actually reports it. Anyone who clicks through lands on the wrong species' page.

**3. Gaps**
- No outdoor pond guide is linked anywhere in this set's sidebar, though the tank setup and enrichment pages recommend "a secure, predator-proof outdoor pond" three separate times. Nothing here explains how to actually build one.
- No thermostat in the buy list despite both a submersible heater and a basking lamp, and the shared heating/thermostat guide going unused in Health and More.
- The three-to-six-month quarantine figure for a second slider links to the general snakes-and-lizards quarantine guide, which explicitly says tortoises and box turtles should use the chelonian herpesvirus protocol instead. It never says which protocol an aquatic turtle should follow, leaving the actual duration for this species ambiguous.

**4. Trust**
The mismatched enrichment link above is the real problem here, it reads like content copied from another species' page without checking where the link actually goes. Otherwise sourcing is properly attributed by name (VCA, LafeberVet, Animal Diversity Web, a named 2025 PLOS ONE paper) and nothing reads like a product pitch.

**5. Set grade: B+**
The husbandry guidance is accurate and practical everywhere I checked it, but the outdoor-pond gap and the broken enrichment link are concrete, fixable problems a real reader would hit. First two fixes: point "chelonian enrichment study" at the box turtle enrichment guide instead of the red-footed tortoise one, and add or link an actual outdoor pond guide since the pages recommend one repeatedly without explaining it.

## red-footed-tortoise

**1. Pages**
- Care guide hub: thorough, and the "opposite of a Russian tortoise" framing runs consistently through every section. A
- Encyclopedia: strong history, the Lenten pastel de morrocoy detail and the CITES export numbers are real specifics. A
- Cost guide: detailed, but leans on one named breeder and one named product (see Trust). B+
- Handling guide: clear on shell-injury risk and gives an actual growth chart tied to enclosure planning. A
- Health issues guide: the "dry and cool causes respiratory infection here, not damp" reversal is explained clearly and repeated usefully. A
- Tank setup guide: excellent, includes an actual lamp-height table for two different UVB bulb strengths. A
- Feeding guide: clear 90/10 split and makes the protein-isn't-optional case specifically, not generically. A
- Enrichment guide: genuinely interesting cited cognition research, not padding. A

**2. Conflicts**
None found. Humidity, pyramiding, and protein numbers agree across the set, and match the sidebar soaking and quarantine guides where those overlap.

**3. Gaps**
- No guidance anywhere on housing more than one red-footed tortoise together. The red-eared slider set has a detailed cohabitation section; this set has nothing, for a reader considering a pair.
- Breeding, egg-laying, or nest-site needs for a female are never mentioned on the species pages. It's covered only in the shared Tortoise Sexing, Eggs, and Egg Binding guide, which nothing here links to.
- "Is It Legal to Own a Red-Footed Tortoise?" is referenced constantly but wasn't part of this review set.

**4. Trust**
The cost guide names a specific breeder ("Florida Iguana & Tortoise Breeders," down to their "Andrews" bloodline) and recommends a specific misting system by brand twice ("MistKing Starter"). Neither claim looks false, but naming one seller and one product this specifically inside an affiliate-disclosed article reads closer to a product plug than a neutral cost breakdown.

**5. Set grade: A-**
The husbandry logic is consistently well explained and internally consistent, the strongest of the three sets on "opposite of what you'd assume" clarity. It loses ground for the breeder/brand namedrops undercutting the cost guide's neutrality, and for the missing cohabitation guidance. First two fixes: rewrite the breeder and MistKing mentions as generic examples, and add a line on multi-tortoise housing, even if the answer is "keep them solitary."
