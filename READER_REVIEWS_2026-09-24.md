Source: 95 reader reviews in `.reader/r0924-out/` (s01 to s70 species care sets, b01 to b25 bundles). c08 was skipped (duplicates s61 to s64). Legal guides were excluded from this run on purpose, so every "legal page missing" flag is ignored below. One set grade (quaker parakeet, B+) was lowered only for the missing legal guide; on content alone it reads as A-.

Grades are the readers' own. "NG" means the reader commented on a page without giving it a letter.

### The 5 most important findings

1. **The care guides are strong and readers trust them.** 87% of all pages got A or A-. Readers repeatedly praised dated prices, named sources, and the site saying "no study exists on this species" instead of inventing one. Only 2 species care pages fell to C (corydoras handling, tiger salamander vs. axolotl).
2. **Cross-page number drift is the main defect, and it is fixable.** Readers quoted 83 concrete conflicts (section 4). Lifespan is the worst offender (19 rows across 18 sets), usually encyclopedia vs. hub and cost guide. Cost guides have arithmetic that does not match their own tables (iguana, pacman frog, fire skink, fire-bellied toad, cockatoo, bearded dragon, degu, hognose). "Vs." comparison pages drift from the species' own guides (tiger salamander, chinchilla, cockatiel, tarantula, corn snake, degu).
3. **The weak pages are almost all outside the care guides.** 8 of the 11 C/D pages sit in dogs-cats-general-4 and the wild animal bundles (one more is a Chronicles story): generic "why X needs Y" posts, hype listicles, an unfinished axolotl profile. These are the pages most likely to read as "low value content" to an AdSense reviewer.
4. **Three page types underperform within care sets.** Encyclopedias (40% at B+ or lower, often restating hub stats on dog and cat pages), handling guides for animals that are not handled (fish, shrimp, inverts, tortoises), and enrichment guides that repeat the tank setup guide.
5. **The same gaps recur across species.** Sexing (17 sets), introducing or pairing animals (18), quarantine and day-one steps (13), health follow-through after a diagnosis (13), equipment spec numbers (16), grooming and bathing how-to (11). Template repetition (the same comparison blurb pasted into 5 articles, the same fun fact 3 or 4 times, identical FAQ answers on every page) was noticed in about 20 sets.

## 2. By guide type

"Tank setup" includes cage, enclosure, housing and pond guides. Counts of sets are counts of species sets whose reader raised the item.

### Hub (108 pages): typical grade A (100 A, 4 A-, 3 B+, 1 B)

- **Praised:** works as a router, every number tied to a source page, emergency card, buy list, first-week numbers. Readers checked hub numbers against deep dives and usually found exact matches.
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. Hub flattens nuance its own deep dive keeps (hatchling feeding tier, gut-load window, feeding frequency, "no insects ever", lifespan "commonly 70 to 100"): 11 sets
  2. Quarantine clock not tied to taming/handling clock, day-one steps: 5 sets
  3. Size-class and universal dog/cat hubs never link to breed pages: 3 sets
- **Recurring problems:** hub fun facts that overshoot the site's own sourced content (African grey "over 1,000 words"); hub budget lines that don't reconcile (pacman frog, cockatoo, fire skink); medium-breed FAQ restating the body verbatim.

### Encyclopedia (103 pages): typical grade A- (40 A, 22 A-, 26 B+, 15 B). The weakest page type.

- **Praised:** unique history nowhere else on the site (CITES votes and quotas, naming history, trade and invasion history, founding populations). Readers called this "real content, not filler".
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. Lifespan, size or origin figure disagrees with hub, cost or another guide, often with no note that one is wild and one is captive: 20 sets
  2. Nothing actionable, pure background (accepted by most readers, graded B): 15 pages
- **Recurring problems:** dog and cat encyclopedias repeat the hub's headline statistic near verbatim (maine coon, rottweiler, sphynx, bulldog, golden, dachshund, labrador, ASH, DSH, scottish fold). Readers asked for one clear cross-reference instead.

### Cost (84 pages): typical grade A (75 A, 3 A-, 1 B+, 4 B, 1 B-)

- **Praised:** dated price checks with named retailers, "budget for the adult, not the hatchling", honest that the animal is the cheap part.
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. Dollar figures for specific procedures (IVDD surgery, spay/neuter, tumor removal, cesarean, deslorelin implant, quarantine fecal): 8 sets
  2. Real total when the priced kit is the wrong kit (dimming thermostat unpriced), cost split by diet type: 2 sets
- **Recurring problems:** arithmetic that does not match its own table, 8 sets (iguana, pacman frog, fire skink, fire-bellied toad, cockatoo, bearded dragon, degu, hognose). This is the most concentrated fixable error class on the site.

### Handling (84 pages): typical grade A (64 A, 8 A-, 6 B+, 5 B, 1 C)

- **Praised:** body-language tables, stress signal checklists, tail autotomy and bite guidance, honest "this is a display animal".
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. How to sex the animal: 17 sets (most often raised here or in health)
  2. How to introduce a new animal or pair (timeline, signs to separate): 18 sets
- **Recurring problems:** "handling" guides for animals that are not handled restate hub facts to fill length: corydoras (C), jackson's chameleon, cardinal tetra, goldfish, russian tortoise, praying mantis (B); amano, molly, bristlenose, swordtail, rosy boa (B+). Readers suggested reframing them around netting, acclimation or observation. Ghost shrimp's handling guide was praised for doing exactly that.

### Health issues (84 pages): typical grade A (71 A, 8 A-, 1 B+, 2 B, 2 NG)

- **Praised:** home-vs-vet triage, myth corrections (hedgehog WHS 3% not 10%), every condition tied to a husbandry cause.
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. What happens after a diagnosis (HCM treatment, amyloidosis plan, MDR1 drug list, stuck molt home action, copper remediation, metronidazole dose, named parasite, dangerous antibiotics): 13 sets
  2. How to find or vet an exotic, avian, aquatic or reptile vet: 5 sets
- **Recurring problems:** boa health guide has zero citations and ends every section with the same "always see a vet" line (B). Health and feeding guides disagree on confidence level for the same cause (pyramiding, sand impaction, barbel erosion).

### Tank setup (84 pages): typical grade A (73 A, 4 A-, 3 B+, 4 B)

- **Praised:** disclosed source disagreements (humidity, heating, square footage) with the site's pick explained.
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. Equipment spec numbers (filter GPH, chiller sizing, night heat source, bulb wattage, UVB choice, substrate depth, screen gap, dig zone temperature, salt mix ratio): 16 sets
  2. Cleaning and substrate maintenance schedules: 5 sets
- **Recurring problems:** duplicated paragraphs back to back (neon tetra, cardinal tetra); a table row that undercuts itself (crested gecko basking "82-85°F (some sources: 72-75°F)"); a setup guide that contradicts its own FAQ (degu cage size); "Tank Setup" label on bird and mammal cage guides.

### Feeding (85 pages): typical grade A (79 A, 5 A-, 1 B+). The strongest care page type.

- **Praised:** "why it stopped eating" lists, life-stage tables, honest "sources disagree" notes.
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. Portion amount by weight (dogs especially: beagle, husky, bulldog), supplement dose, egg food amount: 8 sets
  2. Which of two disputed schedules the hub follows: 4 sets
- **Recurring problems:** golden retriever feeding has a generic second half that fits any breed (B+, "some filler"); feeding and hub disagree on frequency wording (box turtle, swordtail, betta, tokay).

### Enrichment (87 pages): typical grade A (52 A, 22 A-, 6 B+, 6 B, 1 B-)

- **Praised more than any other type for honesty:** "no study exists on this species", named borrowed studies with caveats, null results reported.
- **Recurring missing information:**
  1. Body condition reference the page tells you to use: 2 sets
  2. Session frequency for training or puzzle feeding: 1 set
- **Recurring problems:** restating tank setup instead of adding new material, 7 sets (red-eared slider, uromastyx, millipede, budgie, crested gecko, amano shrimp, ghost shrimp). Generic parrot advice on the budgie page. Unsourced "decapod sentience review" section on ghost shrimp.

### Species "other" deep dives (37 pages: vs. pages, special topics): typical grade A (21 A, 11 A-, 1 B+, 2 B, 1 C, 1 NG)

- **Praised:** special topic pages are among the best on the site (rabbit GI stasis, guinea pig scurvy, bearded dragon brumation, eggs and growth, crested gecko humidity, leopard gecko temperature, betta water parameters).
- **Recurring problems:** comparison pages restate numbers with less care than the main guides, 7 sets (tiger salamander vs. axolotl C, degu vs. chinchilla, tarantula vs. scorpion, chinchilla vs. hamster, cockatiel vs. cockatoo, corn vs. hognose, African grey vs. cockatoo). Koi vs. goldfish reruns cost/setup numbers (B, filler). Ferret adrenal guide duplicates the health guide (B, thin).

### Bundles: shared class guides (65 pages in b01 to b09, b19 to b21): typical grade A (53 A, 7 A-, 3 B+, 2 B)

- **Praised:** the benchmark content on the site (fish quarantine, pH/GH/KH, invertebrate pesticides, rehousing, enterotoxemia, bird body language, egg laying, tortoise herpesvirus, heating and thermostats).
- **Recurring missing information:** title scope wider than content (amphibian hardness only for axolotls, choosing a bird lifespan table only 2 species, T5 UVB table only 3 groups, travel section thin in bird emergency plan, no max basking surface temperature in the heating guide).
- **Recurring problems:** "Why Bearded Dragons Need UVB" (B, filler, no named sources); bioactive dragon guide (B, anecdote and affiliate driven); T5 vs. compact (B+, promotional); misfiled tegu feeding in the aquarium bundle; gut-loading page carrying a White's tree frog sidebar.

### Bundles: dog and cat health (29 pages in b12 to b15): typical grade A- (14 A, 7 A-, 4 B, 1 B-, 3 C)

- **Praised:** IVDD, eye disease, dental, kidney, BOAS, cardiomyopathy, DNA test and BCS guides correct their field's myths with odds ratios and sample sizes.
- **Recurring problems:** dogs-cats-general-4 is the weakest bundle on the site (B-): three C pages of generic copy, unsourced numbers, an empty heading. Golden vs. GSD omits bloat for the GSD though the site's bloat guide lists it as top risk. Affiliate plug mid-sentence in "10 Surprising Golden Retriever Facts". Succession planning (about cockatoos and tortoises) filed under dogs and cats.

### Bundles: overviews (24 pages in b17 to b18): typical grade A (24 A)

- **Praised:** every piece delivers one checkable surprise per species and links to real guides.
- **Recurring problems:** the tegu paragraph appears near verbatim in 3 overviews; two entries cover only two species and read thin next to the four-species format.

### Bundles: wild animals and fun facts (68 pages in b16, b22 to b25): typical grade A (51 A, 1 A-, 11 B, 4 C, 1 D)

- **Praised:** myth-correcting wildlife pieces (constriction, dolphin names, wolverine, pangolin, tardigrade, giraffe heart) are some of the most trusted writing on the site.
- **Recurring problems:** 5 of the 11 lowest pages are here. Six fun-facts-2 pages have no care guide link and empty sidebars. Every page in wild-2, wild-4 and wild-6 shows "none" for both sidebar lists. Generic pet-care copy in wild-5 (rabbits hay, axolotls cold water, parrots social) is far below the site's standard.

### Bundles: Chronicles (30 pages in b10 to b11): typical grade A- (12 A, 10 A-, 4 B+, 3 B, 1 C)

- **Praised:** accurate animal behavior (shedding, owl freeze, cat pathogen reason for the glass), age-appropriate, the non-dream stories are the strongest.
- **Recurring problems:** the nap-dream-wake structure is used 6 times in 16 parts in each series; one Otis story breaks continuity; one Dex story has a timeline math slip.
