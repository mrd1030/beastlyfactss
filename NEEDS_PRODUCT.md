# Needs Product

The live queue for gear the site names but the /gear catalog
(`src/lib/data/affiliateProducts.js`) cannot link yet, because no catalog
product plausibly is that item. Open items only. **Finished work does not stay
here**: once a product lands in the catalog and gets wired in, the line moves to
`archive/docs-completed/NEEDS_PRODUCT_COMPLETED_<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`, newest batch
first, and is deleted from this file, which is the only place that history
lives.

How to read a line:

- **NEED** means a hub buy list (`src/lib/data/guides/*.js`) or a cost guide
  `<ComparisonTable>` row already names the item, or a fix agent tried to link
  it and found nothing to link to.
- **COULD** means only tank-setup prose recommends it. Lower priority.
- "Hub" is the species hub buy list, "cost" is the cost guide table, "setup
  prose" is the tank-setup guide body. One line per distinct product, merged
  across species; sizes or strengths that differ materially get their own line.
- Dog and cat pages and legal guides are out of scope. Links are supplied by
  the owner, so no line carries one.

catalog already covers but a page simply left unlinked are not listed here: that
