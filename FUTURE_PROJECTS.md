## Print care books, "2026 Edition" (noted 2026-10-04)

Turn the care packages into real printed books (KDP, like the Chronicles of Dex picture book), starting with one pilot.

**Pilot: Bearded Dragon, 2026 Edition.** The flagship package, and the one with the owner's own story and photos (Dex and Cera). If it sells, the other packages follow on the same template; one book per animal rather than one big compendium.

**What carries over:** the package text, already sourced, Fable-reviewed and consistent with the site. The HTML-to-PDF render pipeline can produce a 6x9 print layout (trim size, margins, bleed) with a new template.

**What changes in print:**
- A book is frozen; the PDF is not. Leave out what goes stale within a year: exact prices and the state-by-state law tables. Use rough cost ranges and a short "check your local law" note, with a QR code to the live cost and legal pages. The year in the title makes yearly editions the natural plan.
- Length: a 6x9 page holds roughly half a letter page, so the 42 to 54 page PDFs become about 90 to 120 pages. Wide tables, two-column layouts and the budget pages need redesigning for the narrow page.
- Images: many more than the PDFs. From the owner's own photos, free-licensed sources (owner approves each one; see the image rule in CLAUDE.md), licensed stock or illustrations, and the existing diagrams. No image repeated anywhere.
- Color: health signs (mouth rot, stuck shed, metabolic bone disease) need color photos, but a color interior costs several times a black and white one, pushing the price toward $25 to $35. Decide: color throughout, or black and white with a color cover.
- Positioning: the PDF stays the living, updated edition; the print book is the keepsake. Consider including a code for the PDF with the book.
- Credibility: an ISBN, the author bio, and ideally a vet review line (see the credentials idea below).

**First step when it starts:** lay out two or three bearded dragon chapters at 6x9 as a sample, to see how tables and photos read at that size and how much image work a full book needs.
