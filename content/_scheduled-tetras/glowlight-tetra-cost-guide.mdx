# How Much Does Glowlight Tetra Ownership Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

The glowlight tetra (Hemigrammus erythrozonus) is a small fish from the Essequibo River in Guyana, about 1.6 inches (4 cm) long, with a cherry-red stripe down each side. It is sold by the school, and the school is the cheap part of the bill.

## The Fish: %%animal:glowlight-tetra%% for a School of 10

Stores and online sellers list glowlight tetras at about $3 to $5 a fish. The group to buy is 10, with 6 as the lowest workable number, and 10 fish come to %%animal:glowlight-tetra%% before shipping, which online orders add on top.

At that price the fish are a small line next to the tank. The same is true of a school of [neon tetras](/blog/neon-tetra-cost-guide/), and in both cases most of the budget is the glass and the equipment.

<FunFact>
The glowlight tetra reached aquarists from Guyana in the 1930s, and its name has been revised so many times since that one early guess placed it in an entirely different tetra genus. The species name itself is plain description: erythrozonus means red-banded.
</FunFact>

## Upfront Setup: %%setup:glowlight-tetra%%

<CostTable guide="glowlight-tetra" section="necessities" />

The tank is the biggest line, and it is sized by floor space: a base of at least 24 x 15 inches (60 x 38 cm), which comes to roughly 18 to 20 gallons, for a school of 10. A 10 gallon tank is sometimes sold as enough for these fish, and the [glowlight tetra tank setup guide](/blog/glowlight-tetra-tank-setup-guide/) explains why the larger base is the recommendation.

The other lines follow from the fish's range. Glowlights are tropical and kept at 75 to 82°F (24 to 28°C), so the heater is not optional, and a sponge filter covers the filtration on a tank this size. Live plants of varying heights, with some free swimming room left open in the middle, suit them best.

The liquid test kit earns its place twice. It confirms the tank has finished cycling before the fish go in, which is a reading and not a count of days, as the [aquarium cycling guide](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/) explains. It also reads pH, and glowlights are kept at a pH of 5.5 to 7.5, with the acidic end bringing out the best color. Tap water outside that range is a GH and KH question, and [what moves them and what does not](/blog/freshwater-ph-gh-kh-guide/) decides whether a tap needs any work at all.

## The Extras: A Spare Tank for Quarantine

<CostTable guide="glowlight-tetra" section="extras" />

New fish spend 30 days in a separate tank before joining the display, and the [fish quarantine guide](/blog/fish-quarantine-and-treatment-guide/) covers how to run the bare tank priced above. The same tank is where an ich outbreak is treated. Heat only goes up to the top of the range at 82°F to speed the parasite's cycle, so a bottle of salt or medication is the likely expense, as the [glowlight tetra health guide](/blog/glowlight-tetra-health-issues-guide/) lays out.

## Ongoing Costs: Low

Glowlights eat almost anything offered, and the staple is a micro pellet or small flake. The first supply in the table runs %%price:fish-food-micro-pellets%%. Small live and frozen foods such as bloodworm, daphnia and brine shrimp add a modest recurring cost on top.

Water conditioner at %%price:water-conditioner%% a bottle, a little electricity for the heater and filter, and test kit refills make up the rest. Conditioner goes into every change, so the [water change schedule](/blog/aquarium-water-changes-guide/) a tank runs on sets how fast a bottle empties.

## Vet Costs

Rarely a line item. A school of glowlights is kept healthy through water quality and, when needed, over-the-counter treatment. When fish start dying, the [signs that separate a water problem from a disease](/blog/spotting-a-sick-fish-guide/) decide whether the next spend is a water change, a medication, or a visit to a vet who treats fish.

## Lifespan

Glowlight tetras live about 2 to 4 years. That is short next to the equipment: the heater, filter, test kit and 20 gallon tank all outlast the first school and can house the next one.
