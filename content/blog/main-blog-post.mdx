# Welcome to BeastlyFacts!

Hello and welcome.

My name is Michael Ryan. I go by Mike. If you are here, there is a good chance you have a bearded dragon, a gecko, a rabbit, or some other animal you are trying to do right by.

I started this because I got tired of the same bad advice. "Just feed them crickets and lettuce." "They only need a 20 gallon tank." A lot of that is outdated or wrong, and it ends up hurting animals.

I am not a veterinarian. I am a keeper who researches against papers, agencies, and good exotic vets, then checks it against the animals in front of me. I live with two rescued bearded dragons, Dex and Cera. I also watch my sister's rabbit, Otis, for weeks at a time. They have taught me a lot, sometimes the hard way.

The longer version of how this started is on the [About page](/about/).

## What started as a few guides got a little out of hand

When I first wrote this post, the site was a handful of care guides and some fun facts. It has grown, so here is the current tour:

- [Care guides](/guides/) for over 100 species, split into the parts people actually search for: cost, handling, health, tank setup, and feeding
- The [Critter Digest](/blog/), with deep dives and comparisons, plus [is it legal?](/exotic-pet-laws/) pages for the exotic species people ask about most
- Checked [animal facts](/facts/), each with a photo in the [gallery](/gallery/)
- An [encyclopedia](/encyclopedia/) of pet species and a [Beastlypedia](/beastlypedia/) for the wild ones
- [Quizzes](/quiz/) built from real pages on this site
- The [Chronicles of Dex](/chronicles/dex/), and the [Chronicles of Otis](/chronicles/otis/), because one lizard and one visiting rabbit both demanded a series

Everything still gets researched the same way it did on day one. Sources on the page. Corrections when I learn more.

I am not here to sell you a pile of gear you do not need. I am here because I want people to find the answers in one place instead of ten tabs that disagree with each other.

If there is a topic you want covered, email me. I read everything.

Thanks for stopping by. I am glad you are here.

Mike
