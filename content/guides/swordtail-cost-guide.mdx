# How Much Does Swordtail Fish Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Swordtails cost a few dollars each, but a group needs a tank about 29 gallons and 4 feet long, which is where the real cost sits.

## The Fish: %%animal:swordtail%%, More for Lyretail and Specialty Patterns

Standard assorted color swordtails run about $3 to $6 each. Red velvet and pineapple swordtails sell for about $4 at large pet chains, and single tank-bred fish from specialty sellers run closer to $6 to $7. Hi-fin and lyretail varieties cost more, and rarer patterns, koi and neon swordtails among them, cost more again from specialty sellers.

<FunFact>
The male's namesake sword is not just decoration. It isn't a swimming aid, and in a related swordtail species a longer sword raised the energy cost of swimming. Females show a measurable preference for males with longer swords, and a 1990 study on female mate choice in this exact species found that the strength of the preference increases as the gap in sword length grows. It's a textbook example of sexual selection through female choice.
</FunFact>

## Upfront Setup: %%setup:swordtail%%, More If You Size Up

<CostTable guide="swordtail" section="necessities" />

A tank 48 inches long and 12 inches front to back, about 29 gallons, is the smallest to consider for a small trio, one male and two or three females. Swordtails are active, open-water swimmers, so the realistic starting point costs somewhat more than the table above, since [a 29 or 30-gallon tank](/blog/swordtail-tank-setup-guide/) itself runs higher than the 20-gallon product linked here.

Live plants aren't just decor here either. A male swordtail can be a persistent, near-constant presence around a female he's courting, and dense planting gives her somewhere to break line of sight, a genuine practical reason to budget for real cover from the start rather than treat it as optional.

## Vet Costs

As with most aquarium fish, swordtails seldom see a vet in any formal sense. Most swordtail health issues, ich, fin rot, and dropsy among them, get managed at home with water quality correction and over-the-counter medication rather than a clinic visit. Dedicated aquatic vets exist but are uncommon.

## Lifespan

Swordtails kept in stable water commonly live a few years, and no firm lifespan figure has been published for the species. A [molly](/blog/molly-cost-guide/) is the closest comparison, a similarly priced fish that gets by on a smaller tank.

## The Cost to Plan For: More Fish, and Possibly the Wrong Fish

Swordtails are livebearers, and a mixed-sex tank produces fry on a monthly rhythm. The cost is not the fish: it is the second tank, the extra food and the time spent finding homes for juveniles, or the decision to keep a single-sex group from the start.

The more species-specific risk is hybridization. Swordtails and platies both belong to the genus *Xiphophorus* and can hybridize, deliberate crossbreeding between the two has gone on for close to a century, and many swordtails sold in the hobby carry platy ancestry from that long history. Keep an unplanned mix of swordtails and [platies](/guides/platy/) in the same tank, and the fry you end up with may not be cleanly one species or the other, worth knowing before combining the two just because they're both easy, inexpensive community fish.
