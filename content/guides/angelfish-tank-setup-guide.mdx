# Angelfish Tank Setup: Height Matters More Than You'd Think

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Angelfish have an unusual body shape for a community fish, tall and laterally flattened, and that shape drives nearly every decision in setting up their tank correctly. This covers the setup itself, for what it all costs, see our [Angelfish cost guide](/blog/angelfish-cost-guide/), or the full [Angelfish care guide](/guides/angelfish/) for everything else.

## Tank Size

29 gallons, and specifically a tall configuration, is the practical minimum for a single adult. A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08BJB7FX4?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Tetra Glass Aquarium, 55 Gallons (NV52018)">55-gallon tank</AffiliateLink> is the better choice for a small group of four to six, and at a standard 20 inches tall, it already clears this species' height requirement without needing a specialty configuration. Here's the detail that trips up a lot of first-time buyers: a "20-gallon long" tank, despite the larger-sounding volume, is often only about 12 inches tall, genuinely too shallow for this species. Prioritize height, aim for at least 18 to 20 inches, over length when choosing a tank for angelfish.

## Temperature

A genuinely tropical species, comfortable in the 78 to 82°F range, some sources extend slightly lower to 75°F. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eQY1L5" product="Submersible aquarium heater">heater</AffiliateLink> is essential, budget roughly 3 to 5 watts per gallon, meaning a 200 to 250 watt heater for a 55-gallon tank. Keep fluctuation within about 2 degrees in either direction.

## Water Chemistry

Slightly acidic to neutral, pH 6.5 to 7.0, with soft water genuinely preferred, especially important if you're hoping for breeding pairs to form. Stable, fully cycled water with zero ammonia and zero nitrite matters more for this species than for some hardier community fish. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4p1T7yd" product="Water test kit">test kit</AffiliateLink> is the only reliable way to confirm those numbers rather than guess at them.

## Filtration

Gentle to moderate flow works best, angelfish are relatively weak swimmers and don't do well fighting strong current. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eQAk5K" product="Canister filter (Fluval 107, up to 30 gal)">canister filter</AffiliateLink> sized for the single-fish 29-gallon tall tank, or a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vNGY2v" product="Canister filter (Fluval 407, up to 100 gal)">heavier-duty canister filter</AffiliateLink> or a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000260FUW?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="AquaClear 70 Power Filter (40-70 gal)">hang-on-back unit</AffiliateLink> for the 55-gallon group setup, all work well for the main tank. If you're breeding, a sponge filter protects fry from being pulled into stronger filtration.

<FunFact>
Angelfish look built for open water with those tall, sweeping fins, but the reality is closer to the opposite. Weak swimmers relative to their size, they do best in gentle to moderate flow, and a filter rated for strong turnover in a small tank can genuinely stress this fish rather than just keep the water clean.
</FunFact>

## Substrate

Fine sand or small, smooth <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DQQZ69J6?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="CaribSea Super Naturals Aquascape Sunset Gold, 10 lb">gravel</AffiliateLink>. Avoid sharp or coarse gravel entirely, angelfish have genuinely delicate fins that tear easily against rough substrate.

## Decor and Plants

Build vertically. Tall <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eR0Z2l" product="Driftwood">driftwood</AffiliateLink> and <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4grZYPq" product="Live terrarium plants">broadleaf plants</AffiliateLink>, Amazon sword, Java fern, and anubias all work well, give this tall-bodied fish natural cover, help break sightlines to reduce territorial aggression between fish, and double as spawning surfaces if a pair bonds, the pair-bonding behavior covered in our [Angelfish handling guide](/blog/angelfish-handling-guide/).

## Lighting

A standard LED on an 8 to 10 hour daily cycle is sufficient, no special requirements beyond what supports any live plants you're keeping.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: A Secure Lid

Angelfish can jump, and a tank left uncovered, or covered with meaningful gaps, is a real risk. Combined with prioritizing height over length and stocking angelfish last in a community tank, covered in our [Angelfish handling guide](/blog/angelfish-handling-guide/), these details are what separate a setup this species genuinely thrives in from one that just barely works.

## The Bottom Line

None of this is complicated, but it does require accepting that an angelfish needs meaningfully more tank height than its calm, ornamental look suggests. Set it up right from the start and this is a genuinely long-lived fish, 8 to 12 years is typical, covered in more detail in our [Angelfish cost guide](/blog/angelfish-cost-guide/). Getting the tank right also heads off a lot of the problems in our [Angelfish health issues guide](/blog/angelfish-health-issues-guide/), water quality and stress drive nearly everything on that list.
