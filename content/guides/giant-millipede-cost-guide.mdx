# How Much Does a Giant Millipede Really Cost? (And a Supply Note Worth Knowing)

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Giant African millipedes are inexpensive to keep month to month, though the animal itself costs more than you might expect for an invertebrate, and there's a real reason behind that.

## Why the Price Runs Higher Than Other Invertebrates

The USDA maintains an import embargo on giant African millipedes, tied to concerns about a potentially damaging mite species found on imported animals, and a USDA PPQ 526 plant pest permit is required to import one or move it across state lines, with the state-by-state picture in the giant millipede legal guide. Practically, this means most giant millipedes available in the US today are captive-bred or from older stock rather than freshly imported, which keeps supply genuinely limited and prices higher than you'd expect for an animal this low-maintenance to care for. Some states also have their own additional sales restrictions or permit requirements, worth checking before you buy.

## The Millipede Itself: $75 to $150 or More

Compared to most pet invertebrates, this is a real step up in purchase price, driven entirely by the supply situation above rather than anything about the animal's care difficulty.

## Ongoing Costs: Minimal

Fresh produce and periodic substrate or leaf litter replenishment make up nearly the entire monthly cost, just a few dollars. The [deep organic substrate](/blog/giant-millipede-tank-setup-guide/) is both the biggest line here and the animal's food, which is why topping it up is not optional the way a decoration would be.

## Vet Costs

Essentially none. Invertebrate-experienced exotic vets exist but are rarely needed, most issues are managed through husbandry alone.

## Lifespan: Exceptionally Long for an Invertebrate

5 to 7 years in the wild, and up to 10 years in captivity. This is remarkable longevity for an invertebrate, considerably longer than the [praying mantis](/blog/praying-mantis-cost-guide/), [jumping spider](/blog/jumping-spider-cost-guide/), or [hissing cockroach](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-cost-guide/), and worth factoring into your decision as a real, multi-year commitment rather than a short-lived novelty pet.
