# How Much Does a Green Iguana Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Green iguanas are cheap to buy as babies, and that low starting price is exactly what leads so many owners to underestimate what they're actually taking on. For the full care picture beyond budgeting, see our complete [Green Iguana care guide](/guides/green-iguana/).

## The iguana: $20 to $1,000+

Baby green iguanas are farm-raised and inexpensive, commonly $20 to $100. Color morphs push the price up considerably, albino, hypomelanistic, axanthic, and purple translucent lines commonly run $300 to $1,000 or more. A related but genuinely different, IUCN-endangered species, the blue iguana, can run into the tens of thousands, worth noting so you don't confuse the two when shopping.

## Upfront setup: $700 to $2,500 or more

This is where the real cost of iguana ownership actually shows up. A proper adult enclosure has to be custom-built or walk-in, no commercial tank is large enough, and by the time you add a cluster of <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DJRLWX1H?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Arcadia GoldenSun Basking Halogen Lamp 100W">halogen basking bulbs</AffiliateLink>, high-output <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/43AcIvA" product='Arcadia T5 UVB Reptile Light Fixture Kit (36" / 14% UVB Dragon)'>UVB</AffiliateLink>, an <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4fibBY2" product="Automatic misting system/fogger">automatic misting system</AffiliateLink>, and a large soaking tub, total setup cost commonly runs well past $700 and often past $2,000.

## Ongoing costs: roughly $60 to $150 a month

Fresh produce, supplements, substrate, and meaningful electricity to run heating and lighting for a large enclosure add up to a genuinely higher monthly cost than most reptiles on this site, produce alone commonly runs $30 to $50 a month, with electricity adding another $20 to $50. One cost aggregator models an all-in annual ownership cost around $2,140 a year once every category is included.

## Vet costs

Reptile vets are specialized, and iguana-specific issues, metabolic bone disease treatment or egg-binding surgery among them, can run into real money. Budget for an annual exam at minimum, and know that emergency care for this species tends toward the expensive end.

## Lifespan: 15 to 25 years, if the iguana survives its first year

Here's the number that should reframe this whole cost conversation. With excellent care, green iguanas live 15 to 20 years, sometimes 25. But inadequate diet, lighting, and housing are so common in this species that Wikipedia's entry on green iguanas in captivity, citing de Vosjoli (1992), states that inadequate care causes an estimated 70% of green iguanas in captivity to die within their first year of life. The low purchase price has nothing to do with what it actually costs, in setup and in animal welfare, to keep this species alive and thriving long-term.

## The honest budgeting takeaway

A green iguana's sticker price is almost irrelevant to what responsible ownership costs. This is genuinely one of the more expensive reptiles on this site to house correctly, and the setup investment isn't optional padding, it's the difference between an iguana that reaches its 20-year potential and one that becomes part of that 70% first-year statistic.
