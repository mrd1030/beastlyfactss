# Green Anole Enclosure Setup

Green anoles need genuinely more setup investment than their small size and low price suggest, tropical humidity, real UVB, and careful heat placement all matter here. ## Enclosure Size

24 inches long by 24 inches wide by 24 inches tall as a minimum for one adult, larger is better. This species is semi-arboreal, so a tall, front-opening enclosure with good ventilation suits it well. Don't house multiple anoles together, they don't coexist peacefully.

## Temperature

Basking spot around 90F, cool side 70 to 77F, nighttime 65 to 75F. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4bqzQAS" product="Basking bulb (standard, 75W)">60-watt incandescent basking bulb</AffiliateLink> in a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44eEEFH" product="Basking dome/fixture">ceramic-socket dome</AffiliateLink> works well. A genuinely important safety detail, mount the heat lamp above or outside the enclosure's mesh top, not resting directly on it. Green anoles climb and rest inverted on mesh, and a bulb positioned where they can reach it risks real burns.

## Humidity

60 to 70% during the day, higher overnight. Achieve this through morning and evening misting with a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vPKMjX" product="Fine mist spray bottle">pump sprayer</AffiliateLink>, and a cool-mist humidifier or fogger overnight in genuinely dry climates, using distilled water. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3QHrM81" product="Digital thermometer/hygrometer combo">thermometer/hygrometer combo</AffiliateLink> is how you'll actually confirm you're hitting that range rather than guessing.

## Substrate

Coconut fiber or a tropical soil blend, roughly 60% organic topsoil to 40% coconut fiber, works well, layered about 2 inches deep with leaf litter on top. Avoid gravel, sand, and anything containing vermiculite or pesticide residue. A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CY23K32S?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="VCEPJH Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate, 2 Pack">coconut fiber substrate</AffiliateLink> covers the base of that mix.

## Lighting

UVB is genuinely necessary for this diurnal species. Target a UV index of 3.0 to 4.0 at the basking branch, using an Arcadia ShadeDweller kit or a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3TiqeC4" product="UVB - Arcadia Forest 6% (ProT5 kit, 36&quot;/39W)">T5 HO bulb in the 5 to 6% range</AffiliateLink>, paired with a separate 6500K LED for general brightness and to support live plants. Run a 10 to 14 hour photoperiod.

<FunFact>
Since the warmest spot in a green anole's enclosure sits up near the basking lamp rather than down on the floor, this species spends much of its day moving vertically just to regulate its temperature, which is exactly why climbing structure has to run the full height of the enclosure, not just the bottom.
</FunFact>

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Climbing Material at Every Level

Provide branches and foliage spanning the enclosure's full height, not concentrated near the bottom. Since the warmest area sits at the top near the basking lamp, your anole needs a genuine path to thermoregulate by moving vertically, exactly why the mesh-burn risk mentioned above matters so much, and why climbing structure throughout the enclosure isn't just decorative, it's functional.
