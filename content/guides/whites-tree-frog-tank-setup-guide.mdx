# White's Tree Frog Tank Setup: What Your Frog Actually Needs

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Humidity that never drops causes more problems than humidity that dips a bit low between mistings.

## Enclosure size

18x18x24 inches is the standard minimum for one adult. Height is the dimension that matters. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vlb6Bp" product="18x18x24 in glass terrarium (arboreal)">20-gallon-equivalent enclosure</AffiliateLink> is comfortable for a single frog. 24x18x24 inches works for a small group of two to four.

## Temperature

Cool side 70 to 75F. Basking 80 to 85F. Mid-70s at night. A low-wattage bulb or a side-mounted heat mat holds that. This species does not need intense heat.

## Humidity: the detail worth getting right

Hold 50 to 60% between mistings. Mist once or twice a day so humidity spikes to 70 to 80%, then let it fall. Ranges of 70 to 80% around the clock circulate widely. Constant saturation is what drives red-leg and skin infection.

Use only <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3Rg5gTT" product="Water conditioner">dechlorinated tap water or spring water</AffiliateLink> for misting and drinking. Never distilled or reverse-osmosis water. The [amphibian quarantine and water guide](/blog/amphibian-quarantine-and-water-guide/) has the reason: distilled and RO water are usually not electrolyte-balanced, and can be fatal without rebalancing.

## Substrate

Coarse orchid bark, coco fiber or coco husk, or a bioactive soil mix, kept lightly moist rather than soggy. Avoid fine, loose substrate that is easy to swallow during feeding.

## Lighting

A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00M9ONY1W?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med 5.0 T5-HO 14" Terrarium Hood with ReptiSun T5 HO 5.0 UVB Lamp">low-output T5 in the 5 to 7% range</AffiliateLink>, basking branch at least 6 inches below the fixture. Replace every 9 to 12 months even while the bulb still glows.

<FunFact>
A T5 UVB bulb can keep glowing long after its UVB output has crashed. That is why the bulb gets replaced on a schedule, not when it looks dead.
</FunFact>

## Furnishings

Sturdy <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3SKmfOx" product="Zoo Med Natural Cork Rounds Medium (7"-10" Long) (3)">branches for climbing</AffiliateLink>, <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4gmXC4j" product="Artificial/silk terrarium plants">broad leaves or platforms</AffiliateLink> for resting, and a shallow water dish that is easy to keep clean.

If the frog is always immediately visible, there is not enough in there.

Once this setup is dialed in, this is a species that, unlike the [Pacman frog](/blog/pacman-frog-tank-setup-guide/), tolerates being picked up.

For how UVB works and why distance matters as much as the bulb, see the [complete guide to UVB lighting](/blog/uvb-lighting-complete-guide/).
