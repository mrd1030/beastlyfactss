# Common Uromastyx Health Issues and What Causes Them

<VetDisclaimer />
<AffiliateDisclosure />

Uromastyx are hardy desert specialists, and most health problems trace directly back to getting this species' unusually specific heat, humidity, or dietary needs wrong. See our [tank setup guide](/blog/uromastyx-tank-setup-guide/) for the husbandry side of prevention.

## Metabolic Bone Disease (MBD)

The most common issue in captivity. Watch for a soft or hanging jaw, swollen limbs, tremors, deformed bones, and lethargy. Caused by inadequate calcium or vitamin D3, insufficient UVB, or a diet too high in phosphorus or oxalates. Always see a vet, severe cases can be fatal. ## Impaction

Bloating, no bowel movements, appetite loss, and lethargy point here. Caused by ingesting loose substrate, particularly calcium sand or walnut shell products, or eating food that's too large. See a vet if it doesn't resolve, this can become fatal. Choosing an appropriate <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0002DIZKC?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med ReptiSand, 10 Pounds, Desert White">sand substrate</AffiliateLink> and avoiding calcium sand or walnut shell products entirely is part of avoiding this risk.

## Respiratory Infection

Wheezing, open-mouth breathing, and mucus at the nostrils. Caused by humidity running too high or temperatures too low. Always see a vet.

## Parasites

Diarrhea and weight loss, more common in wild-caught or stressed animals. See a vet for a fecal exam and treatment.

<FunFact>
A white, crusty buildup around a uromastyx's nostrils can look like a sign of illness, but it's actually normal: specialized glands let this species excrete excess salt through the nose, and wiping it away is all the maintenance it needs.
</FunFact>

## Salt Crusting: Not Actually a Health Problem

A white, crusty buildup around the nostrils is completely normal, it's how uromastyx excrete excess salt through specialized glands. Manageable simply by wiping it away.

## The Husbandry Pattern Worth Remembering

Inadequate UVB and calcium cause MBD, the most serious and most preventable issue on this list. Loose or inappropriate substrate causes impaction. And humidity or temperature running outside this species' genuinely narrow comfort zone causes respiratory infection.
