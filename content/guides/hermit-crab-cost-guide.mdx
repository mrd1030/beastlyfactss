# How Much Does a Hermit Crab Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Hermit crabs are often sold as an impulse buy at beach shops and boardwalk stands. That is the wrong way to think about them. They need a real setup, and they are social animals that should not be kept alone.

## The crabs: $3 to $40 each

Usually the smallest line in the budget. Keep at least two. A single crab is not a proper start.

Buy from a keeper or a shop that already houses crabs on deep substrate with both water dishes, not from a painted-shell souvenir stand. The animal is the same species either way. The starting condition is not.

<FunFact>
In the wild, hermit crabs looking to trade up will queue by size next to a vacant shell in what researchers call a vacancy chain, waiting their turn to try it on before the next crab in line gets a shot. That is why several spare shells, even in captivity, keep a crab from settling for one that fits poorly.
</FunFact>

## Ongoing costs: $10 to $30 a month

Food, calcium, and salt mix make up most of the monthly spend. This is a low-cost pet to keep once the tank is built. Most of the expense is front-loaded.

## Why a cheap boardwalk crab is the wrong budget

The few-dollar crab creates the impression that this is a low-investment pet. The setup is what determines whether it thrives: tank, substrate depth, humidity, spare shells. None of that is optional. Skipping it is a common reason boardwalk-shop crabs do not last, not because the species is fragile, but because the cheap version of ownership is not adequate care. Most hermit crab health problems trace to that gap. The [hermit crab health issues guide](/blog/hermit-crab-health-issues-guide/) works through the ones that gap produces.

Hermit crabs can live 10 to 20 years or more when kept well. The upfront tank is reasonable against that span. The hermit crab also shows up alongside the axolotl, goldfish, and chinchilla in [Four Pets That Break the Usual Mold](/blog/four-unusual-pets-overview/).
