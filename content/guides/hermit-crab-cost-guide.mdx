# How Much Does a Hermit Crab Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Hermit crabs are often sold as an impulse buy at beach shops and boardwalk stands, which is exactly the wrong way to think about them. Done properly, they need a real setup, and they're social animals that shouldn't be kept alone.

## The Crabs Themselves: $5 to $40 Each

Usually the smallest line item in the whole budget. Keep at least two, hermit crabs are social and don't do well in isolation, so budgeting for a single crab isn't really an option if you want to keep them properly.

## Pricing a Tank for a Group

A 10-gallon tank holds up to two adult crabs. As a group grows, plan on about 5 more gallons for each crab past the first two, so four crabs call for 20 gallons, six for 30, and eight for 40. A bare 20-gallon long runs about $60 and a 40-gallon breeder about $150 at full price, before a lid. The rest of the list scales with the floor: a larger tank takes proportionally more sand and coconut fiber to reach the same 6-inch depth, a longer heat mat, and more spare shells, since every crab needs several to choose from. Planning for the final group size at the start is cheaper than buying a 10-gallon now and replacing it when the colony grows. That last line covers the extras that are easy to underestimate, including a pair of <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eKyN0W" product="Large soak-able water dish">soakable water dishes</AffiliateLink>, since hermit crabs need both a fresh water dish and a separate marine saltwater dish, not just one.

<FunFact>
In the wild, hermit crabs looking to trade up will actually queue by size next to a vacant shell in what researchers call a vacancy chain, waiting their turn to try it on before the next crab in line gets a shot. It's part of why keeping several spare shells on hand, even in captivity, keeps a crab from settling for one that fits poorly.
</FunFact>

## Ongoing Costs: Roughly $10 to $30 a Month

Food and general supplies make up most of the monthly spend for hermit crabs. This is a genuinely low-cost pet to maintain once the initial setup is built, most of the expense is front-loaded into getting the tank right the first time.

## Why "Cheap Boardwalk Crab" Is a Misleading Way to Think About Cost

The low individual price of a hermit crab, often just a few dollars, creates the impression that this is a low-investment pet overall. In reality, the setup, tank, substrate depth, humidity control, and multiple spare shells, is what determines whether a hermit crab thrives, and none of that is optional. Skipping that setup to save money is a common reason hermit crabs from boardwalk shops don't survive long, not because the species is inherently fragile, but because the cheap version of ownership isn't adequate care. Most hermit crab health problems trace straight back to that gap.

Hermit crabs can live 10 years or more when kept well, and some reach 30, which makes the upfront investment in a proper setup a reasonable one relative to how long you'll actually have the animal. That lifespan is one reason it sits beside the axolotl and the goldfish among [pets that break the usual mold](/blog/four-unusual-pets-overview/).
