# Flying Squirrel Enrichment: A Rodent That Solved the Same Problem

<AffiliateDisclosure />

The nearest comparison for this animal is a marsupial from the other side of the world. That is stranger than it sounds and it works.

<KeyTakeaway>
A flying squirrel and a sugar glider are not related. One is a rodent, the other a marsupial, and gliding evolved separately in each lineage. They converged on the same body plan, the same nocturnal arboreal life and, for a keeper, very nearly the same enclosure.
</KeyTakeaway>

For everything else about them, see the [Flying Squirrel care guide](/guides/flying-squirrel/).

## What the Research Says

Nothing on pet flying squirrel enrichment specifically. The borrow is from the sugar glider, covered in full in our [Sugar Glider enrichment guide](/blog/sugar-glider-enrichment-guide/).

That guide's picture is: colonial animals where solitary housing is treated as a major welfare risk, height and glide routes over floor space, a pouch or cavity to sleep in through the day, foraging presented at height, and a solid-track wheel.

The usual warning about borrowing across a taxonomic gap does not really apply here, because the gap is the interesting part. **Convergent evolution** means the two animals face the same problems in captivity even though they are separated by roughly the whole mammal family tree. What transfers is the ecology, not the physiology, and ecology is what an enclosure has to answer.

Where it does break down: flying squirrels are rodents with **continuously growing incisors**. Gnawing is a requirement here in a way it is not for a glider.

<FunFact>
Gliding has evolved independently at least half a dozen times in mammals, in rodents, marsupials and colugos among others. It is one of the clearest cases in biology of the same solution being found repeatedly, which is why a sugar glider care sheet transfers so well to an animal that is not remotely a sugar glider.
</FunFact>

Free-roam time in a squirrel-proofed room is genuinely valuable here, supervised, because no cage sold provides real gliding distance.

## A Nest Cavity

Cavity nesters that sleep the day enclosed and in contact with each other. A nest box mounted high, plus a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07N658NL6?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Niteangel Hanging Hammock Nap Sack Swing Bag Pet Sleeper for Ferret Rat Sugar Glider">hanging fleece sleeper</AffiliateLink> or two, gives them the dark enclosed space the day requires.

Provide more than one and site them high. Check fabric for loose threads, since toes and gliding membranes catch easily.

## Company

Social animals that nest communally, sometimes in substantial huddles when it is cold. Same-sex pairs or small groups, and a single flying squirrel is a compromise.

Introductions take time. Bonding with a fast nocturnal animal takes more patience than most people expect.

## Gnawing, Which the Borrow Does Not Cover

The rodent difference. Continuously growing incisors that need constant work: untreated branches, nuts in shell, hard chews and cuttlebone.

Nuts in shell do double duty, since getting into one is both a gnawing task and a foraging problem.

## Foraging at Height

Wild flying squirrels forage across a home range for nuts, seeds, fungi and insects. Put food high so getting to it involves climbing, hide portions in paper parcels and foraging containers, scatter the rest, and vary where it appears.

Live insects are worth including. The calcium balance is the part most improvised diets get wrong.

## Priority Order

1. Height, filled with branches and separated glide points
2. A pair or small group
3. Nest boxes and hanging sleepers mounted high, more than one
4. Constant gnawing material
5. Foraging presented at height and hidden
6. Supervised free-roam in a proofed room
7. Novelty and rearrangement, last

## What Not to Do

Do not keep one alone. Do not buy a tall cage and leave the height empty. Do not use fabric with loose threads. Do not skip gnawing material, which is the one thing the sugar glider comparison will not warn you about. Do not improvise the diet without getting the calcium side right. Do not handle during the day as a routine. And check our [legal guide](/blog/flying-squirrel-legal-guide/) first, since they are regulated or prohibited in many places and are native wildlife in others.
