# Ackie Monitor Enrichment: Eight Out of Eight, First Try, Ten Minutes

Some enrichment results need explaining. This one does the persuading on its own. For everything else about the species, see our complete [Ackie Monitor care guide](/guides/ackie-monitor/).

<KeyTakeaway>
Eight juvenile black-throated monitors were presented with a transparent tube containing food. All eight worked out how to open it within ten minutes, on the first trial. For an animal like this, a bare enclosure is not a neutral environment.
</KeyTakeaway>

## What the Research Actually Says

Manrod, Hartdegen and Burghardt tested juvenile black-throated monitors (*Varanus albigularis*) at Knoxville Zoological Gardens with a transparent food tube apparatus. **All eight opened the tube within ten minutes in the first trial.**

Later work looked at latency in problem solving as evidence of learning in varanid and helodermatid lizards: across longitudinal testing with puzzle-feeder devices, all tested lizards showed a pattern of **decreasing latencies over time**, interpreted as learning rather than trial-and-error repetition. A zoo study in Thailand separately examined enrichment effects on the activity and spatial distribution of captive monitor lizards.

<FunFact>
The literature notes that while providing stimulus enrichment is accepted practice for mammals, extending it to non-avian reptiles has barely been explored as an aid to cognitive development, despite monitor lizards having a long-standing reputation for being highly curious and intelligent.
</FunFact>

The honest scope note: these are *Varanus albigularis* results and the ackie is *Varanus acanthurus*. This is genus-level evidence. What transfers is that varanids solve novel food problems fast, which is a property of the group.

## Substrate Depth, Which Is the Whole Enclosure Decision

Ackies are obligate burrowers that construct extensive tunnel systems, and depth is the single largest housing choice you make for them. A shallow layer is bedding, not a burrow.

Give them enough <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07Z5CJ573?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Bio Dude Terra Sahara substrate, 36 qt">burrowing substrate</AffiliateLink> to build in, mixed so it holds a tunnel rather than collapsing. Other quantities are available and a proper depth needs several. Our [tank setup guide](/blog/ackie-monitor-tank-setup-guide/) covers the depth and gradient specifics.

A separate damper dig zone lets them excavate humid tunnels without you keeping the entire arid-species enclosure wet.

## Puzzle Feeding, the Item the Research Points At

A transparent container with food visible inside is essentially the study apparatus, and it is trivially easy to improvise. Vary it: food under a moveable object, inside a tube, buried at a new spot, in a container that has to be tipped.

## Space, Which Is Not Optional Here

Ackies are active foragers that cover ground. A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DC2292M8?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="48x24x48 PVC reptile enclosure">large PVC enclosure</AffiliateLink> gives them both the footprint to range and the height for the climbing they also do. Rock stacks, cork and branches turn that volume into terrain rather than empty air.

## Target Training

Monitors are routinely target-trained in zoo collections, and it works as cognitive enrichment and cooperative-care husbandry simultaneously. A target stick and a few minutes daily makes weighing, health checks and shifting the animal much less stressful for both of you. There is no target stick in our gear list yet either.

## Priority Order

1. Substrate deep enough for a real burrow system
2. Enclosure footprint and height, since this is an active forager
3. Puzzle and extraction feeding, the part the research points at
4. A separate humid dig zone
5. Climbing structure and rock stacks
6. Target and station training
7. Scent novelty and rearrangement

## What Not to Do

Do not keep a burrowing monitor on a shallow substrate layer, since burrowing is the behavior the enclosure exists to allow. Do not bowl-feed an animal that solves puzzle tubes in ten minutes. Do not skip the basking surface temperatures this species needs while chasing enrichment, because thermal husbandry outranks everything here and our [health issues guide](/blog/ackie-monitor-health-issues-guide/) covers what happens when it slips. Do not overfeed while making food more interesting, since ackies gain weight readily.
