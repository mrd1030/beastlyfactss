# Tokay Gecko Enclosure Setup: Warm, Vertical, and Genuinely Humid

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Tokay gecko care runs warm and genuinely humid, closer to a rainforest setup than the arid conditions many other geckos on this site prefer. For what all of it costs, see our [cost guide](/blog/tokay-gecko-cost-guide/).

## Enclosure Size

18 inches long by 18 inches wide by 36 inches tall as a minimum, larger is better. This is an arboreal species, so vertical space genuinely matters more than floor space. A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08YY64J5X?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="WACOOL 18x18x36in Front-Opening Glass Terrarium (50 Gallon)">front-opening enclosure</AffiliateLink> makes maintenance easier given how defensive this species can be, something our [handling guide](/blog/tokay-gecko-handling-guide/) covers in more detail. House one gecko per enclosure, tokays are highly territorial.

## Temperature

Basking area 90 to 105F, cooler zone 80 to 85F, and nighttime should stay warmer than many homes naturally run, 75 to 80F, not dropping below about 70F. Use a halogen basking bulb during the day, and pair it with a lightless <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0002AQCQO?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med Repticare Infrared Ceramic Heat Emitter (150 watt)">ceramic heat emitter</AffiliateLink> or deep heat projector on a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08R29S2WB?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Exo Terra Dimming and Pulse Proportional Thermostat for Reptile Terrariums">thermostat</AffiliateLink> for overnight warmth, this species genuinely needs supplemental night heat more than most.

## Humidity: High, and Genuinely Important

60 to 80% is the target range, considerably more humid than what suits a desert species. Achieve this with daily or twice-daily misting using a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vPKMjX" product="Fine mist spray bottle">fine mist spray bottle</AffiliateLink>, moisture-retentive substrate, <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4bpnvNm" product="Sphagnum moss (for moist hide)">sphagnum moss</AffiliateLink>, and leaf litter. Good ventilation matters here too, high humidity without airflow invites mold.

<FunFact>
A tokay gecko's basking area can run as hot as 105F, while its nighttime enclosure still needs to stay warmer than most homes naturally run, 75 to 80F, a combination that makes this one of the more demanding heating setups among commonly kept geckos.
</FunFact>

## Substrate

<AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CN8ZRYA?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med Eco Earth Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate (8 qt, 2-pack)">Coconut fiber</AffiliateLink> or a bioactive-style mix, at least 2 to 3 inches deep, with moss and leaf litter layered on top. Avoid pine and cedar entirely, both release fumes that are toxic to reptiles.

## Lighting

As a nocturnal species, tokays can technically survive without UVB if their diet supplies enough vitamin D3, but low-level UVB, a 5% or forest-strength <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00M9ONY1W?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product='Zoo Med 5.0 T5-HO 14" Terrarium Hood with ReptiSun T5 HO 5.0 UVB Lamp'>T5 bulb</AffiliateLink>, offers real benefit. Replace the bulb every 6 to 12 months. Keep nights genuinely dark.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Climbing Structures at Every Level

Provide branches, cork bark, and foliage spanning the full height of the enclosure, not just near the top or bottom. This lets your tokay move along the vertical temperature gradient to thermoregulate properly.
