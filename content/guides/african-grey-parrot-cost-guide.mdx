# How Much Does an African Grey Parrot Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

African grey parrots are genuinely one of the most expensive pets covered on this site, and the purchase price is really just the entry fee for a decades-long commitment.

## The Bird: $1,000 to $4,000

Price varies by subspecies, the larger Congo grey versus the smaller Timneh, along with age, whether the bird is hand-raised, and the individual breeder. Most African greys fall in the $1,500 to $3,500 range. If you're importing a bird, permit fees add another $111 to $565 on top of the purchase price.

<FunFact>
Research tracking captive lifespans puts the mean African grey lifespan at 45 years, with some individuals reaching 60, compared to an average of about 23 years in the wild. Some exceptional individuals have lived 70 to 80 years, meaning a grey acquired as a young adult can genuinely outlive its owner.
</FunFact>

## Vet Costs

An annual avian wellness exam runs $150 to $300, and baseline bloodwork plus testing for conditions like PBFD is genuinely worth budgeting for on top of that. Emergencies, hypocalcemic seizures or aspergillosis among them (both covered in our [health issues guide](/blog/african-grey-parrot-health-issues-guide/)), can run several hundred to over a thousand dollars.

## Lifespan: The Number That Changes Everything

Research tracking captive lifespans puts the mean at 45 years, with some individuals reaching 60, compared to an average of about 23 years in the wild. Some exceptional individuals live 70 to 80 years. A grey acquired as a young adult can genuinely outlive its owner, which is worth sitting with before committing to this species.
