# How Much Does a Western Hognose Snake Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Western hognose snakes stay small and cheap to run once the tank is built.

## The snake: $50 to $1,000+, depending on morph

<ComparisonTable
  headers={["Morph", "Price Range"]}
  rows={[
    ["Normal, wild-type", "$50 - $100"],

    ["Rare combinations (coral, snow, and similar)", "$1,000+"],
  ]}
/>

Females typically cost $50 to $200 more than males across every tier. They grow larger and are valued for breeding.

Those rows add to $180 to $330 a year, about $15 to $28 a month. Food and substrate are about half. Electricity and one wellness exam are the rest. The old "$10 to $25 a month" line did not match this table.

## Vet costs

A routine exam runs roughly $50 to $100, fecal test $25 to $50. Radiographs $100 to $250 per view. Treating a respiratory infection or an impaction can run into the hundreds. The [hognose health issues guide](/blog/hognose-snake-health-issues-guide/) is the page behind those visits.

<FunFact>
A snow or coral morph can cost more than ten times a normal wild-type. Genetically and behaviorally it is the same animal. Same enclosure, same heat, same diet. The gap is pattern rarity, not care difficulty.
</FunFact>

## Lifespan and the long game

10 to 15 years is typical. 15 to 20 is achievable under good care. That is a long commitment for a snake that stays small.

Setup and running cost sit near a [corn snake](/blog/corn-snake-cost-guide/) and under a [ball python](/blog/ball-python-cost-guide/) at the higher size tiers. The fuller side by side is [Corn Snake vs. Hognose Snake](/blog/corn-snake-vs-hognose-snake-guide/).
