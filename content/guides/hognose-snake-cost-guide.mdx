# How Much Does a Western Hognose Snake Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Western hognose snakes are one of the more affordable pet snakes out there, with a low ongoing cost that makes them one of the cheaper long-term reptiles to keep once the initial setup is built.

## The Snake Itself: $50 to $1,000+, Depending on Morph

<ComparisonTable
  headers={["Morph", "Price Range"]}
  rows={[
    ["Normal, wild-type", "$50 - $100"],
    ["Albino", "$150 - $200"],
    ["Anaconda", "$175 - $500"],
    ["Rare combinations (coral, snow, and similar)", "$1,000+"],
  ]}
/>

Females typically cost $50 to $200 more than males across every morph tier, since they grow larger and are more sought after for breeding.

## Ongoing Costs: Roughly $10 to $25 a Month

Frozen and thawed rodents, fed infrequently since hognoses don't eat as often as some other snakes, plus occasional substrate replacement, make up nearly the entire monthly cost.

## Vet Costs

A routine reptile wellness exam runs roughly $50 to $100, with a fecal test around $25 to $50. Radiographs, if needed, run $100 to $250 per view. Treating a respiratory infection or, less commonly, an impaction can run into the hundreds.

<FunFact>
A snow or coral morph hognose can cost more than ten times what a normal, wild-type hognose costs. Genetically and behaviorally, though, it's the identical animal underneath, needing the exact same enclosure, heat, and diet. The price gap is entirely about pattern and color rarity, not care difficulty.
</FunFact>

## Lifespan and the Long Game

10 to 15 years is the commonly cited lifespan range for a Western hognose, with 15 to 20 years genuinely achievable under excellent husbandry. That's a long commitment for a snake that stays small and costs little to maintain day to day, worth keeping in mind even though the monthly numbers look modest.

## The Value Proposition

Among the beginner snakes, hognoses land on the affordable end for both setup and ongoing cost, broadly comparable to a [corn snake](/blog/corn-snake-cost-guide/) and cheaper to run than a [ball python](/blog/ball-python-cost-guide/) at the higher size tiers.

The fuller side by side, beyond just the price tags, lives in our [Corn Snake vs. Hognose Snake guide](/blog/corn-snake-vs-hognose-snake-guide/), which covers temperament and handling differences too. The morph market adds real price variation if you want a specific look, but a normal, wild-type hognose is one of the most budget-friendly reptiles you can responsibly keep.
