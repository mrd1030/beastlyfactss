# Red-Eared Slider Tank Setup: Plan for the Adult, Not the Hatchling

<AffiliateDisclosure />

The hatchling box is not the adult tank. Plan the second one on day one.

<Figure src="/assets/images/red-eared-slider-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of an adult red-eared slider tank showing UVB lighting, a basking platform, strong filtration, and a secure lid" caption="UVB, a dry dock, and a filter bigger than the tank." shareable />

## Size

About 10 gallons of water per inch of shell. That lands at 75 to 100 gallons for one adult. 100 to 125 is better water. Females average about 10 inches of shell, males about 7. A hatchling can start in 20 to 40 gallons. Budget a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07W5346Z2?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="REPTIZOO Large Reptile Terrarium 120 Gallon, Tempered Glass Tank 48in x 24in x 24in">120-gallon-class tank</AffiliateLink> from the start. A secure outdoor pond works for adults in the right climate.

## Temperature

Water 72 to 82F. Basking surface 85 to 95F. Cool-side air mid-70s to low 80s by day, mid-60s to mid-70s at night. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eQY1L5" product="Submersible aquarium heater">heater</AffiliateLink> sized to the water plus a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44eEEFH" product="Basking dome/fixture">basking lamp</AffiliateLink>. UVB does not heat the dock.

## Humidity

Not the main number. The dock has to dry the turtle. A wet dock is how shell problems start.

## Substrate

Bare bottom is the clean default. Large rock bigger than the head is fine. No fine sand. No small gravel.

## Lighting

UVB is required. Ferguson Zone 3. Target UVI about 2.0 to 3.0 at the basking surface. A linear <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FF9GHCDC?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med ReptiSun T5 HO Terrarium Hood with 10.0 Bulb 24in L">T5 HO fixture</AffiliateLink> across the tank, 10 to 12 hours on a timer. Replace the bulb every 6 to 12 months even if it still glows.

<FunFact>
A 2025 PLOS ONE health assessment lists this species among the world's most damaging invasives, mostly from owners dumping turtles they could not house. A secure pond keeps the turtle in and wildlife out.
</FunFact>

## The details that catch people

Oversize the <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vNGY2v" product="Canister filter (Fluval 407, up to 100 gal)">canister</AffiliateLink>. Change water at least weekly. Cycle the tank before the turtle moves in. The [aquarium cycling guide](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/) is that page.

The dock must dry the whole turtle. Water depth at least 1.5 to 2 times the shell length, enough to right itself, not so deep a sick animal cannot surface. Secure lid. They climb. Wash hands after the turtle or the water. The [Salmonella hygiene guide](/blog/reptile-salmonella-hygiene-guide/) is the sink rule. A gravid female needs a nest site or she risks egg binding.

The [encyclopedia](/encyclopedia/animal/red-eared-slider/) is the species page. The [UVB guide](/blog/uvb-lighting-complete-guide/) is Ferguson Zones.
