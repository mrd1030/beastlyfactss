# Guppy Tank Setup: Warm Water, Gentle Filtration, and a Plan for Fry

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Warm water. A cycle. Gentle flow. That is most of it.

<Figure src="/assets/images/guppy-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of a guppy tank showing gentle sponge filtration, dense live plants for fry, and a water test kit for cycling" caption="Warm, cycled, gentle flow." shareable />

## Tank size

5 gallons is the floor for a trio. 10 gallons is the start for 5 or 6. They breed, so 20 gallons is the long tank if sexes stay together. Long and rectangular, not tall. Use a lid. They jump. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4pjiyeT" product="10 gallon tank">10-gallon</AffiliateLink> is the usual first tank.

## Temperature

72 to 82F. Target 76 to 78F. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eQY1L5" product="Submersible aquarium heater">heater</AffiliateLink> unless the room stays warm. About 3 to 5 watts per gallon: 25 to 50 watts on a 10-gallon.

## Water chemistry

pH 7.0 or above. Moderately hard. Soft tap gets a mineral buffer.

## Filtration

Gentle. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3T8xyAd" product="Sponge filter">sponge filter</AffiliateLink> if fry are in the tank. Hang-on-back with adjustable flow if they are not. No bettas. No goldfish.

## Substrate

Fine gravel or sand. Darker gravel shows the color. Nothing sharp.

## Lighting

LED, 8 to 10 hours. Optional for the fish. Useful for plants.

## Cycle and conditioner

Cycle before fish. [Four to six weeks fishless](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/), shorter with bottled bacteria. Every tap fill gets <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3Rg5gTT" product="Water conditioner">conditioner</AffiliateLink>.

<FunFact>
Dense plants like <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4grZYPq" product="Live terrarium plants">Java fern or vallisneria</AffiliateLink> hide fry. Skip the cover if you want predation to hold the numbers.
</FunFact>
