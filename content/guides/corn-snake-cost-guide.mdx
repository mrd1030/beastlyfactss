# How Much Does a Corn Snake Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Corn snakes are one of the most budget-friendly beginner snakes out there. The animal itself is cheap, and once the setup is built, ongoing costs stay low for the length of a corn snake's life, which can stretch two decades or more.

### Extras, Not Counted in the Setup Total

Neither is required for a corn snake, but both are worth adding when the budget allows.

<CostTable guide="corn-snake" section="extras" />

Corn snakes are terrestrial, so floor space matters more than height. The adult minimum is a 40-gallon breeder (36x18x18 inches), running $80 to $250 in glass, or $200 to $400 for a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4f8WVcx" product="4x2x2 ft PVC enclosure">front-opening PVC enclosure</AffiliateLink>. A hatchling can start in a 10 to 20 gallon tank and move up later.

The thermostat genuinely is not optional, our [enclosure setup guide](/blog/corn-snake-tank-setup-guide/) covers the temperature gradient it controls. A heat mat running without one can get hot enough to cause a serious burn.

The table is a solid basic setup. A fuller build, with a PVC enclosure, the optional UVB below and premium fixtures, climbs well past its top end. An initial vet exam, $50 to $160, comes on top of either.

## Month to Month: Roughly $22 to $55

<ComparisonTable
  headers={["Item", "Monthly Cost"]}
  rows={[
    ["Frozen mice, 1 to 2 for an adult", "$10 - $20"],

    ["Vet exam, set aside monthly", "$4 - $13"],

That is the annual table above divided by twelve, and the rodent line is where the range comes from. A mature snake eats once every two to three weeks, so an adult corn snake eats one to two mice a month. Bought fifty at a time, a large adult mouse is $0.99 at RodentPro, which puts the meal bill at about $1 to $2 a month; the $10 to $20 in the table is what it looks like buying a handful at a time. Aspen is about $10 for an 8 quart bag. The heat itself is cheap to run: an 8 watt heat mat around the clock is about 5.8 kWh a month, close to $1 at the June 2026 US residential average of 18.34 cents per kWh, and a 75 watt halogen for 12 hours a day is 27 kWh, about $5. The top of the electricity row covers a bigger enclosure with more fixtures on all day.

<FunFact>
Corn snakes get their name from the pattern on their belly scales, which resembles Indian corn kernels, not from being found in corn fields. They actually are commonly found around corn fields and grain stores in the wild, drawn there by the rodents the grain attracts, so the name works out true either way.
</FunFact>

## The Long Game

Corn snakes commonly live 10 to 15 years in captivity, a well-kept one can pass 20, and the oldest on record reached 32. Over a decade, the %%setup:corn-snake%% setup plus ten years at %%annual:corn-snake%% a year still makes them one of the more affordable long-term reptile pets relative to how long they live. The setup cost is the budgeting decision, everything after that is manageable.
