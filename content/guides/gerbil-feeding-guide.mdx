# Gerbil Feeding: Schedule, Diet, and Why Seed Mixes Fail This Species

A sunflower-seed-heavy mix looks like a complete gerbil diet on the label, but a gerbil will happily pick out the fatty seeds and leave the balanced parts behind, which is exactly how a seemingly complete diet quietly causes obesity. For the rest of their care needs, see our complete [Gerbil care guide](/guides/gerbil/).

## The Staple Diet

A nutritionally complete, uniform pelleted diet or lab block formulated for gerbils, or a low-fat rodent formula, is the best base because it prevents the selective feeding a loose mix invites. The American Gerbil Society is explicit about the target: gerbil food needs a minimum of 14% protein, and many products labeled simply "Hamster and Gerbil Food" fall short of that, they're really formulated for hamsters. Aim for roughly 14 to 16% protein and low fat around 4 to 8%, <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B06XY8RHVB?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Oxbow Garden Select Hamster & Gerbil Food">Oxbow's Garden Select and similar lines</AffiliateLink> land in that range. Supplement with small amounts of fresh vegetables like carrot, broccoli, kale, or squash, and occasional animal protein such as a few mealworms a week or a bit of hard-boiled egg.

## Feeding Frequency and Portions

Feed once daily, roughly 5 to 10 grams, about a tablespoon, of pellets per adult gerbil. Offer fresh vegetables every other day or a few times weekly, and treat fruit as an occasional weekly indulgence given the sugar content. Treats and supplemental foods together should stay under about 10% of the diet.

<KeyTakeaway>
Young gerbils under 6 months and pregnant or nursing females need more calories and protein than a maintenance adult, some commercial diets are formulated to be fed without the usual portion cap during those life stages.
</KeyTakeaway>

## Foods to Avoid

Sunflower-seed-heavy and muesli-style mixes as a staple are the central risk, the RSPCA specifically warns against excessive amounts of fatty seeds like sunflower for exactly the obesity reason above. Toxic foods to keep away entirely: grapes and raisins, rhubarb, citrus fruit, chocolate, onion and garlic, avocado, raw potato, raw kidney beans, and apple seeds, plus salty, sugary, or processed human food and dairy. Watery lettuce can cause diarrhea, and any hoarded fresh food should be removed before it rots in a cache.

## Supplementation

None needed with a complete pelleted diet. Occasional insect protein and fresh produce provide variety rather than correct a deficiency. What matters more day to day is constant fresh water and safe wood to gnaw, gerbil teeth grow continuously.

## The Mistakes That Actually Matter

A seed or sunflower-heavy mix as the staple is the single biggest risk, gerbils pick their favorites and ignore the balanced components, leading to obesity and nutritional imbalance that shortens lifespan.

<FunFact>
Over-treating with fruit or fatty seeds adds weight gain on top of possible dental and digestive problems. And leaving hoarded fresh food in a cache invites spoilage and digestive upset, gerbils are natural hoarders, which makes this an easy habit to miss.
</FunFact>
