# How Much Does a White's Tree Frog Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

White's tree frogs, also called dumpy tree frogs, are cheap to buy and modest to keep. The long runway is the real cost.

## The frog: $20 to $60

Standard animals run $20 to $60. Blue and other designer morphs cost more. Captive-bred is the better choice over wild-caught.

<FunFact>
The Smithsonian's National Zoo has recorded a White's tree frog living 21 years in human care, against an average of about 16. For a pet a lot of people picture lasting a summer, that is a long-lived amphibian.
</FunFact>

## Vet costs

An exotic or amphibian-experienced exam runs $50 to $150. A dedicated exotic emergency visit fee often starts around $250 before treatment. Amphibian veterinary care has real limits. Prevention through husbandry does more work than treatment.

## Lifespan

The Smithsonian's National Zoo puts the average at about 16 years, with one individual at 21 in human care. Budget for well over a decade, not a summer frog.

## The bottom line

Cheap to acquire, modest to maintain, hardy compared with many frogs. The financial risk is the runway, not the first receipt.
