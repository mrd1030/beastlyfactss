# Should You Handle a Pacman Frog? Rarely, and Carefully

Sit-and-wait. Buried. Built to strike at whatever moves near the face. That is why this is not a hold-me pet.

## Why handling stays rare

The bite is strong and it hurts. The lunge is not a fight. It is feeding. Watch it. Do not wave a hand at it.

<FunFact>
Pound for pound the bite has been compared to some predatory dinosaurs relative to body size. The frog spends most of its life not moving.
</FunFact>

## When you must

Short. Support from underneath or at the base of the rear legs. Expect a jump with no plan for the landing. Wash before and after. Skin and Salmonella both matter.

## Never hand-feed

Forceps, tongs, or a dish. Never fingers. It cannot always tell them apart.

## First-timer mistakes

- Hand-feeding
- Two frogs in one tank. They eat each other. One per enclosure.
- Handling to bond

## Sexing

Not as a juvenile. Around 5 to 6 months: males get a dark nuptial pad on the first front digit, loose throat skin, and a loud croak. Females skip the pads and grow much larger, sometimes near twice the mass.

A White's tree frog is the handleable amphibian. This one is not.
