# Hognose Enrichment: They Were Asked, and They Chose

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Most enrichment research measures what an animal does. The hognose study did something better: it let the snakes vote.

<KeyTakeaway>
Given a choice between an enriched side and a standard one, with shelters and a thermal gradient on both, western hognose snakes chose the enriched side, and the preference got stronger as the study went on. Boldness did not predict it. Every snake leaned the same way.
</KeyTakeaway>

## What the Research Says

Nagabaskaran, Skinner and Miller set up a preference test, published in the journal Animals in 2022. The enriched side had burrowing substrate, interactive stimuli and a large water dish. The standard side had paper towel substrate and a smaller dish. Crucially, **both sides had shelters and a thermal gradient**, so what was being compared was enrichment on top of adequate husbandry rather than enrichment against neglect.

The snakes preferred the enriched side, and the preference **strengthened over time**. It was stronger still when the enriched side happened to be the cooler one, which the authors connect to hognose burrowing behavior. No relationship was found between enrichment preference and individual boldness.

## Substrate You Can Vanish Into

The hognose rostral scale is an upturned, shovel-shaped digging tool. A snake on paper towel is carrying specialized equipment it will never use.

Give it enough loose burrowing substrate to disappear into completely, with <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0002DIZKC?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med ReptiSand for burrowing">reptile sand</AffiliateLink> as the sand part of the mix. Depth is the variable, and a [soil and sand mix](/blog/hognose-snake-tank-setup-guide/) that holds a tunnel instead of collapsing does the job better than any single material. A depth a hognose can vanish into takes more substrate than one small bag holds. Do not handle a hognose that is mid-dig, which interrupts the behavior the enclosure exists to allow.

## Put the Digging on the Cool Side

This is the most transferable detail in the study. The preference was stronger when the enriched side was cooler, which means substrate depth and thermal choice reinforce each other when you place them together. Split them up and they compete.

Practically: deep substrate on the cool end, basking surface on the warm end, cover at both. The snake never has to choose between the temperature it wants and the behavior it wants.

## A Water Dish Big Enough to Get Into

The enriched side in the study included a **large** water dish, and that word is doing work. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eKyN0W" product="Large soak-able water dish">dish the snake can enter</AffiliateLink> supports [shedding](/blog/reptile-shedding-complete-guide/) and hydration and gives the animal an option it controls, which is the whole shape of enrichment for a reptile.

## Cover at Both Ends

Two secure hides minimum, one warm and one cool, so being concealed never costs the snake its preferred temperature. <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3SKmfOx" product="Cork bark round hide">Cork tubes and flats</AffiliateLink> plus leaf litter break the floor up further, so the snake has cover everywhere.

Hognoses are famous for their [defensive theater](/blog/hognose-snake-handling-guide/): hooding, hissing, and rolling over to play dead. Those are anti-predator responses, and a snake that performs them constantly during routine keeping is reporting something. Cover reduces how often it feels the need.

## Priority Order

1. Substrate deep enough to burrow into completely
2. Deep substrate placed on the cool side, since that is what the study found preferred
3. A water dish large enough to soak in
4. Secure cover at both ends of the gradient

## Let the Snake Keep Voting

Given the choice, the snakes in the study picked the enriched side more firmly the longer it was on offer, and a home enclosure can put that same choice in front of a hognose every day. Put the depth where the cool is, cover both ends, and let where the snake spends its time tell you what is working.
