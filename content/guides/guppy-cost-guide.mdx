# How Much Does Guppy Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Guppies are cheap to buy and cheap to keep. The cost people do not budget for is more guppies than they planned on.

## The fish: $2 to $20, more for specialty strains

Standard wild-type guppies run $2 to $5 each. Fancy varieties run $10 to $20 per pair. Rare strains, Purple Moscow, 24k Gold, Dragon, and Koi, can run $25 and up, occasionally hundreds from specialty breeders.

<FunFact>
A $200 Dragon guppy and a $3 wild-type guppy are the same species with the same basic needs. The fancier strains tend to be less resilient than plain wild-type fish. You are paying for looks, not hardiness.
</FunFact>

## Vet costs

Most guppy problems are water and over-the-counter medication, not a clinic visit. Dedicated aquatic vets exist and are uncommon. Test the water first.

## Lifespan

2 to 3 years on average, up to 4 or 5 with excellent care. Female fertility drops after around age 2. That is a short commitment next to a sulcata tortoise. The [sulcata tortoise cost guide](/blog/sulcata-tortoise-cost-guide/) is the other end of that scale.

## The cost to plan for: more guppies than you started with

Guppies are livebearers. A female can store sperm for 5 to 10 months after one mating and produce multiple broods from it, up to around 8, without meeting a male again. Put males and females in the same tank with no plan and you will have far more fish within months, which means [more tank space](/blog/guppy-tank-setup-guide/), more filtration, and more of every ongoing cost above.

Budget for growth, not a fixed population.
