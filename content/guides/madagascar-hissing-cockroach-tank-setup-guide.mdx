# Madagascar Hissing Cockroach Enclosure Setup: Humidity, Substrate, and Keeping Them From Climbing Out

<AffiliateDisclosure />

This is genuinely one of the simpler enclosures on this entire site to set up correctly, with one specific detail that matters more than anything else: keeping them from climbing out. This covers the setup itself, for what all of it costs, see our [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach cost guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-cost-guide/), or the full [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach care guide](/guides/hissing-cockroach/) for everything else.

## Enclosure Size

A 5-gallon tank works as a minimum for a single animal or a small group. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4pjiyeT" product="10 gallon tank">10-20+ gallon tank</AffiliateLink> or plastic tub suits a larger colony better, floor space matters more here than height for this ground-dwelling species.

## Temperature

75 to 85°F for general pet-keeping, room temperature above about 70°F is genuinely fine if you're not actively trying to breed them. Breeding specifically gets triggered around 85 to 95°F, worth knowing if you want to avoid an accidental population boom, or if you're deliberately trying to encourage one. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44hAxJ1" product="Under-tank heat mat + thermostat kit">thermostat-controlled heat mat</AffiliateLink> on one side of the enclosure creates a useful gradient if you do want warmer conditions.

## Humidity

60 to 70%, maintained through daily misting and a substrate that holds moisture well. A simple humidity chamber, a small container with a damp sponge inside the main enclosure, works well too, a technique used by professional insect-keeping programs.

## Substrate

<AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CN8ZRYA?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med Eco Earth Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate (8 qt, 2-pack)">Coconut fiber</AffiliateLink>, peat moss, or orchid bark, 2 to 3 inches deep, supports this species' semi-burrowing habits well. Provide egg-crate stacks or <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3SKmfOx" product="Cork bark round hide">cork bark</AffiliateLink> as hides, they'll use these constantly.

## Lighting

None needed. This species is nocturnal and photophobic, genuinely prefers darkness, and doesn't require any special lighting setup at all.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: A Petroleum Jelly Barrier

This is the detail that actually matters most for a successful setup. Madagascar hissing cockroaches are excellent climbers, capable of scaling smooth glass with ease. Smear a band of <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09HHH2Z9Y?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Amazon Basics Petroleum Jelly, Skin Protectant, 7.5 oz">plain petroleum jelly</AffiliateLink> around the top 2 to 3 inches of the enclosure interior, combined with a secure, tight-fitting lid, and escapes become a non-issue. Skip this step, and you'll likely find roaches outside the enclosure sooner or later.

<FunFact>
Their sticky foot pads and leg hooks, the same grip that makes gentle handling important, are exactly what lets them scale smooth glass with ease. A secure lid alone genuinely isn't enough for this species, which is why the plain petroleum jelly barrier around the enclosure's upper interior does so much of the real escape prevention work.
</FunFact>

One compliance note worth knowing: this species can't be legally shipped to Arizona or Florida due to state agricultural regulations, worth checking before ordering if you live in either state.
