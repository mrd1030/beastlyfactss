# Parrotlet Enrichment: Five Inches of Full-Sized Parrot

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Parrotlets are sold on the basis of being small. Everything difficult about them comes from being a parrot.

<KeyTakeaway>
A parrotlet has a full parrot's intelligence, destructiveness and temperament in a five inch body. The requirements do not scale down with the animal, and a parrotlet housed and stimulated like a budgie is the standard route to a nippy, territorial, bored bird.
</KeyTakeaway>

## What the Research Says

Nothing on parrotlets. Two borrowed threads from the wider parrot literature, one from [African greys](/blog/african-grey-parrot-enrichment-guide/) and one from [cockatoos](/blog/cockatoo-enrichment-guide/).

**Foraging opportunity and increased physical complexity both prevented and reduced psychogenic feather picking** in young Amazon parrots, and foraging enrichment has repeatedly improved feather condition across parrot species.

Reviews of feather damaging behavior list **environmental enrichment, foraging enrichment and reinforcement-based training** together as interventions, without ranking one above the others.

Both are borrows from much larger birds. What transfers is the mechanism rather than the scale: an intelligent, social parrot with a full bowl and an empty day.

<FunFact>
Parrotlets are famously unbothered by size differences and will square up to birds several times their weight. It is charming until it is a vet bill, and it is the main reason they are not mixed-aviary birds.
</FunFact>

## Foraging Alongside the Bowl

The cheapest and highest-value change. Wild parrots spend a large share of the waking day working for food, and a full dish as the only source removes all of it.

<AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4wMu5Wu" product="Hanging Sola Ball Chew/Forage Toys, 2 Pack">Hanging forage toys</AffiliateLink> sized for a small beak, plus food wrapped in paper, tucked into cardboard, and split across several sites so the bird searches instead of landing. Rotate the presentation, because a parrotlet works things out fast. None of this changes what goes in: the [pellet base](/blog/parrotlet-feeding-guide/) is the same either way, and a bowl of it stays available as the fallback, since a small bird that fails to solve a toy still has to eat that day.

## Chewing, in Constant Supply

Small beaks that never stop. Untreated soft wood, plain paper, cardboard and consumable shreddable toys, replaced as they get destroyed.

## Perches at Varied Diameter

Small feet on uniform dowel all day is how pressure sores develop. <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4pbY2ge" product="Bird perches (varied diameter)">Perches of varied diameter</AffiliateLink> and natural branch let the grip change through the day.

Site them so there is still a clear span to fly across.

## Training and Attention

Short daily positive-reinforcement sessions sit on the same short list of supported interventions as foraging. Target training, step-up and recall all work fine with a bird this size.

Ten minutes twice a day, ended while the bird is still engaged. It also does more than anything else to keep a territorial little parrot manageable.

## Time Out of the Cage

A parrotlet has to be handled every day to keep its tameness, and the requirement is set in days, not hours. A hand-raised bird left in the cage for a stretch of busy weeks drifts back toward nipping, and winning it back takes longer than the weeks that were skipped.

Every minute out is supervised. The hazards scale with the bird: a parrotlet on the floor gets stepped on, one that squeezes into a gap behind furniture gets trapped, and a cat or dog treats it as prey. Close the doors, put other pets in another room, and keep the session in one proofed space rather than the run of the house. The standing risks a loose parrot finds in any home (fans, open water, nonstick fumes) are the same for every species and sit in the [bird household hazards guide](/blog/bird-household-hazards-guide/).

A keeper who cannot give that daily session is the case a second parrotlet exists for. Two birds of opposite sex keep each other company through a long working day. The trade is the one below: they bond to each other first.

## Company, One Way or the Other

Either a bonded pair or a genuinely present owner. A bonded pair covers the social requirement and usually bonds less to you, which is a trade and not a downside.

Do not mix parrotlets into a community aviary. They are bold out of all proportion and will take on much larger birds.

## Bathing and Sleep

They bathe readily and it keeps feather condition good. Offer shallow water in the morning.

Ten to twelve hours of dark, quiet sleep. Under-slept small parrots get nippier, which is the behavior owners then attribute to temperament.

## Priority Order

1. Part of the food delivered through foraging, with the pellet bowl as the fallback
2. Constant chewing and shredding material
3. Daily short training sessions
4. Perches of varied diameter, with a clear flight span
5. A bonded pair, or daily supervised time out with a genuinely involved owner
6. Ten to twelve hours of dark, quiet sleep
7. Novel objects, rotated and never piled up

## What Not to Do

Do not house a parrotlet like a budgie. Do not make a full bowl the only way food arrives. Do not run out of destructible material. Do not put one in a mixed aviary. Do not use uniform dowel perches or sandpaper covers. Do not skip sleep and then treat the biting as personality. And do not leave one loose around cats, dogs or larger birds, since a parrotlet has no sense of its own size.
