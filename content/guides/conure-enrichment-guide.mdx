# Conure Enrichment: A Big Parrot's Needs in a Small Parrot's Body

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Conures get bought as a manageable parrot. They are a manageable size, which is a different thing. For everything else about them, see our complete [Conure care guide](/guides/conure/).

<KeyTakeaway>
The gap this species lives in is time. Wild parrots spend **up to six hours a day** searching for, selecting and manipulating food. A conure with a full bowl is finished in minutes, and everything that goes wrong behaviourally with parrots tends to grow in that empty stretch.
</KeyTakeaway>

## What the Research Actually Says

Nothing on conures specifically. The borrow is from the parrot foraging literature, covered in full in our [African Grey Parrot enrichment guide](/blog/african-grey-parrot-enrichment-guide/).

**Foraging opportunity and physical complexity both prevented and reduced psychogenic feather picking** in young Amazon parrots. Increased foraging opportunity has repeatedly improved feather condition across parrot species. And in sulphur-crested cockatoos, **training sessions outperformed medication and feeding enrichment** as a plucking treatment, which our [Cockatoo enrichment guide](/blog/cockatoo-enrichment-guide/) covers.

Conures are not Amazons or cockatoos, and what transfers is the shape of the problem: an intelligent social bird with a bowl of food and nothing to do.

<FunFact>
The six hour figure is worth sitting with. It is not that wild parrots are hungry for six hours, it is that finding and processing food is the activity that fills a parrot's day. Removing it does not give the bird leisure, it gives the bird a vacancy.
</FunFact>

## Foraging, as the Default Way Food Arrives

The highest-value change and it costs almost nothing. Stop using a bowl as the primary delivery method.

A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4fFoJqn" product="Kyouki Natural Foraging Box Toy">foraging box</AffiliateLink> plus improvised versions: food wrapped in paper, pushed into cardboard tubes, threaded on skewers, hidden in several places around the cage so the bird has to search rather than arrive.

Rotate it. A conure solves a puzzle quickly and then it stops being one. Our [feeding guide](/blog/conure-feeding-guide/) covers the diet, and the presentation is what this page is about.

## Destruction, Provided On Purpose

Conures chew and shred constantly. Supply it deliberately and they wreck the supply. Do not supply it and they wreck the cage, the furniture, or their own feathers.

## Space and Out-of-Cage Time

A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4wuT808" product="Large bird flight cage">large flight cage</AffiliateLink> is the baseline, and hours out of it daily is what actually matters. A conure needs a stand, a proofed room and things to climb and chew outside the bars.

Fill the cage properly but leave a flight path. Our [tank setup guide](/blog/conure-tank-setup-guide/) covers dimensions and bar spacing.

## A Place to Sleep Enclosed

Many conures, green cheeks especially, are cavity roosters and prefer to sleep in an enclosed hanging space rather than on an open perch. A sleeping pouch suits that.

Check it constantly. Frayed threads and chewed holes are how birds get toes caught, and a conure will chew the pouch. Replace rather than repair.

## Training

Given the cockatoo result, short daily positive-reinforcement sessions are worth building into the routine. Target training, step-up, recall and simple problem solving.

Ten minutes twice a day, ending while the bird is still engaged. It is the intervention with the best evidence in the parrot literature and the one owners most often skip.

## Sleep and Noise

Ten to twelve hours of dark quiet sleep, which is the invisible cause of a lot of parrot behaviour problems.

The noise itself is not fixable. Conures are loud for their size and sun conures especially so. Choose accordingly rather than expecting enrichment to quiet the bird. Our [health issues guide](/blog/conure-health-issues-guide/) covers what chronic stress and poor sleep look like when they show up physically.

## Priority Order

1. All food delivered through foraging, across several sites
2. Constant destructible material
3. Hours out of the cage in a proofed space
4. Daily short training sessions
5. Ten to twelve hours of dark, quiet sleep
6. An enclosed sleeping space, checked often
7. Novel objects, rotated rather than accumulated

## What Not to Do

Do not use a food bowl as the main delivery method. Do not buy a conure expecting a quiet bird. Do not leave a frayed sleeping pouch in the cage. Do not skip sleep. Do not reinforce screaming with attention, since coming when the bird shrieks teaches it to shriek. And see our [handling guide](/blog/conure-handling-guide/) before assuming a bird that wants constant contact will accept being put down.
