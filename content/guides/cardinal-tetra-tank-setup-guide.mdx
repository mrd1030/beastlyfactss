# Cardinal Tetra Tank Setup: Soft Water, Warmer Temps, and a Blackwater Feel

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Cardinal tetras are native to the upper Orinoco basin and the Negro River in the Amazon basin, according to a [2023 phylogeography study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10257900/) of the species, the same kind of soft, dim, tannin-stained blackwater country neon tetra comes from. This species just runs a few degrees warmer and is usually kept in a slightly bigger school. This covers the full setup, for what it all costs, see our [Cardinal tetra cost guide](/blog/cardinal-tetra-cost-guide/), or the full [Cardinal tetra care guide](/guides/cardinal-tetra/) for everything else.

## Tank Size

A 10-gallon tank is workable for the bare minimum school of 6. [Seriously Fish](https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/paracheirodon-axelrodi/) recommends a base footprint of roughly 60x30cm, close to 24x12 inches, which lines up well with a 20-gallon long tank rather than a standard 10-gallon. If you're stocking the fuller 8 to 10 fish school this species is usually kept in, that's the better practical minimum. Schooling behavior plays out horizontally, so favor a longer tank over a taller one.

For the more typical 8 to 10 fish school, a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eSbKl2" product="20 gallon long tank">20-gallon long tank</AffiliateLink> is worth the upsize, our [handling guide](/blog/cardinal-tetra-handling-guide/) covers why the school size itself matters as much as the tank does.

## Temperature

73 to 81°F is the range most care sources converge on, according to [Fishlore](https://www.fishlore.com/profiles_cardinal_tetra.htm), with [Seriously Fish](https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/paracheirodon-axelrodi/) extending comfortable tolerance up to about 84°F. [Practical Fishkeeping](https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/features/cardinal-tetra-paracheirodon-axelrodi/) draws the comparison directly: neon tetra tops out comfortably around 25°C (77°F), while cardinal tetra tolerates water closer to 30°C (86°F). A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eQY1L5" product="Submersible aquarium heater">heater</AffiliateLink> is necessary for any tank this size, and keeping the temperature stable matters as much as hitting the right number.

<FunFact>
A 2008 laboratory study in [Acta Amazonica](https://acta.inpa.gov.br/fasciculos/38-4/BODY/v38n4a23.html) pushed cardinal tetra to genuine extremes to find its lethal limits: total mortality set in below roughly 15°C (59°F) and above about 35°C (95°F), with the fish's 50% survival thresholds landing near 19.6°C and 33.7°C. That's a useful reminder of the difference between what a fish can survive and what it actually needs to thrive, the real comfort range keepers should aim for is the much narrower 73 to 84°F band above, not the edges of what the species can technically tolerate.
</FunFact>

## Water Chemistry

Cardinal tetras want soft, acidic water. [Fishlore](https://www.fishlore.com/profiles_cardinal_tetra.htm) puts the ideal pH around 5.0, while [Seriously Fish](https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/paracheirodon-axelrodi/) gives a wider comfortable range extending toward neutral. In practice, aiming for roughly pH 5.0 to 7.0, toward the lower end where possible, works well, and it's worth testing your source water before assuming your setup is automatically suitable. Ammonia and nitrite both need to read zero before stocking, our [Cardinal tetra health issues guide](/blog/cardinal-tetra-health-issues-guide/) covers what a completed cycle is actually protecting against.

## Filtration

Keep the flow gentle. Seriously Fish describes this species' native habitat as slow-moving forest streams full of fallen branches and leaf litter, not open water with strong current. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3T8xyAd" product="Sponge filter">sponge filter</AffiliateLink> is a common, effective choice, and a hang-on-back filter works too if you can baffle or reduce its output.

## Substrate

Fine gravel or sand, and a darker color makes cardinal tetra's red and blue stripe stand out more against the background, the same principle covered in our [Neon tetra tank setup guide](/blog/neon-tetra-tank-setup-guide/) for that closely related species.

## Lighting

Dim to moderate. This species comes from shaded, densely vegetated streams, and bright, intense lighting stresses fish built for filtered, dappled light rather than open water.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Recreate Blackwater Conditions

This is the detail that takes a cardinal tetra tank from adequate to genuinely great. <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eR0Z2l" product="Driftwood">Driftwood</AffiliateLink> and Indian almond leaves release natural tannins that stain the water and lower pH, mimicking the blackwater habitat this species evolved in, the same fallen branches and leaf litter Seriously Fish describes as the defining feature of its native streams.

Always quarantine new fish before adding them, given the disease risk covered in our [health issues guide](/blog/cardinal-tetra-health-issues-guide/), and keep them well fed on a proper <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B004PBB5TK?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Hikari Tropical Semi-Floating Micro Pellets, 0.77 oz">micro-pellet food</AffiliateLink> sized for their small mouths. Always use a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3Rg5gTT" product="Water conditioner">water conditioner</AffiliateLink> on tap water before it goes in the tank, dechlorinating and neutralizing chloramine and heavy metals that are otherwise harmful to fish.
