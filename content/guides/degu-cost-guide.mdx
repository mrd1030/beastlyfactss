# How Much Does Degu Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Degus are affordable individually, and the budgeting point with this species is that "individually" isn't how you should be thinking about the purchase at all.

## The Degu: $10 to $50 Each

Whether from a breeder, pet store, or rescue, individual degus run $10 to $50, with adoption fees often landing at the lower end. Since this species is obligately social, plan on at least a same-sex pair from day one rather than a single degu. Two degus at $10 to $50 each is the real starting line, not the single-animal price tag most first-time buyers plan around.

One cost worth flagging early: sugary treats and fruit are off the table for this species, which actually keeps the diet-related budget simpler, not more expensive.

## Vet Costs

You'll want an exotics-experienced vet. The two most likely reasons for a vet visit, [dental work for overgrown teeth and diabetes management](/blog/degu-health-issues-guide/), both come up often enough in this species to budget for proactively rather than treating as a remote possibility.

## Lifespan: Long for a Small Rodent

5 to 9 years in captivity, against an animal that seldom passes 2 years in the wild, where it is prey. Chinchillas, a similarly long-lived small pet, typically run even longer at 10 to 20 years, and that extra decade, along with a nocturnal schedule against the degu's daytime one, is what [a degu set against a chinchilla](/blog/degu-vs-chinchilla-guide/) comes down to.

<FunFact>
This is notably longer than many small rodents, longer than a hamster, and worth sitting with as a real multi-year commitment before buying a pair. A hamster's cost math resets every year or two; a degu's setup is something you're likely living with for the better part of a decade.
</FunFact>

## The Honest Budgeting Takeaway

The individual animal's low price tag doesn't reflect the ask here, which is two animals, a spacious cage built for a social pair, and a diet that requires real discipline around sugar. None of this is expensive in absolute terms, but it's more than the sticker price on a single degu suggests. The gerbil is the other social, day-active rodent sold in pairs, and [a pair of gerbils costs](/blog/gerbil-cost-guide/) far less to house for a much shorter commitment; [Two Pets Where You're Really Buying a Pair](/blog/degu-gerbil-overview/) weighs the two side by side.

The [Degu encyclopedia profile](/encyclopedia/animal/degu/) covers the Chilean scrubland colonies that make a pair the starting unit.
