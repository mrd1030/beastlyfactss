# How Much Does Degu Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Degus are genuinely affordable individually, and the real budgeting point with this species is that "individually" isn't really how you should be thinking about the purchase at all. For the full care picture beyond budgeting, see our complete [Degu care guide](/guides/degu/), or our companion guides on [handling](/blog/degu-handling-guide/), [health issues](/blog/degu-health-issues-guide/), and [enclosure setup](/blog/degu-tank-setup-guide/).

## The Degu: $10 to $50 Each

Whether from a breeder, pet store, or rescue, individual degus run $10 to $50, with adoption fees often landing at the lower end. Since this species is obligately social, covered in the [handling guide](/blog/degu-handling-guide/), budget for at least a same-sex pair from the start rather than one animal. Two degus at $10 to $50 each is the real starting line, not the single-animal price tag most first-time buyers plan around.

One cost worth flagging early: sugary treats and fruit are off the table for this species, more in the [health issues guide](/blog/degu-health-issues-guide/), which actually keeps the diet-related budget simpler, not more expensive.

## Vet Costs

You'll want an exotics-experienced vet. The two most likely reasons for a vet visit, dental work for overgrown teeth and diabetes management, both come up often enough in this species to budget for proactively rather than treating as a remote possibility, covered in full in our [Degu health issues guide](/blog/degu-health-issues-guide/).

## Lifespan: Genuinely Long for a Small Rodent

5 to 8 years is typical, with some degus reaching 10 to 13.

<FunFact>
This is notably longer than many small rodents, longer than the hamster already covered on this site, and worth sitting with as a real multi-year, potentially decade-long commitment before buying a pair. A hamster's cost math resets every year or two; a degu's setup is something you're likely living with for the better part of a decade.
</FunFact>

## The Honest Budgeting Takeaway

The individual animal's low price tag doesn't reflect the real ask here, which is really two animals, a genuinely spacious cage built for a social pair, and a diet that requires real discipline around sugar. None of this is expensive in absolute terms, but it's more than the sticker price on a single degu suggests.
