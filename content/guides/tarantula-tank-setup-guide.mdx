# Tarantula Enclosure Setup: Shape, Substrate, and a Water Dish

<AffiliateDisclosure />

No lighting rack. No heat gradient for most rooms. Shape and substrate still matter.

<Figure src="/assets/images/tarantula-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of a tarantula enclosure showing the secure ventilated lid, deep substrate, hide, and water dish" caption="Low and wide. Acrylic lid. Three inches of substrate. A water dish." shareable />

## Enclosure shape and size

Horizontal, wider than tall. About 20 by 10 by 10 inches for an adult, or a 10-gallon, sized to about three times the leg span. Height under about 12 inches.

Secure lid. Never mesh. Acrylic with drilled holes.

## Substrate

At least 3 inches. Fill roughly half to two-thirds of the enclosure. Coconut fiber, peat, or organic pesticide-free soil.

<FunFact>
Wild terrestrial tarantulas spend a lot of time in burrows. Depth here is not decoration. It is the burrow.
</FunFact>

## Furnishings

One hide: <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3SKmfOx" product="Cork bark round hide">cork bark</AffiliateLink>, a half-log, or half a terracotta pot. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44hAzR9" product="Small/shallow water dish">shallow water dish</AffiliateLink>, changed daily, shallow enough that it cannot drown.

## Temperature

Room temperature, about 70 to 80F, is enough in most houses. If the room runs cold, a heat mat on the side, never the bottom, on a thermostat. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3QHrM81" product="Digital thermometer/hygrometer combo">digital thermometer/hygrometer</AffiliateLink> beats guessing.

## Humidity

A full water dish and one damp corner. Light mist with a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vPKMjX" product="Fine mist spray bottle">fine mist bottle</AffiliateLink> if that corner dries. Heavy misting grows mold.

## Lighting

None. They are nocturnal. No bright light. No direct sun.

## Diet and housing

Live crickets or <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4uFr6hU" product="Dubia Roaches (feeder bag)">dubia</AffiliateLink> no larger than the abdomen. Pull uneaten prey in 24 hours. The [feeding guide](/blog/tarantula-feeding-guide/) is the schedule. The [molting guide](/blog/invertebrate-molting-guide/) is the window before a molt.

One spider per enclosure.
