# How Much Does a Madagascar Hissing Cockroach Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Madagascar hissing cockroaches are the most affordable pet on this entire site, both to buy and to keep.

## The Cockroach: $3 to $65

Individual nymphs run around $3 each. A breeding pair runs about $24, and a starter colony of 25 runs around $65. Because this same species is also sold widely as a feeder insect for reptiles, availability and pricing stay consistently low across most sources, this is genuinely one of the cheapest animals on this entire site to acquire.

A tank or plastic tub in the 5 to 20 gallon range with a secure, screened lid is the enclosure baseline, paired with coconut fiber substrate, a hide or two, and a water dish. The optional heat mat and thermostat only earns its keep if you're breeding rather than just keeping a small display group, and [an unthermostatted mat is a real overheating risk](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-health-issues-guide/) rather than a theoretical one.

## Ongoing Costs: A Few Dollars a Month

Fresh vegetables and fruit, occasional commercial roach or dog food, and modest substrate replacement make up the entire monthly cost. This is about as low-maintenance as a pet gets financially.

## Vet Costs

Effectively none. Invertebrates are rarely taken to a vet, and very few vets treat them at all. Care here is entirely husbandry-based, correct humidity and substrate do all the real work.

<FunFact>
This exact species is sold both as a pet and as a feeder insect for larger reptiles. As pets, they're valued for their manageable size, calm temperament, genuine longevity, and the hiss itself. As feeders, that same animal is valued for its large body size and how well it gut loads nutrients for whatever's eating it, proof that the low price tag is a function of wide production, not a lesser animal.
</FunFact>

## Lifespan: 2 to 5 Years

2 to 5 years is the typical range, most commonly 2 to 3, with 4 to 5 achievable under excellent care. This is a genuinely long-lived invertebrate for the cost, especially compared to something like a [praying mantis](/blog/praying-mantis-cost-guide/), which lives only a fraction as long.

## The Honest Value Proposition

If cost is a real factor in choosing a pet, this is close to the floor. A complete setup for a small group commonly costs less than a single vet visit for most pets, and the ongoing cost is close to negligible. What you're trading for that affordability is a display and observation pet rather than a handling-focused one. The [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach encyclopedia profile](/encyclopedia/animal/hissing-cockroach/) has the full natural history.
