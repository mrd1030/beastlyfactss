# How Much Does a Madagascar Hissing Cockroach Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Madagascar hissing cockroaches are genuinely the most affordable pet on this entire site, both to buy and to keep. For everything else about day to day care, see our full [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach care guide](/guides/hissing-cockroach/).

## The Cockroach: $3 to $65

Individual nymphs run around $3 each. A breeding pair runs about $24, and a starter colony of 25 runs around $65. Because this same species is also sold widely as a feeder insect for reptiles, availability and pricing stay consistently low across most sources, this is genuinely one of the cheapest animals on this entire site to acquire.

A tank or plastic tub in the 5 to 20 gallon range with a secure, screened lid is the enclosure baseline, paired with coconut fiber substrate, a hide or two, and a water dish. The optional heat mat and thermostat only really earns its keep if you're breeding rather than just keeping a small display group. Sizing details and the one escape prevention step that actually matters most are covered in our [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach tank setup guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-tank-setup-guide/).

## Ongoing Costs: A Few Dollars a Month

Fresh vegetables and fruit, occasional commercial roach or dog food, and modest substrate replacement make up the entire monthly cost. This is about as low-maintenance as a pet gets financially. For exactly how much to offer and how often, see our [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach feeding guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-feeding-guide/).

## Vet Costs

Effectively none. Invertebrates are rarely taken to a vet, and very few vets treat them at all. Care here is entirely husbandry-based, correct humidity and substrate do all the real work, our [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach health issues guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-health-issues-guide/) covers exactly what that looks like in practice.

<FunFact>
This exact species is sold both as a pet and as a feeder insect for larger reptiles. As pets, they're valued for their manageable size, calm temperament, genuine longevity, and the hiss itself. As feeders, that same animal is valued for its large body size and how well it gut loads nutrients for whatever's eating it, proof that the low price tag is a function of wide production, not a lesser animal.
</FunFact>

## Lifespan: 2 to 5 Years

2 to 5 years is the typical range, most commonly 2 to 3, with 4 to 5 achievable under excellent care. This is a genuinely long-lived invertebrate for the cost, especially compared to something like a [praying mantis](/blog/praying-mantis-cost-guide/), which lives only a fraction as long.

## The Honest Value Proposition

If cost is a real factor in choosing a pet, this is close to the floor. A complete setup for a small group commonly costs less than a single vet visit for almost any other animal on this site, and the ongoing cost is close to negligible. What you're trading for that affordability is a display and observation pet rather than a handling-focused one. Our [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach handling guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-handling-guide/) covers what that actually looks like day to day, or browse the rest of our [Invertebrates care guide category](/guides/category/invertebrates/) and the [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach encyclopedia profile](/encyclopedia/animal/hissing-cockroach/) for more.
