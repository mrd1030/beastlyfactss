# Quaker Parakeet Cage Setup

<AffiliateDisclosure />

A Quaker's cage needs to genuinely support an intelligent, assertive, chew-happy bird with a strong instinct to build, and getting the size, bar spacing, and enrichment right matters more than it might for a quieter species. For the full care picture, see our complete [Quaker Parakeet care guide](/guides/quaker-parakeet/), and for what all of it costs, our [cost guide](/blog/quaker-parakeet-cost-guide/).

## Cage Size

A reasonable minimum is 24 by 24 by 36 inches, though a wider <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4ymrwMJ" product="Prevue Hendryx Wrought Iron Flight Cage (5 ft, rolling)">flight-style cage</AffiliateLink> in the 30 to 36 inch range is a better real-world target for this active bird. Use 1/2 to 5/8 inch bar spacing, don't exceed 3/4 inch, and choose heavy-gauge bars, this species is a genuinely strong chewer.

## Temperature

Normal comfortable room temperature works well. Avoid drafts and temperature extremes, and never leave a Quaker outdoors once temperatures climb toward 90°F.

## Humidity

No special humidity requirement, normal household levels are fine.

## Substrate and Lining

Plain cage liner or newspaper, changed regularly, is genuinely all this species needs on the cage floor, no loose particulate bedding required.

## Lighting

Roughly 3 to 4 hours of daily UVB exposure supports healthy vitamin D and calcium metabolism. Provide a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4fbLrVy" product="Penn-Plax E-Cuttlebone, Omega 3 & Honey Enriched (2 pack)">cuttlebone</AffiliateLink> as an additional calcium source.

<FunFact>
A Quaker only about eleven inches long uses every inch of a cage this size, stocky, busy, and territorial in a way that makes floor space and bar strength matter more than the bird's own dimensions would suggest.
</FunFact>

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Manage the Nest-Building Instinct Deliberately

This is genuinely the detail that separates a well-adjusted pet Quaker from a hormonal, territorial one, covered from the behavior side in the [handling guide](/blog/quaker-parakeet-handling-guide/). Provide plenty of shreddable wood and <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4wMu5Wu" product="Hanging Sola Ball Chew/Forage Toys, 2 Pack (BBjinronjy)">foraging toys</AffiliateLink>, this species needs an outlet for its natural building instinct, but **never provide a permanent nest box for a pet bird**, doing so reliably triggers unwanted breeding-style hormonal aggression. Offer varied perch diameters to support foot health, and plan for several hours of supervised out-of-cage time daily, this is too intelligent and social a bird to thrive on cage time alone.
