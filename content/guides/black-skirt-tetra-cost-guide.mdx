# How Much Does Black Skirt Tetra Ownership Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

The black skirt tetra (Gymnocorymbus ternetzi), also sold as the black widow tetra, comes from the Rio Paraguay and Guaporé basins of South America and grows to about 2 to 2.5 inches in a home aquarium, 3 inches at most. The trade runs on captive-bred fish, and a whole school costs less than the tank it lives in.

## The Fish: %%animal:black-skirt-tetra%% for a School of 12

Pet stores sell plain black skirts for about $3 to $6 a fish. The white skirt and the long-fin are the same species, need the same care, and sell inside that same band. Twelve is the number to price, because a school that size keeps the species' fin nipping inside the group, and 12 fish come to %%animal:black-skirt-tetra%% at the store.

Online sellers often charge less per fish in packs of 12, but live fish travel overnight, and the shipping on one order can cost as much as the fish in it.

GloFish Skirt Tetras are black skirts too: a genetically engineered line of the white skirt, kept exactly like the plain fish. The color is the whole price difference. Stores charge about $12 to $13 apiece, so a school of 12 lands around $144 to $156, against %%animal:black-skirt-tetra%% for plain fish.

<FunFact>
Black or white is settled by two genes working as a pair. A skirt tetra grows the dark body and vertical bars only when it carries a dominant copy at both of them. Lacking that at either one, it develops pale and unbanded: the white skirt.
</FunFact>

## Upfront Setup: %%setup:black-skirt-tetra%%

<CostTable guide="black-skirt-tetra" section="necessities" />

The tank priced for 12 fish is a 20 gallon long, with a 30 x 12 inch (75 x 30 cm) floor. Store tags sometimes list 10 gallons for this fish, and the [black skirt tetra tank setup guide](/blog/black-skirt-tetra-tank-setup-guide/) explains why the longer tank is the one to buy.

Black skirts are subtropical fish kept at 68 to 79°F (20 to 26°C), so the heater's work is holding the water inside that span when the room cools. A gentle filter suits a fish whose wild home is slow creeks under overhanging plants. Thick clumps of planting with a few floating plants to dim the light show the black at its best, and the middle of the tank stays open for swimming.

Before any fish go in, the liquid test kit has to show a finished cycle, a result read off the kit rather than counted in days, as the [aquarium cycling guide](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/) lays out. Captive-bred black skirts are unfussy about water chemistry and do well anywhere from pH 6.0 to 7.5, so the budget carries no softening or acidifying products. A tap that reads outside that span is the case [what moves pH, GH and KH](/blog/freshwater-ph-gh-kh-guide/) deals with.

## The Extras: A Spare Tank for Quarantine

<CostTable guide="black-skirt-tetra" section="extras" />

New arrivals live apart for 30 days before joining the display. A bare 20 gallon long is priced here because twelve fish of this size need room for a month, and the [fish quarantine guide](/blog/fish-quarantine-and-treatment-guide/) covers running it. Ich gets treated in that tank too, and since heat for this species stops at the 79°F top of its range, the cost of [an ich outbreak](/blog/black-skirt-tetra-health-issues-guide/) is a bottle of salt or medication.

## Ongoing Costs: Low

Black skirts take almost any food offered. Flakes or small pellets make the everyday diet, and the first supply in the table costs %%price:fish-food-micro-pellets%%. Small frozen or live foods such as daphnia and bloodworm, given now and then, are the only other food spend.

A bottle of water conditioner runs %%price:water-conditioner%% and gets used at every change, which makes the [water change schedule](/blog/aquarium-water-changes-guide/) the thing that sets how often it is replaced. Power for the heater and filter and fresh test reagents cover the remainder.

## Vet Costs

Seldom part of the budget. Black skirts stay healthy on clean water, with over-the-counter treatment when something breaks out. When fish begin to die, [the signs that tell a water problem from a disease](/blog/spotting-a-sick-fish-guide/) decide whether the money goes on a water change, a medicine, or a vet who sees fish.

## Lifespan

A black skirt tetra lives about 3 to 5 years. The heater, the filter and the 20 gallon long keep working long after that, which spreads their cost across more than one school.
