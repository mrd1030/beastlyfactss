# How Much Does an Ackie Monitor Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Ackie monitors are a genuine step up in investment from most beginner lizards, both in what the animal costs and in what it takes to house one properly. For the full care picture beyond budgeting, see our complete [Ackie Monitor care guide](/guides/ackie-monitor/).

## The Monitor: $150 to $450

Yellow ackies, the more common form in the pet trade, typically run toward the lower end of this range. Red ackies are larger, less commonly available, and command a real premium, often reaching $250 to $450 for a baby.

This is where ackie ownership genuinely separates from more casual reptile keeping. A large custom or PVC enclosure, deep burrowing substrate, a UVB fixture, multiple high-wattage basking bulbs, a thermostat, a Retes stack, hides, and monitoring equipment add up fast. ReptiFiles is direct about this, reptiles in general aren't cheap pets, and the ackie's specific space and heat requirements push the total well past what a bearded dragon or leopard gecko setup would run. If you're weighing the jump from a more sedentary lizard, our [Bearded Dragon tank setup guide](/blog/bearded-dragon-tank-setup-guide/) shows exactly what a comparatively modest setup looks like by contrast.

## Ongoing Costs: Roughly $40 to $80 a Month

Feeder insects, dubia roaches and crickets, plus calcium and vitamin supplements, and meaningful electricity for the high-wattage basking bulbs this species needs. Culturing your own roaches can bring the feeder cost down over time.

<FunFact>
An ackie monitor's $800 to $1,500 upfront setup has to work for 15 to 20 years, roughly the same lifespan the animal itself is expected to reach. That long horizon is exactly why the enclosure and equipment, not the $150 to $450 purchase price of the monitor, end up being the real financial commitment in this hobby.
</FunFact>

## Vet Costs

A routine exotic exam runs $50 to $100, with a fecal test adding $25 to $50. Emergency visits start at $150 to $300 for the exam alone, and a serious case involving X-rays and bloodwork can reach $500 to $1,500.

## Lifespan

15 to 20 years with proper housing and care. This is a long-term commitment on top of a genuinely demanding setup, worth weighing both factors together rather than focusing on the relatively modest purchase price of the animal itself.
