# Jumping Spider Enclosure Setup: Small, Simple, and All About Humidity

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Jumping spider setups are small, inexpensive, and genuinely simple once you understand the one factor that matters most: keeping humidity consistent. For what all of it costs, see our [cost guide](/blog/jumping-spider-cost-guide/).

## Enclosure Size

A minimum of about 4 by 4 by 7 inches, vertically oriented, works for an adult, and something closer to 8 to 10 inches tall gives more genuine climbing room. This is an arboreal species, up and down space matters more than floor area. Use a front-opening design with cross-ventilation, and house one spider per enclosure, they're cannibalistic toward each other.

A tall, vertical enclosure like the <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0041P5PBE?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Exo Terra Glass Nano Terrarium Kit, 8 x 8 x 12 in">Exo Terra Nano</AffiliateLink> fits these proportions well.

## Temperature

72 to 82F suits this species well, and normal room temperature is usually fine without any supplemental heat at all. If your space runs consistently below about 70F, a thermostat-controlled heat mat on the side wall works, but never place heat under the substrate, and never run one without a thermostat.

## Humidity: The Factor That Matters Most

Target 50 to 60% for Phidippus species like the regal jumping spider, tropical genera need more. Mist one corner of the enclosure every 2 to 3 days with dechlorinated water, using a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vPKMjX" product="Fine mist spray bottle">fine mist spray bottle</AffiliateLink>, and aim to leave drinkable droplets rather than soaking everything. Track it with a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3QHrM81" product="Digital thermometer/hygrometer combo">digital hygrometer</AffiliateLink> rather than guessing. Good ventilation matters here too, humidity without airflow invites mold.

## Substrate

<AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CN8ZRYA?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med Eco Earth Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate (8 qt, 2-pack)">Coconut fiber</AffiliateLink>, or a coconut fiber and sphagnum moss blend, about an inch or two deep, helps buffer humidity swings between mistings. Avoid sand or vermiculite, and skip using bare paper towel as a long-term substrate, it doesn't hold moisture well enough.

## Lighting

This species is diurnal and genuinely relies on vision to hunt, so provide bright ambient light on a normal 12-hour day and night cycle. UVB is not required for this species.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Leave Room Near the Top

Jumping spiders build their silk retreat near the top of the enclosure, so it's worth positioning your access opening lower or to the side.
