## Parasites on wild-caught Indonesians

Most Indonesians in the US trade are wild-caught.

<FunFact>
Wolf and colleagues, Acta Veterinaria Scandinavica 2014, found parasites in 93.2% of the reptile fecal samples they examined. Northerns, almost all captive-bred, sit far lower.
</FunFact>

Fecal, then fenbendazole or praziquantel to match what shows. Budget it on unknown origin.

## Respiratory infection

Discharge, bubbling, open-mouth breathing, wheezing. Fix <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3QHrM81" product="Digital thermometer/hygrometer combo">temperature and humidity</AffiliateLink> first. Advanced cases need a vet and antibiotics. Dust is a cause too.

## Obesity

They eat. They do not self-limit. Portion about the size of the skull. The [feeding guide](/blog/blue-tongue-skink-feeding-guide/) is the schedule.

## Retained shed and scale rot

Dry air holds shed on toes and tail. Damp substrate rots the belly.
