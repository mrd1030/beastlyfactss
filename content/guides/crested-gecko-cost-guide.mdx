# How Much Does a Crested Gecko Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Crested geckos sit near the affordable end of the reptile hobby, and unlike heat-hungry desert species, a lot of their ongoing cost comes down to food and humidity upkeep rather than electricity.

## The Gecko Itself

Most normal and patterned crested geckos from a breeder or store sell for under $100, and standard morphs top out around $200. Rare and specialty morphs from established lines run upward of $1,000. Adoption through a rescue typically costs $50 to $175, and a "free" gecko from a rehoming post often comes with medical bills attached. Males show a [hemipenal bulge](/blog/crested-gecko-handling-guide/) only at 18 to 25 grams, so a juvenile sold below that weight is unsexed, whatever the listing guesses.

## Costs That Catch Owners Later

- **A second gecko is a second setup.** Two males can never share, and even two females need at least double the 18x18x24 inch single-adult minimum, about 15,500 cubic inches, plus duplicate dishes and hides.
- **Every adult female needs a lay box.** Females lay infertile eggs with no male present, typically two eggs about once a month through the season, so a lay box of moist coconut fiber and fir bark is part of her kit whether she is bred or not. Each clutch draws on her calcium, which keeps the supplement line on the monthly budget.
- **Emergencies cost more than exams.** A routine exotic vet visit runs about $40 to $70, but advanced imaging for an injury or egg binding can pass $1,000.

<FunFact>
Crested geckos were considered extinct for much of the twentieth century until a 1994 field expedition rediscovered them in New Caledonia, after a tropical storm that year. Every crested gecko in the pet trade today descends from that rediscovered population.
</FunFact>

## The Long-Game Cost

Crested geckos live 10 to 20 years in captivity, and the earliest known captive individuals have lived into their 30s. That's the number worth sitting with before bringing one home. This isn't a short-term pet, and the setup you buy now should be one you're comfortable maintaining for decades.

## One Gecko, One Enclosure, Twenty Years

The expensive surprises with this species come from a second animal or an egg-bound female, so one gecko in one well-built vertical enclosure is the cheapest version of this pet. Buy the enclosure and the thermostat at the right spec on day one, and the rest is a jar of powder, a few insects and a yearly exam for the next two decades. Set beside a [leopard gecko](/blog/leopard-gecko-vs-crested-gecko-guide/), most of the difference is heat.
