# How Much Does a Tarantula Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Cheap per year. Long if it is a female.

## The spider: $25 to $100

Beginner species $25 to $100. A Chilean rose hair or curly hair often $20 to $50. Spiderlings cost less than adults.

## Vet care

No insurance. Often no vet. The emergency that matters is a dry dish and a shriveled abdomen, not a wellness plan.

<FunFact>
Females keep molting for life. Males stop after the last molt. That is a large part of 15 to 20 years versus 4 to 7.
</FunFact>

## Longevity

Female Chilean rose hair: 15 to 20 years or more. Male: 4 to 7. Tiny monthly cost. Real years.
