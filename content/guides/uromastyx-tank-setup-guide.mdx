# Uromastyx Enclosure Setup: Hotter and Drier Than You'd Guess

## Enclosure Size

4 feet long by 2 feet wide by 2 feet tall is the minimum for species up to about 18 inches. Larger species, the Egyptian uromastyx especially, need up to 8x4x4 feet for a full-grown adult. House one uromastyx per enclosure.

## Temperature: The Defining Requirement

Basking surface temperature needs to reach 120 to 130F, with the giant Egyptian species needing at least 130F. Cool zone should sit around 85F, with nighttime dropping to around 65F, usually without needing supplemental night heat.

## Humidity: The Other Defining Requirement

ReptiFiles puts it plainly: uromastyx need humidity levels low enough to make a bearded dragon look tropical by comparison, just 20 to 30% ambient. High humidity makes this species genuinely sick. A humid microclimate within a burrow is beneficial, the key is keeping the surface layer of substrate dry while allowing moisture to persist deeper down. A few coastal species, like the Yemen uromastyx, want closer to 50% humidity. See our [Bearded Dragon tank setup guide](/blog/bearded-dragon-tank-setup-guide/) for how much more humid that species' own setup runs by comparison.

## Substrate

Fine sand or a sandy soil mix, or a DIY blend of roughly 50% play sand, 30% topsoil, and 20% excavator clay, packed at least 4 inches deep to support burrowing. Avoid moisture-retaining substrates like coco fiber, mulch, or moss, and skip loose calcium sand or walnut shell products entirely, both carry real impaction risk.

## Lighting

Strong UVB is required, not optional, for this diurnal species. A high-output <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/43AcIvA" product="Arcadia T5 UVB Reptile Light Fixture Kit (36&quot; / 14% UVB Dragon)">T5 HO bulb in the 14% range</AffiliateLink>, targeting a basking-area UVI of 4.5 to 6.0, paired with a bright daylight LED bar. Replace UVB bulbs on the manufacturer's schedule, typically every 6 to 12 months. A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B076GXJFJG?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Solarmeter Model 6.5R Reptile UV Index Meter">UV index meter</AffiliateLink> is an optional way to confirm your setup is actually hitting that 4.5 to 6.0 target rather than guessing.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Seeds in the Diet

Uromastyx are strict herbivores, unusual among commonly kept lizards. Beyond leafy greens and vegetables, this species needs seeds like lentils and millet as a regular dietary component, and gets most of its hydration from food rather than drinking.
