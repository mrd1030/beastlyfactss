# Western Hognose Snake Tank Setup: Dry, Deep, and Built for Burrowing

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Keep it dry. Keep the substrate deep enough to disappear into. Put a halogen bulb over the substrate. That is most of the husbandry.

<Figure src="/assets/images/hognose-snake-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of a Western hognose snake enclosure showing the halogen basking bulb, low-output UVB, deep dry substrate, and a dedicated humid hide" caption="Keep it dry, keep the substrate deep enough to burrow in, and get the heat source right." shareable />

## Enclosure size

Roughly one square foot of floor space per foot of snake. Adult males: a 20-gallon-equivalent (around 30x13x13 inches) as a minimum. Females grow larger: a 40-gallon-equivalent (36x18x16 inches). A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CJB82285?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="REPTI ZOO 50 Gallon PVC Reptile Enclosure, 36x18x18 inch">2x2x2 foot or similarly sized front-opening PVC enclosure</AffiliateLink> works as an adult setup for either sex.

Floor space matters more than height. This is a burrower, not a climber. If you use glass, cover three sides. The lid has to actually latch. Hognoses push gaps.

## Temperature

Basking 90 to 95F on one end. Cool side 70 to 75F. Night around 75 to 78F.

Heat is a low-wattage halogen basking bulb over the substrate, on a thermostat or dimmer. A heat mat or ceramic emitter is not the primary source. The <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44hAxJ1" product="Under-tank heat mat + thermostat kit">thermostat</AffiliateLink> is not optional either way.

## Humidity: keep it low

30 to 50% ambient. Do not mist the whole enclosure. One <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3SKmfOx" product="Cork bark round hide">humid hide</AffiliateLink> for shedding. Moisture only there. A damp enclosure is a path to respiratory infection and scale rot.

A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3QHrM81" product="Digital thermometer/hygrometer combo">digital thermometer and hygrometer</AffiliateLink> is core equipment. This husbandry hangs on humidity you can read, not guess.

## Substrate: deep enough to burrow in

3 to 6 inches of loose, dry, diggable substrate. About 70% soil-based to 30% reptile-safe sand. <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3TkxAVF" product="Aspen shavings substrate">Aspen or coconut fiber</AffiliateLink> works as the soil-based part. Keep it dry. The [cool end is where a hognose digs](/blog/hognose-snake-enrichment-guide/).

Feed off the substrate, in a separate container, so the snake does not swallow bedding. The [feeding guide](/blog/hognose-snake-feeding-guide/) is the page that sizes the prey.

<FunFact>
Hognoses are diurnal. That is why this setup uses a low-output UVB bulb and a 12-hour light and dark cycle, which a nocturnal ball python setup does not.
</FunFact>

## Lighting

A 12-hour light and dark cycle. A low-output linear T5 spanning part of the enclosure. This species is out in the day. Treat UVB as part of the build, not an optional extra.

## The non-obvious requirement

Deep, dry, burrowable substrate is the core of the setup, not decoration. Combined with off-substrate feeding, that is most of hognose husbandry. It is drier and more active than a [corn snake](/blog/corn-snake-tank-setup-guide/) or [ball python](/blog/ball-python-tank-setup-guide/) needs.

The [hognose encyclopedia](/encyclopedia/animal/hognose-snake/) covers the species itself.
