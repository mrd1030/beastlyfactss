# How Much Does a Leaf-Tailed Gecko Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Leaf-tailed geckos are the most expensive gecko covered on this site, and the price reflects both their striking camouflage and the skill involved in breeding them successfully.

## The Gecko: $250 to $800+

Satanic leaf-tailed geckos, the smallest and most dramatically leaf-mimicking species, commonly run $750 to $800. Larger species cost less per animal: Henkel's leaf-tailed geckos run roughly $290 to $500, mossy leaf-tailed around $250, and the giant leaf-tailed species around $350.

<FunFact>
Pricing runs backward from what you'd expect with this genus. The smallest species, the satanic leaf-tailed gecko, is also the most expensive, commonly costing more than double the larger Henkel's, mossy, or giant leaf-tailed geckos.
</FunFact>

## Species and Locality, Not Color Morphs

Unlike many reptiles on this site, leaf-tailed geckos aren't sold by designer color morph. Pricing is driven by which species you're buying and whether it's captive-bred or wild-caught, not selectively bred color genetics. Natural variation, including bark mimics, dead-leaf mimics, and lichen-covered patterns, exists across the group, but there's no real morph market to speak of.

Altogether, this species runs roughly $300 to $600 before the gecko itself.

## Ongoing Costs: Roughly $20 to $40 a Month

Live insects, crickets, dubia roaches, and silkworms among the common choices, plus calcium and multivitamin supplements and modest electricity for misting equipment and lighting.

## Vet Costs

A routine exotic exam runs $60 to $135, with a fecal test adding $15 to $35. Emergency visits start around $150 to $300 for the exam alone, and a serious case with diagnostics and treatment can reach $500 to $1,500. Reptile-experienced vets aren't universally available, worth locating one before you need it.

## Lifespan

5 to 15 years in captivity. Wild-caught individuals consistently live shorter lives than captive-bred ones, one more reason captive-bred is strongly worth the higher price for this species specifically.

## The Honest Budgeting Takeaway

The higher purchase price for this species reflects genuine rarity and breeding difficulty, not marketing.
