# Pacman Frog Feeding: Schedule, Diet, and Why They Stop Eating

The problem is getting them to stop, not to start.

## Feeding schedule by age

- **Juveniles:** daily.
- **Adults:** twice a week. Drop to once a week if the body looks bloated. Stay at three times a week if it looks thin.

Prey no wider than the head. Round, not bloated. They will keep eating.

## What Pacman frogs eat

Gut-loaded crickets, dubia roaches, earthworms or nightcrawlers. Mealworms and waxworms as treats only. Silkworms and hornworms are softer. Adults only: thawed frozen rodents, never live, never microwaved, roughly every other week at most. Juveniles get no rodents.

Gut-load 24 to 72 hours. Dust calcium with D3 most days for juveniles, a few times a week for adults. Multivitamin about once a week. A cricket-only diet is a metabolic bone disease path. Earthworms are the calcium exception. Everything else needs dust.

Tongs. Never fingers.

## Foods to avoid

Live rodents. Microwaved rodents. Refrozen refused prey. Wild-caught insects. Fireflies. Sand or gravel substrate: they strike blind and swallow it.

## Why Pacman frogs stop eating

1. **Temperature or humidity.** 75 to 85F day, 65 to 75F night, 50 to 80% humidity.
2. **A move or handling.**
3. **Shedding.** They often eat the skin. Appetite dips.
4. **Aestivation.** Mucus cocoon, months without food or water. Can start any time the tank drifts. Fatal under about 12 months. Keep heat and light steady on a young frog.
5. **Breeding cool-down.** A planned 60-day dry rest. Not a pet default.
6. **Pickiness.** New prey gets refused once.
7. **Illness.** Parasites, red-leg, weight loss with lethargy.
8. **Impaction.** Substrate in the strike.

## When to worry

An adult holding weight can miss 2 to 4 weeks. A juvenile past about a week is already a closer look. Weigh weekly. About 10% down over a couple of weeks is the call.

See an exotics vet for lethargy, visible weight loss, a sunken look, no normal feces, a month off food, or red or inflamed skin. A frog under a year that stops eating and looks dormant is not left to sleep it out.
