# How Much Does Cardinal Tetra Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Cardinal tetras cost about the same as neon tetras to buy and keep, and the budgeting variable is the same one too: this is a schooling fish, so you're never pricing out a single animal.

## The Fish: About $3 Each

Cardinal tetras run about $3 a fish from online sellers, a little less per fish in a larger pack. Since this is a schooling species, you're buying a group, not one fish. A starting school of 8 to 10, the range most care sources recommend for this species, runs roughly $25 to $30.

Fish prices last checked September 2026 at Aquahuna and LiveAquaria. Retail prices move; treat these as a snapshot, not a quote.

That's a slightly larger recommended school than [neon tetra](/blog/neon-tetra-cost-guide/), which is commonly kept from a floor of 6. It's a small difference, but it's a real one worth budgeting for rather than discovering after the fact.

<FunFact>
Cardinal tetra sometimes prices a touch higher than neon tetra, and there's a real supply-chain reason behind it. Neon tetra is now almost entirely captive-bred. Cardinal tetra is genuinely harder to breed at commercial scale, so a meaningful share of the trade still comes from a documented wild fishery on Brazil's Rio Negro. Cardinal tetra alone makes up roughly 80% of the artisanal aquarium fish trade coming out of the Rio Negro region.
</FunFact>

Food and conditioner are low, close to what [neon tetra](/blog/neon-tetra-cost-guide/) costs to run. The test kit is the one item worth budgeting for on purpose: cardinal tetras shrug off more ammonia than most people expect but are sensitive to nitrite, the compound that shows up next in an incompletely cycled tank. Confirming your cycle before stocking, not after, is the cheapest insurance this species has, and [what a finished cycle reads on a test kit](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/) is a different question from how long the tank has been running.

## Vet Costs

Not applicable in the traditional sense. Fish health management here is entirely about water quality and, when needed, over-the-counter treatments, not veterinary visits.

## Lifespan

A well-kept cardinal tetra lives about five years in captivity under good, stable conditions, and some individuals reach ten. That is dramatically longer than what this species gets in the wild, where it is close to an annual fish in its native blackwater streams. During the dry season, the shallow pools these fish live in shrink and strand huge numbers of them, and most don't survive to see a second year. An aquarium, stable water and no seasonal dry-out, is a genuinely easier life than the one this fish evolved for.

## Why This Costs About What Neon Tetra Does

Cardinal tetra and neon tetra land in almost the same budget bracket, and the schooling requirement is the reason both do. Budgeting for a full group from the start, rather than starting with two or three and adding more later, is both the more humane approach and the more cost-effective one, since an understocked, stressed school is more prone to health issues, which cost more to correct than simply stocking correctly on day one.
