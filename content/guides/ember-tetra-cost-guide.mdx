# How Much Does Ember Tetra Ownership Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

A school of ten ember tetras costs less than the tank it swims in. Each fish is an orange characin about 0.8 inch (2 cm) long from the Araguaia River basin of central Brazil, and it is kept in groups. Nobody buys one. The purchase is a school, and most of the budget goes into the planted tank, the spare tank beside it and, for fish ordered online, the shipping.

## The Fish: %%animal:ember-tetra%% for a School of 10

Online sellers list ember tetras at about $2.50 to $3.50 a fish before shipping, with the per-fish price dropping in larger packs. The recommended purchase is a minimum of 8 to 10, and 10 is the number to price: a school of 10 comes to %%animal:ember-tetra%% before shipping, which is charged on top.

That lands the ember in the same bracket as a school of cardinal tetras, which are also priced at a group of 10.

Buy the whole school at once. Every batch of new fish spends its own 30 days in quarantine before it joins the display, so ten fish bought together go through one wait, and the same ten bought in threes and fours go through three.

<FunFact source="Seriously Fish">
The ember tetra goes by several names in the trade. Shops also sell it as the fire tetra, the red tetra, the dwarf red tetra and Amanda's tetra, so any of those labels on a tank can mean the same fish. The scientific name on the label, Hyphessobrycon amandae, settles it.
</FunFact>

## Upfront Setup: %%setup:ember-tetra%% for a Planted 10 Gallon

<CostTable guide="ember-tetra" section="necessities" />

A 10 gallon tank with an 18 x 12 inch (45 x 30 cm) footprint is the size priced here for a school of 10. A 5 gallon nano sometimes gets suggested for a fish this tiny, and the [ember tetra tank setup guide](/blog/ember-tetra-tank-setup-guide/) sets out why 10 gallons is the recommendation instead.

Every other line follows from where the fish comes from. Embers are tropical and kept at 75 to 82°F (24 to 28°C), so the heater is not optional. Filtration only needs to be gentle, which a small sponge filter covers. Dense planting and a dark floor suit them best, which is why the sand in the table is a black one, and a few floating plants help on top.

The liquid test kit earns its place twice. It confirms the tank has finished cycling before the school goes in, which is a reading on a kit and not a number of days: ammonia and nitrite are tested until neither registers. Fish added before that point tend to turn lethargic, stop eating and often die, and replacing a school costs more than the kit. The kit also reads pH, and embers come from soft, weakly acidic water: a pH of 5.0 to 7.0 and a hardness of 1 to 10 dGH. Hardness takes a separate GH test, and what does and does not move GH and KH decides whether soft water is worth chasing for a given tap.

## The Extras: A Spare Tank for Quarantine

<CostTable guide="ember-tetra" section="extras" />

New fish get 30 days in a separate tank before they join the display. Thirty days is the minimum, and a longer stay is sometimes needed. The rule holds wherever the fish come from: a local store, an online seller or another keeper. The tank stays bare, with no substrate or decor, and the [fish quarantine guide](/blog/fish-quarantine-and-treatment-guide/) covers how to run it.

The same tank is where an ich outbreak gets treated. The ember's 82°F ceiling rules out the heat cure, so a bottle of salt or medication is the likely cost of one.

The quarantine tank keeps earning its place after the first school. Each new batch, whether two replacement fish or a second group of ten, restarts the 30-day clock, and the display school waits until it runs out. With a lifespan of about 2 to 4 years, a school gets topped up or replaced well within the life of the equipment, so this tank gets used again.

## Buying Checklist: What Healthy Stock Looks Like

A healthy ember looks the same in a store tank as in a home one. Check the fish, and then check the tank they share.

| What to check | Buy | Walk away |
|---|---|---|
| Skin and scales | Smooth and unblemished | Spots, patches or sores |
| Fins | Intact | Bloody or ragged |
| Eyes | Bright | Cloudy |
| Swimming | Steady, moving normally around the tank | Circling, drifting or listless |
| The tank itself | Clean, with healthy-looking fish around them | Dirty, or holding fish that look sick |

Color varies with sex. Adult males are the more intensely colored fish, especially in spawning condition, and females are rounder in the body, so a school with a few duller, plumper fish in it is a normal mix.

The checklist does not replace the quarantine tank. It decides which fish go into it.

## Shipping Fish Online

Ordering online skips the store tank and swaps it for hours in a bag. On a long trip, ammonia builds up from the fish's own waste and pH drops as carbon dioxide accumulates. That is why a shipped school goes into a dark room on arrival, with the tank lights off, and why tank water is mixed into the bag a little at a time over 30 to 60 minutes. The bag water itself goes down the drain, never into the tank.

## Ongoing Costs: Food, Conditioner and Test Kit Refills

Embers are micropredators of tiny invertebrates and zooplankton, with small mouths suited to tiny, slow-sinking food. The staple is a micro pellet or a flake crushed fine, and the first supply in the table runs %%price:fish-food-micro-pellets%%. Dry food kept at room temperature should be discarded 2 months after it is opened, so that pot is a purchase every two months whether or not ten fish have finished it, and a small container is a better buy than a large one.

Small live or frozen foods such as baby brine shrimp and moina round out the diet, which adds a small recurring cost on top of the pellets. Frozen food keeps for up to a year in the freezer, and thawed food should not be refrozen, so a cube comes out only when it is about to be fed.

Water conditioner at %%price:water-conditioner%% a bottle, a little electricity for the heater, filter and light, and test kit refills make up the rest. The heater and light add to the power bill every month, by an amount that depends on the equipment and the room. Conditioner goes into every change, so the water change schedule a tank runs on is what sets how often a new bottle is needed.

## Vet Costs

Rarely a line item. A school of embers is kept healthy through water quality and, when needed, over-the-counter treatment, dosed in the quarantine tank priced above. When fish start dying, the [signs that separate a water problem from a disease](/blog/spotting-a-sick-fish-guide/) decide whether the next spend is a water change, a medication, or a visit to a vet who treats fish.

## Lifespan

Ember tetras live about 2 to 4 years in captivity. That is short for the money spent on the tank: the heater, filter, test kit and planted 10 gallon all outlast the first school and can house the next one.
