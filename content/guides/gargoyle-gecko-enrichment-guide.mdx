# Gargoyle Gecko Enrichment: Build Up, and Fill What You Build

<AffiliateDisclosure />

The most common mistake with arboreal geckos is buying a tall enclosure and leaving the top two thirds empty.

<KeyTakeaway>
The closest studied relative is the crested gecko, where individuals that engaged with novel objects sooner and for longer had lower baseline stress-hormone levels. That is a correlation, not proof that novelty causes calm, and it is still the best steer available for this genus.
</KeyTakeaway>

## What the Research Says

Nothing on *Rhacodactylus auriculatus*. Two borrowed threads.

**Crested geckos.** A study comparing reaction to novel objects against fecal glucocorticoid metabolite levels in two nocturnal gecko species found crested geckos had **higher and more variable stress-hormone levels** than leopard geckos, and that individuals **interacting with novel objects sooner and longer had lower basal levels**. The full study sits with the [crested gecko](/blog/crested-gecko-enrichment-guide/).

**Leopard geckos.** Five enrichment types were tested and ranked, with thermal and feeding highest and visual lowest. That work sits with the [leopard gecko](/blog/leopard-gecko-enrichment-guide/).

<FunFact>
For an arboreal gecko, the gargoyle lives surprisingly low. Wild animals are found mostly within a few meters of the ground rather than up in the canopy, and they come down to the forest floor to hide or to cross between plants. A good enclosure uses the full height and still gives the bottom third something worth visiting.
</FunFact>

## Vertical Structure, Filled

Gargoyles are arboreal and spend their time on vertical surfaces. An <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vlb6Bp" product="18x18x24 glass terrarium">18x18x24 terrarium</AffiliateLink> is a reasonable adult footprint, and the height only counts once there is something in it.

Cork slabs run vertically, branches set horizontal, diagonal and vertical so the gecko can climb, jump and rest at different heights, and dense <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4grZYPq" product="Live terrarium plants">live planting</AffiliateLink> that holds humidity and gives cover at every level. The test is whether the gecko can get from the floor to the top without touching glass.

That test matters for a gargoyle's health as well as its activity. An animal that repeatedly sleeps hanging upside down risks floppy tail syndrome, where the tail folds over the back, and the prevention is clutter: enough hides and decor that the gecko has better places to sleep. A 3D background with ledges turns dead wall into climbing surface and does the same job. Every piece of decor should do at least one of three jobs: a place to hide, a place to climb or jump, or a surface that holds water droplets after misting. Broad leaves and moss do the last one well.

## The Bottom Third

Because wild gargoyles spend time near and on the ground, the floor is part of the enrichment, not dead space under the planting. Leaf litter over the substrate, a low cork tube or hollow, and plants rooted at ground level give a gecko reason to come down to forage for released insects and somewhere to hide when it does.

## Cover at Every Height

An arboreal gecko needs to be able to hide *up there*, not only at the bottom. Broad leaves, cork tubes mounted high, and planting dense enough to disappear into mean the animal can be concealed wherever it happens to be.

## Feeding, Both Halves

A prepared <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00KO450UC?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Pangea Watermelon Complete Gecko Diet, 8 oz">fruit-based diet</AffiliateLink> in shallow dishes, placed at different heights so getting to food involves climbing.

Then live insects released into the planting, which is the half a dish cannot do. Feeding ranked second in the leopard gecko study, and hunting is the part that gets removed when everything arrives in a bowl.

## Novelty, Carefully Framed

Occasional new cork or a rearranged branch is reasonable and cheap. Keep the expectation modest: visual enrichment ranked last in the leopard gecko study, and structure the gecko can use every night matters more than novelty it sees once.

## Priority Order

1. Vertical structure that is genuinely climbable
2. Cover available at every height
3. Humidity and live planting
4. Feeding presentation across both diet halves
5. Feeding stations placed to require climbing
6. Occasional novelty, last

## What Not to Do

Do not buy height and leave it empty. Do not house gargoyles together casually, since they are known for nipping tails in shared enclosures, unlike the colonial mourning gecko. And do not treat handling as enrichment for a species that drops its tail.
