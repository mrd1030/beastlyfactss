## What the Research Actually Says

Nothing on *Rhacodactylus auriculatus*. Two borrowed threads.

**Crested geckos.** A study comparing reaction to novel objects against faecal glucocorticoid metabolite levels in two nocturnal gecko species found crested geckos had **higher and more variable stress-hormone levels** than leopard geckos, and that individuals **interacting with novel objects sooner and longer had lower basal levels**. Our [Crested Gecko enrichment guide](/blog/crested-gecko-enrichment-guide/) covers this in full.

**Leopard geckos.** Five enrichment types were tested and ranked, with thermal and feeding highest and visual lowest. Covered in our [Leopard Gecko enrichment guide](/blog/leopard-gecko-enrichment-guide/).

<FunFact>
The direction of that crested gecko correlation is genuinely unclear. Bolder geckos may be less stressed, or less stressed geckos may be bolder. A care guide that presents it as "novel objects reduce stress" is overreading it, and the honest version is more interesting anyway.
</FunFact>

## Vertical Structure, Actually Filled

Gargoyles are arboreal and spend their time on vertical surfaces. An <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4vlb6Bp" product="18x18x24 glass terrarium">18x18x24 terrarium</AffiliateLink> is a reasonable adult footprint, and the height only counts once there is something in it.

Cork slabs run vertically, branches at varied angles, and dense <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4grZYPq" product="Live terrarium plants">live planting</AffiliateLink> that holds humidity and gives cover at every level. The test is whether the gecko can get from the floor to the top without touching glass. Our [tank setup guide](/blog/gargoyle-gecko-tank-setup-guide/) covers dimensions and humidity.

## Cover at Every Height

An arboreal gecko needs to be able to hide *up there*, not only at the bottom. Broad leaves, cork tubes mounted high, and planting dense enough to disappear into mean the animal can be concealed wherever it happens to be.

## Feeding, Both Halves

A prepared <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00KO450UC?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Pangea Watermelon Complete Gecko Diet, 8 oz">fruit-based diet</AffiliateLink> in shallow dishes, placed at different heights so getting to food involves climbing. Other flavours and sizes are available.

Then live insects released into the planting, which is the half a dish cannot do. Feeding ranked second in the leopard gecko study, and hunting is the part that gets removed when everything arrives in a bowl. Our [feeding guide](/blog/gargoyle-gecko-feeding-guide/) covers the balance.

## Novelty, Carefully Framed

Given the crested gecko correlation, occasional new cork or a rearranged branch is reasonable and cheap. Do not oversell it to yourself: visual enrichment ranked last in the leopard gecko study, and the crested gecko finding does not establish direction.

## Priority Order

1. Vertical structure that is genuinely climbable
2. Cover available at every height
3. Humidity and live planting
4. Feeding presentation across both diet halves
5. Feeding stations placed to require climbing
6. Occasional novelty, last

## What Not to Do

Do not buy height and leave it empty. Do not house gargoyles together casually, since they are known for nipping tails in shared enclosures, unlike the colonial mourning gecko. Do not let the enclosure dry out, covered in our [health issues guide](/blog/gargoyle-gecko-health-issues-guide/). Do not overread the novel-object correlation.
