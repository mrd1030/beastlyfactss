# Common Madagascar Hissing Cockroach Health Issues (Prevention Does Almost All the Work)

<VetDisclaimer />
<AffiliateDisclosure />

Invertebrates are rarely taken to a vet, and very few vets treat them at all, so this list leans heavily on prevention rather than treatment. This species is genuinely hardy, and nearly everything that goes wrong traces back to humidity or basic enclosure hygiene, both easy to get right once you know what to watch for. Get the enclosure right first, our [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach tank setup guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-tank-setup-guide/) covers the husbandry side, and see our full [Madagascar Hissing Cockroach care guide](/guides/hissing-cockroach/) for everything else.

## Desiccation (Too Dry)

Low humidity causes real problems with molting and can lead to death if left uncorrected. The fix is straightforward, maintain 60 to 70% humidity through regular misting and a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CN8ZRYA?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Zoo Med Eco Earth Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate (8 qt, 2-pack)">moisture-holding coconut fiber substrate</AffiliateLink>, covered in more detail in the [tank setup guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-tank-setup-guide/).

## Molting Complications

A freshly molted cockroach is soft and pale white for several hours after shedding its exoskeleton, and genuinely vulnerable during that window. Never handle a roach during or immediately after a molt, it can be injured easily while its new exoskeleton is still hardening, this is covered in more depth in our [handling guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-handling-guide/). Overcrowding and low humidity both make molting problems more likely.

<FunFact>
For several hours after a molt, a hissing cockroach is soft, pale white, and effectively defenseless, its hardened, pigmented exoskeleton hasn't set yet. That short window is exactly why gentle, minimal handling matters so much more around molting time than at any other point in this species' life.
</FunFact>

## Mold and Mite Outbreaks

From uneaten wet food combined with poor ventilation, this can genuinely devastate a colony if it takes hold. Prevention is simple and worth treating as routine maintenance: remove any uneaten fresh food within 24 hours, ensure the enclosure has adequate airflow, and do a deeper clean periodically rather than only when a problem appears.

## Overheating

A heat mat used without a thermostat is a real risk, capable of overheating the roaches or even melting a plastic tub enclosure. If you're using supplemental heat, mainly relevant for breeding rather than casual pet-keeping, always run it through a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44hAxJ1" product="Under-tank heat mat + thermostat kit">thermostat</AffiliateLink>.

## Injury From Mishandling

Not a disease, but the most common keeper-caused harm on this list. A dropped or forcefully pulled roach can be genuinely maimed. This connects directly to the gentle handling technique covered in the [handling guide](/blog/madagascar-hissing-cockroach-handling-guide/), most harm here is entirely preventable with a bit of care.

## The Husbandry Pattern Worth Remembering

Humidity that's too low causes desiccation and molting problems, the most serious issues this species faces. Wet, uneaten food combined with poor airflow invites mold and mites that can spread through an entire colony. And unthermostatted heat is a real overheating risk.
