# How Much Does a Pacman Frog Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Pacman frogs, named for their enormous mouths and round bodies, are one of the more affordable amphibians to keep, and their sedentary lifestyle keeps both the setup and the ongoing cost genuinely modest. For everything else about day-to-day care, see our complete [Pacman Frog care guide](/guides/pacman-frog/).

## The Frog: $15 to $100

Standard morphs, green and albino varieties, typically run $20 to $30. Unique morphs like strawberry, mint, patternless, or apricot run higher, roughly $65 to $100. Overall the range across common species runs $15 to $100.

## Vet Costs

An exotic or amphibian-experienced vet exam commonly runs $50 to $150 based on published exotic clinic pricing, with a dedicated exotic emergency fee often starting around $250 at specialty hospitals. Impaction, covered in detail in the [health issues guide](/blog/pacman-frog-health-issues-guide/), is the condition most likely to require a costly vet visit for this species.

## Lifespan

6 to 10 years is the most commonly cited range, though some sources suggest 10 to 15 years is achievable with excellent care. Either way, this is a genuine multi-year commitment for an animal that spends most of its life buried and waiting.

## The Real Budgeting Takeaway

Pacman frogs are among the cheapest exotic pets to set up properly: low purchase price, modest enclosure, minimal ongoing cost. The financial risk that actually matters here isn't routine expense, it's the vet bill that follows an impaction from careless feeding or the wrong substrate, both entirely preventable and covered in detail in our [tank setup](/blog/pacman-frog-tank-setup-guide/) and [health issues](/blog/pacman-frog-health-issues-guide/) guides.
