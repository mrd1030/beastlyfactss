# How Much Does a Pacman Frog Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Pacman frogs, named for their enormous mouths and round bodies, are one of the more affordable amphibians to keep, and their sedentary lifestyle keeps both the setup and the ongoing cost modest.

## The Frog: %%animal:pacman-frog%%

Standard morphs, green and albino varieties, typically run $20 to $30. Unique morphs like strawberry, mint, patternless, or apricot run higher, roughly $65 to $100. Overall the range across common species runs %%animal:pacman-frog%%.

## Upfront Setup: %%setup:pacman-frog%%

<CostTable guide="pacman-frog" section="necessities" />

A modest terrarium is all this species needs, since Pacman frogs are sedentary and don't need much floor space. Get the terrarium and <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CY23K32S?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="VCEPJH Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate">substrate</AffiliateLink> right from day one, both matter for this species' impaction risk. Even with the frog added, that is a modest first bill for an exotic pet, and most of the spread comes from the morph and the terrarium size.

## Ongoing Costs: Low

Feeder insects and occasional supplements make up nearly the entire monthly cost. The first year costs the most: a juvenile eats every day until it reaches adult size at 6 to 12 months, while an adult eats only one to three times a week, so the feeder bill drops once the frog is grown. A jar of <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D7JWFV5W?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Repashy Calcium Plus 6 oz JAR">calcium with D3 and multivitamin</AffiliateLink> lasts a long time at the dusting frequency this species needs. Electricity for a small heat mat is minimal, and substrate only needs changing every couple of months.

## Vet Costs

A Pacman frog should see a vet once a year, so one routine exam belongs in every year's budget. An exotic or amphibian-experienced vet exam commonly runs $50 to $150, with a dedicated exotic emergency fee often starting around $250 at specialty hospitals. Impaction, the risk that defines this species, is what usually leads to a bill like that.

## Lifespan

6 to 10 years is the most commonly cited range, and 10 to 15 years is achievable with excellent care. Either way, this is a genuine multi-year commitment for an animal that spends most of its life buried and waiting.

## The Budgeting Takeaway

Pacman frogs are among the cheapest exotic pets to set up properly: low purchase price, modest enclosure, minimal ongoing cost. The financial risk that actually matters here isn't routine expense, it's the vet bill that follows an impaction from careless feeding or the wrong substrate, both entirely preventable.

A [White's tree frog](/blog/whites-tree-frog-cost-guide/) costs more to set up, because a climbing frog needs a taller enclosure and UVB, and it is the more handleable of the two. Set against a hamster and a blue-tongued skink in [four pets and four lessons](/blog/hamster-skink-whitestreefrog-pacmanfrog-overview/), this frog is the cheapest to run and the least interactive.
