# How Much Does a Pacman Frog Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Cheap to buy. Cheap to run. The expensive mistake is substrate in the mouth.

## The frog: $15 to $100

Green and albino standards $20 to $30. Strawberry, mint, patternless, apricot $65 to $100.

## Ongoing costs

Feeders and a jar of <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D7JWFV5W?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Repashy Calcium Plus 6 oz JAR">calcium with D3</AffiliateLink>. Heat is a small mat. Substrate every couple of months.

<FunFact>
This frog spends most of its life half buried, waiting. The bite is the other half of the animal.
</FunFact>

## Vet costs

Exam $50 to $150. Exotic emergency fee often from $250. Impaction is the bill.

## Lifespan

6 to 10 years. A buried animal for a decade is still a decade.

## The takeaway

Low purchase price, small tank, cheap months. The risk is the preventable impaction visit.
