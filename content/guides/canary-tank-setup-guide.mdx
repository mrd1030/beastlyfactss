# Canary Cage Setup: Width Matters More Than Height

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Canary cage setup is simple, and the one detail that trips up first-time owners is choosing a tall, narrow parrot-style cage when this species needs the opposite shape entirely.

## Cage Size and Shape

24 inches long by 18 inches wide by 18 inches tall is a reasonable minimum, but the shape matters more than the raw dimensions. Canaries fly horizontally rather than climbing the way parrots do, so prioritize width over height, a genuine <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4wuT808" product="Large bird flight cage">flight cage, wide and long,</AffiliateLink> suits this species far better than a tall, narrow cage built with a climbing bird in mind.

## Bar Spacing

Half an inch or less, this prevents both head entrapment and escape.

## Temperature

Normal household temperature, 65 to 80°F, works well, with 65 to 75°F frequently cited as ideal. Keep the cage away from drafts, air conditioning vents, and radiators. No supplemental heat is needed indoors.

## Humidity

No special humidity requirement for this species. Provide a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000I6ORHS?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Lixit Quick Lock Bird Cage Bath for Lovebirds, Canaries, Finches, Parakeets, and Cockatiels">shallow bird bath</AffiliateLink>, canaries genuinely enjoy bathing and it supports healthy feather condition.

## Substrate and Cage Lining

A paper liner on the cage floor, spot-cleaned daily and fully changed weekly, is the standard, low-maintenance approach. Avoid galvanized metal or lead-painted cage components entirely, powder-coated steel is the safe standard.

## Lighting

Bright, indirect natural or artificial light with a consistent day and night cycle supports normal behavior and, in males, healthy singing. Avoid placing the cage in direct sun where it could overheat. Our [Canary handling guide](/blog/canary-handling-guide/) covers why that singing behavior is specifically tied to males in the first place.

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Genuine Air Quality Sensitivity

This is worth taking seriously. Canaries are extremely sensitive to airborne toxins, non-stick cookware fumes, aerosols, and smoke can all be genuinely dangerous, keep the cage well away from the kitchen and any source of fumes or sprays.
