# How Much Does a Canary Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Canaries are one of the more affordable pet birds you can buy, and the cost scales up meaningfully once you get into specialized song and show breeds.

## The Bird: %%animal:canary%%

Common yellow canaries from pet stores run $25 to $60. Red Factor canaries, bred specifically for color, run $80 to $150. Specialized song breeds, American Singers, German Rollers, and Spanish Timbrados among them, run $100 to $250 or more, with prize singers and exhibition quality birds reaching $200 to $500.

Males typically cost 20 to 50% more than females, since only males sing. That price gap is a direct reflection of demand, most buyers want a singing bird specifically.

## Upfront Setup: %%setup:canary%%

<CostTable guide="canary" section="necessities" />

A quality flight cage runs %%price:cage-flight-24x18x18-half-inch%% on its own, and it's worth spending real money here. Canaries fly horizontally rather than climbing the way parrots do, so a wide, long cage suits this species far better than a tall, narrow one built with a climbing bird in mind.

## Ongoing Costs: Roughly %%monthly:canary%% a Month

Seed or pellet food, fresh produce, cage liners, and occasional toy replacement make up the monthly total, modest for a pet bird next to the larger parrot species. The food line stays small because the bird is small: most canaries eat one to two teaspoons a day of pellets with a little seed, so a bag lasts a long time. The liner is the most frequent purchase, since the paper gets replaced at least weekly, and more often with two birds in one cage.

## Vet Costs

An annual avian wellness exam, worth budgeting for even though it's easy to skip, runs %%vet:canary%% or more. Mite treatment or a respiratory illness can add meaningfully to that if it comes up.

## Lifespan

6 to 12 years is the typical span, with up to 15 reported. The longevity record for a captive canary is 24 years. That is one exceptional bird, so plan on the typical span. It is a long commitment for a bird often bought on impulse for its song.

## What a Canary Costs Over Its Life

Run the monthly range and the annual exam across the typical lifespan and the running cost comes to roughly $170 to $460 a year. Over 6 to 12 years that is about $1,000 to $5,500, before the bird and the setup. Illness sits on top: a mite treatment or a respiratory infection is not in that figure.

## Two Canaries Means Two Cages

Male canaries should not be housed together, because they fight. A buyer who wants two singers is buying two flight cages, which doubles the largest line in the setup table. A canary kept outdoors also needs a pox immunization from an avian vet, a cost an indoor bird skips.

## The Cost Decision That Matters Most: Are You Buying for the Song?

If you specifically want a singing bird, make sure you're buying a [confirmed male](/blog/bird-sexing-weight-body-condition-guide/), this is worth verifying with the seller directly, since females cost less but won't sing the way you might be expecting.

<FunFact>
Only male canaries develop full song. Females mostly produce simple chirps, so a "quiet" canary bought for its singing potential is very often a hen rather than a bird with a quiet personality, worth confirming sex before you buy if song is the whole point.
</FunFact>

This is the single most common source of buyer disappointment with this species, and it's an easy thing to get right upfront simply by asking the right question before you buy.
