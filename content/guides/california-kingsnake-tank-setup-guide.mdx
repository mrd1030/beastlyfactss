# California Kingsnake Enclosure Setup

<AffiliateDisclosure />

California kingsnake husbandry is genuinely straightforward, a big part of why this species has such a strong beginner reputation. Get the basics right and there's not much complexity beyond that. This covers the setup itself, for costs, see our [California Kingsnake cost guide](/blog/california-kingsnake-cost-guide/), or the full [California Kingsnake care guide](/guides/california-kingsnake/) for everything else.

## Enclosure Size

A 20-gallon tank works for a hatchling or young snake, but the ideal adult enclosure is a <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D1B332MY?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="120 Gallon Wooden Reptile Terrarium, 48 x 24 x 24 in">48 inches long by 24 inches wide by 24 inches tall</AffiliateLink> front-opening setup. As a general guide, enclosure length should be at least two-thirds of your snake's body length, these are active snakes that genuinely use the space they're given, not one that sits still in a corner.

## Humidity

Keep it relatively low, 40 to 60%. A well-ventilated enclosure and a water bowl that isn't overfilled both help maintain this range without extra effort.

## Substrate

<AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3TkxAVF" product="Aspen shavings substrate">Aspen shavings</AffiliateLink> are the classic, well-supported choice for this species, supporting natural burrowing behavior. Cypress mulch, sani-chips, or a commercial forest-floor product also work. Avoid pine and cedar entirely, both release aromatic oils that irritate the respiratory system, and skip sand or any chemically treated substrate.

## Lighting

No strict UVB requirement if your snake gets a natural light cycle from the room it's kept in, though low-level UVB offers some benefit and is worth adding if convenient.

<FunFact>
California kingsnakes are such accomplished escape artists that a loose lid isn't just a minor risk, it's genuinely one of the top reasons keepers lose track of this species. A tightly clamped lid matters as much as any temperature or humidity number on this page.
</FunFact>

## The Non-Obvious Requirement: Security, on Two Fronts

California kingsnakes are genuinely accomplished escape artists, a tightly clamped, secure lid isn't optional, it's essential. The second, equally important security requirement isn't about the lid at all: never house a kingsnake with or near another snake. This species retains a real cannibalistic drive, covered in detail in our [California Kingsnake handling guide](/blog/california-kingsnake-handling-guide/), and it belongs in the setup conversation too, since it determines how you plan your entire reptile room, not just this one enclosure, whether that room also holds a milk snake, a boa constrictor, or nothing else at all.
