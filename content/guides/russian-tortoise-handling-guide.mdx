# How to Handle a Russian Tortoise

Russian tortoises are hardy and generally tolerant, but like most tortoises, this isn't really a species built for regular handling, it's better understood as an observation pet with occasional, careful interaction.

## What to Expect

Active and curious for a tortoise, genuinely talented at both climbing and burrowing, which means they're better escape artists than their slow reputation suggests. Over-handling causes real stress, so keep physical interaction purposeful rather than frequent.

## Males and Territorial Behavior

Male Russian tortoises are genuinely territorial and will ram and bully other tortoises, including other males and sometimes even females. House males separately from each other, this isn't a species where multiple males can share space peacefully.

<FunFact>
Despite their slow, docile reputation, Russian tortoises are genuinely talented climbers and diggers, capable of escaping enclosures that look perfectly secure at first glance.
</FunFact>

## First-Timer Mistakes

- Housing in a glass aquarium instead of an open-topped table. Glass provides poor ventilation and tends to stress tortoises, who can see through the walls but not reach what's beyond them, leading to restless pacing along the glass.
- Over-humidifying the enclosure. This is an arid-adapted species, and excess moisture causes more problems than it solves.
- Housing multiple males together, expecting them to coexist peacefully.

## How This Compares to the Sulcata

The sulcata, already covered on this site, is a genuine giant that must have outdoor space as it grows and simply cannot be kept indoors long-term, see our [Sulcata Tortoise handling guide](/blog/sulcata-tortoise-handling-guide/) for what that looks like in practice. The Russian tortoise stays small and can be maintained indoors for its entire multi-decade life, making it a far more realistic choice for apartment or indoor-only living. If you want the tortoise-keeping experience without the eventual outdoor-space requirement, this is genuinely the better-suited species.
