# How to Handle a Red-Eared Slider

They learn the feeding routine and swim to the glass. That is the meal. It is not a request to be held.

## What to expect

Most sliders want to be left alone. They bite if threatened, startled, or if a finger looks like food. The beak draws blood. Claws scratch. Slow and sure beats fast.

<FunFact>
Swimming to the glass when you walk in is recognition of the schedule. It is still about the food.
</FunFact>

## Pickup

Scoop from underneath. Support the whole body. Never grab from above. Both hands the entire move. A drop is a serious injury.

## First-timer mistakes

- Handling too often or too long
- Hand-feeding, which teaches begging and raises bite risk
- Skipping the wash, before and after. The [Salmonella hygiene guide](/blog/reptile-salmonella-hygiene-guide/) is the sink rule
- Expecting a dog
- Two turtles in a tank that only fits one dock

Captive-bred animals are usually less fearful than wild-caught. Buy that way if you have the choice.

This is a look-and-feed pet. The [encyclopedia](/encyclopedia/animal/red-eared-slider/) is the species page.
