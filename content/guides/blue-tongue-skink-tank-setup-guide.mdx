# Blue-Tongued Skink Tank Setup: Northern vs. Indonesian, the Difference That Actually Matters

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Same footprint. Same UVB. Different heat and humidity.

<Figure src="/assets/images/blue-tongue-skink-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of a blue-tongued skink enclosure showing UVB lighting, basking temp, humidity, and substrate for Northern versus Indonesian types" caption="Temperature and humidity split the two types." shareable />

## Enclosure size

4x2x2 feet adult minimum. Front-opening <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4f8WVcx" product="4x2x2 ft PVC enclosure">PVC</AffiliateLink> holds humidity better than glass.

## Temperature

Northern basking 105 to 115F. Indonesian 100 to 105F. Cool side 70 to 80F. Nights above about 70F. Halogen or a deep heat projector in a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44eEEFH" product="Basking dome/fixture">dome</AffiliateLink> on a [thermostat](/blog/reptile-heating-thermostats-guide/).

## Humidity

Northern about 40%. Indonesian 60 to 80%. Moisture-retentive substrate, misting, and a large water bowl get an Indonesian there. A dry Northern setup will dry an Indonesian out.

<FunFact>
Two skinks that look the same can need humidity twice as far apart.
</FunFact>

## Substrate

4 to 6 inches. Northern about 60% topsoil to 40% play sand. Indonesian mix adds leaf litter, sphagnum, or <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CY23K32S?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="VCEPJH Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate, 2 Pack">coco fiber</AffiliateLink>. No loose substrate on a sick animal.

## Lighting

<AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3TiqeC4" product="UVB - Arcadia Forest 6% (ProT5 kit, 36in/39W)">T5 HO</AffiliateLink> 5 to 12%, at least half the warm side, no closer than 10 inches. Replace every 12 months. Photoperiod 11 to 13 hours.

## Quarantine

Indonesian or unknown origin: quarantine and a fecal first. The [quarantine guide](/blog/reptile-quarantine-guide/) is that step.

One solid <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3SKmfOx" product="Cork bark round hide">hide</AffiliateLink> and a soak dish they can submerge in.

The [UVB guide](/blog/uvb-lighting-complete-guide/) is glass and replacement.
