# Cockatoo Cage Setup: Size, Material, and Daily Enrichment That Actually Matters

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Size, material, and daily destruction. Miss any of the three and the bird pays for it.

<Figure src="/assets/images/cockatoo-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of a cockatoo cage showing stainless steel construction, a rooftop play stand, and massive daily enrichment" caption="Size, material, and daily enrichment. All three." shareable />

## Cage size

36 by 24 by 48 inches is the minimum. 48 by 48 by 60 is the comfortable size. A top that opens onto a play stand gives the bird more usable space during the day.

## Bar spacing and security

Three-quarters of an inch to 1 inch for large cockatoos. Locks they cannot pick. They will work a standard latch open.

## Material

Stainless steel. No galvanized or zinc-coated cages. Chewing that coating is metal toxicity.

## Lighting

Full-spectrum UV for an indoor bird. 10 to 12 hours of dark and quiet every night. Short sleep makes screaming worse.

Out-of-cage time every day.
