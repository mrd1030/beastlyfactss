# How Much Does Angelfish Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Angelfish are cheap to buy and genuinely expensive to house properly, the tank, not the fish, is where the real budget goes with this species. See our full [Angelfish care guide](/guides/angelfish/) for everything else.

## The Fish: $3 to $50

Common freshwater angelfish run $3 to $20 for standard varieties. Fancy morphs, koi, platinum, marble, zebra, and veil-finned varieties among them, cost more, and rare or extra-large specimens can push past $100 on the secondary market.

<FunFact>
Typing "angelfish" into a shopping search can turn up two completely different animals. Freshwater angelfish, the ones covered here, run $3 to $50 for most varieties. Marine angelfish are a different, far pricier group entirely, commonly $150 to $1,500, and need a saltwater reef setup rather than a freshwater community tank. Worth double-checking which one is actually in the listing before you buy.
</FunFact>

## Vet Costs

Aquatic vet care is uncommon and specialized. Most keepers manage illness themselves with water quality correction and over-the-counter medications like <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01M5LEDRA?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Seachem MetroPlex Metronidazole">metronidazole (sold as Fish Zole or MetroPlex)</AffiliateLink> or standard ich treatments rather than professional visits. Budget for medication on hand rather than office visits, and keeping a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4p1T7yd" product="Water test kit">water test kit</AffiliateLink> on hand to catch a water quality problem early goes a long way toward avoiding needing medication at all. Our [Angelfish health issues guide](/blog/angelfish-health-issues-guide/) covers the specific conditions this species tends to run into.

## Lifespan

8 to 12 years is typical with good care, up to around 15 years under genuinely ideal conditions. This is a longer-lived fish than many community species, worth factoring into your tank planning since you'll likely be maintaining this setup for the better part of a decade or more. Compare that to a species like the guppy, which typically lives only 2 to 3 years, covered in our [Guppy cost guide](/blog/guppy-cost-guide/), and it's clear these two popular community fish sit at very different points on the commitment scale.

## The Real Cost Lesson With This Species

The fish itself is one of the cheapest animals covered on this site. What actually determines your total cost is tank height and quality filtration, both genuinely non-negotiable for a fish with this body shape and this waste output, and both covered in more detail in our [Angelfish tank setup guide](/blog/angelfish-tank-setup-guide/).
