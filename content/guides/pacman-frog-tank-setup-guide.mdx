# Pacman Frog Tank Setup: Small Footprint, One Big Rule About Substrate

<AffiliateDisclosure />

They sit. The mistakes that kill them are the floor and the heat mat under it.

<Figure src="/assets/images/pacman-frog-tank-setup-guide-1.jpg" alt="Labeled diagram of a Pacman frog enclosure showing deep coco fiber substrate, a side-mounted heat mat, and daily misting" caption="Deep coco fiber. Heat on the side. One frog." shareable />

## Enclosure size

A <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09MQBB6CP?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="Reptile Growth Reptile Terrarium, 10 Gallon Tank 20x10x11 for Invertebrates">10 to 20 gallon terrarium</AffiliateLink>. They spend almost all of their time buried. One frog per enclosure.

## Temperature

Days 75 to 85F, a slightly warmer spot near 83F, nights 65 to 75F. Heat with a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44hAxJ1" product="Under-tank heat mat + thermostat kit">mat on the side of the tank, on a thermostat</AffiliateLink>, or a low-wattage overhead bulb. Never under the tank.

## Humidity

60 to 80%. Wet substrate and a daily mist. Let it fall toward the low end between mists. A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3QHrM81" product="Digital thermometer/hygrometer combo">digital hygrometer</AffiliateLink> beats guessing.

## Substrate

3 to 4 inches of <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CY23K32S?tag=beastlyfacts-20" product="VCEPJH Loose Coconut Fiber Substrate">coco fiber or coco husk</AffiliateLink>, damp enough to burrow. A light layer of sphagnum or leaf litter on top is fine.

No gravel, rocks, bark chips, or charcoal. They strike blind and swallow it. Change the substrate every 2 to 3 months unless the tank is bioactive.

<FunFact>
The bite is the other half of an animal that spends the day under a few inches of fiber.
</FunFact>

## Lighting

A basic LED, 12 hours on, 12 off. UVB is optional.

## Furnishings

A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44hAzR9" product="Small/shallow water dish">shallow water dish</AffiliateLink> and a hide or leaf litter. They do not use a climbing wall.
