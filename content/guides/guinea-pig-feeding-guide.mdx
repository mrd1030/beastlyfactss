# Guinea Pig Feeding: Schedule, Diet, and Why They Stop Eating

<AffiliateDisclosure />

A guinea pig's gut depends on near-constant food. Skipping a meal is a bigger deal here than it is for most pets.

## Feeding schedule

Unlimited grass hay, <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4h9WW2u" product="Oxbow Western Timothy Hay, 90 oz">timothy, orchard, or oat/meadow</AffiliateLink>, at every life stage. That is 70 to 80% of the diet. A measured daily portion of guinea-pig-specific fortified pellets in the morning. A small handful of fresh vegetables daily. Pull uneaten veg after 30 minutes to a couple of hours. Fruit stays under about 10% of the daily diet.

Babies nibble hay, pellets, and vegetables within hours of birth and wean by 3 to 5 weeks. Juveniles under about 6 months get alfalfa alongside or instead of grass hay. Healthy adults stay on grass hay. Pregnant, nursing, senior, or ill animals can use alfalfa and need roughly 2 to 3 times a healthy adult's vitamin C.

## What guinea pigs eat

Grass hay first. Long-strand fiber and the chew that wears the teeth. Then a daily measured portion of guinea-pig pellets, not rabbit pellets. Stabilized vitamin C on the label. Use the bag within about 90 days of manufacture.

Daily vegetables, rotated: bell pepper, ripe tomato, romaine, broccoli, cauliflower, carrot, spinach, kale, parsley, cilantro, cucumber. A daily vitamin C source is mandatory: fortified pellets, those vegetables, or <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/44CDAMd" product="Oxbow Natural Science Vitamin C supplement">a direct supplement</AffiliateLink>. Never in the water bottle.

Fruit is an occasional treat: small amounts of orange, kiwi, apple, or pear. Fresh water always.

## Safe treats and foods to avoid

Safe: the vegetables above, plus dandelion greens and celery, and small bits of orange, kiwi, apple, or pear.

Toxic: avocado, chocolate, onions, garlic, chives, leeks, rhubarb, raw potato, unripe tomato and their leaves, mushrooms, fruit pits and seeds. Skip nuts, seeds, peanut butter, corn, bread, and pasta. No meat, eggs, or dairy.

## Why guinea pigs stop eating

1. **[Dental disease](/blog/guinea-pig-health-issues-guide/).** Overgrown molars. Often invisible without a vet exam.
2. **GI stasis.** Gut slows or stops from low fiber, dehydration, pain, or stress. Cause and effect of not eating. An emergency.
3. **Illness or pain elsewhere.** Respiratory infection, stones, arthritis, injury.
4. **Environmental stress.** A new home, new cage mates, noise. A bonded companion helps.
5. **A new food.** They lock in preferences early. Change hay or pellets slowly.
6. **Pregnancy toxemia.** Overweight or late-term sows.
7. **Vitamin C deficiency.** Appetite drops within 2 to 3 weeks of a deficient diet.

## When to worry

Call a vet at 8 to 12 hours with no eating and no droppings. GI stasis can become life-threatening inside 24 to 48 hours. With prompt treatment, many start improving inside 24 to 72 hours.

Same-day signs: fewer or smaller or dry droppings, lethargy, a hunched posture, teeth grinding, weight loss, a bloated or firm abdomen, less water, labored breathing. Any real drop in eating is a call, not a wait.
