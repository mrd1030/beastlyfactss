# Guppies Aren't Handled, But They Do Need a Population Plan

Watch them. The problem is fry, not pickup technique.

## What they are

Peaceful, active, social. Groups, never one. Males smaller and colored, about 1.5 inches. Females larger and plainer, up to about 2.4 inches.

## How they breed

Livebearers. Free-swimming young. No eggs to tend.

<FunFact>
A female can store sperm 5 to 10 months and drop up to about 8 broods from one mating. Cover in the tank raises fry survival. Mixed-sex tanks grow.
</FunFact>

## Population

Separate sexes if you want zero fry. Males only for color without babies. If you breed, one male to two or three females. Rehome fry or skip dense plants and let predation hold the numbers.

## First-timer mistakes

- Mixed sex, no plan
- [An uncycled tank](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/)
- Too many fish too fast

Move a fish with a net. Hands are not part of care. No bettas. No goldfish.
