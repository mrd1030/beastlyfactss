# How Much Does a Leopard Gecko Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Cheap for a reptile. Still a real setup.

## The gecko itself

Pet store standard: $20 to $45. Breeder basic morph: $50 to $150. Popular morphs: $75 to $300. Rare designer: $500 to $3,500 or more. Online shipping: $35 to $100.

## Upfront setup: $175 to $495 on this site's gear list

The gecko is not in this table. The first vet exam is not in this table.

A first vet exam and fecal test run $150 to $215 on top of the table.

Those rows add to $20 to $82 a month. The old $20 to $50 line only held if electricity and UVB sat at the floor. Bulk feeders or a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4uGamHo" product="Dubia Roach Colony Starter Kit (1 lb)">dubia colony</AffiliateLink> cut the feeder line.

A small dubia colony that you actually keep producing is the cleanest way to cut the monthly number. Buying a kit and letting it stall is not.

## Vet costs

Annual wellness $85 to $105. [Sick or emergency visits $100 to $800 or more](/blog/leopard-gecko-health-issues-guide/). Diagnostics and surgery climb from there.

## The bottom line

Most of the money is the first enclosure and the thermostat. Do not cheap those. Monthly cost is feeders.

If you are comparing beginner reptiles, [Five Reptiles That Actually Make Sense for Beginners](/blog/five-beginner-reptiles-overview/) puts this gecko next to the bearded dragon, ball python, crested gecko, and corn snake.
