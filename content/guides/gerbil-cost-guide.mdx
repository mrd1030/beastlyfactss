# How Much Does Gerbil Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Gerbils are one of the more affordable small pets, with one important caveat: like the degu, you're budgeting for two animals, not one.

## The Gerbil: $5 to $50 Each

Most gerbils run $10 to $20 at a typical pet store, with adoption often landing at $10 or less, sometimes including a starter cage. Since this species must be kept in same-sex pairs or small groups, plan for at least two from the outset.

## Vet Costs

You'll want an exotics-experienced vet. Scent-gland tumor removal is the most common surgical cost in this species, and because gerbils are small, health problems can progress quickly, prompt vet visits matter more than they might for a larger animal.

## Lifespan

2 to 3 years is the normal lifespan for this species.

<FunFact>
Shorter than a degu, but still a genuine multi-year commitment worth planning for rather than treating as disposable. A gerbil bought as a "starter pet" for a child is still, on average, a two to three year relationship.
</FunFact>

## The Honest Budgeting Takeaway

Gerbils are inexpensive individually and inexpensive to maintain, the adjustment for first-timers is the tank itself. A proper glass enclosure with deep digging substrate costs more upfront than the wire cages often marketed for small rodents, but it's the right choice for how this species lives. Guinea pigs trade that vertical digging depth for floor space instead, and our [Gerbil vs. Guinea Pig guide](/blog/gerbil-vs-guinea-pig-guide/) lays out which space requirement fits a given room better. If you're weighing a gerbil against another social, day-active small rodent, our [Degu cost guide](/blog/degu-cost-guide/) covers the closest comparison, and [Two Pets Where You're Really Buying a Pair](/blog/degu-gerbil-overview/) walks through both side by side.
