# How Much Does Gerbil Ownership Really Cost?

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Gerbils are genuinely one of the more affordable small pets on this site, with one important caveat: like the degu also covered here, you're really budgeting for two animals, not one. For the full care picture beyond budgeting, see our complete [Gerbil care guide](/guides/gerbil/), or our companion guides on [handling](/blog/gerbil-handling-guide/), [health issues](/blog/gerbil-health-issues-guide/), and [enclosure setup](/blog/gerbil-tank-setup-guide/).

## The Gerbil: $5 to $50 Each

Most gerbils run $10 to $20 at a typical pet store, with adoption often landing at $10 or less, sometimes including a starter cage. Since this species must be kept in same-sex pairs or small groups, covered in the [handling guide](/blog/gerbil-handling-guide/), plan for at least two from the outset.

## Vet Costs

You'll want an exotics-experienced vet. Scent-gland tumor removal is the most common surgical cost in this species, covered in our [Gerbil health issues guide](/blog/gerbil-health-issues-guide/), and because gerbils are small, health problems can progress quickly, prompt vet visits matter more than they might for a larger animal.

## Lifespan

2 to 5 years, commonly landing around 3 to 4.

<FunFact>
Shorter than the degu also covered on this site, but still a genuine multi-year commitment worth planning for rather than treating as disposable. A gerbil bought as a "starter pet" for a child is still, on average, a three to four year relationship.
</FunFact>

## The Honest Budgeting Takeaway

Gerbils are inexpensive individually and inexpensive to maintain, the real adjustment for first-timers is the tank itself. A proper glass enclosure with deep digging substrate costs more upfront than the wire cages often marketed for small rodents, but it's genuinely the right choice for how this species actually lives, covered in full in the [tank setup guide](/blog/gerbil-tank-setup-guide/). If you're weighing a gerbil against another social, day-active small rodent, our [Degu cost guide](/blog/degu-cost-guide/) covers the closest comparison on this site, and [Two Pets Where You're Really Buying a Pair](/blog/degu-gerbil-overview/) walks through both side by side.
