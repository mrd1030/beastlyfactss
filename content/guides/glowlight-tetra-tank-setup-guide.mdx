# Glowlight Tetra Tank Setup: A 24 x 15 Inch Base, Acidic Water, and Planted Cover

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Glowlight tetras come from forested tributaries of the Essequibo River in Guyana, where the water is stained dark brown by tannins and often strongly acidic. An adult reaches about 1.6 inches (4 cm), and a tank for a school of 10 is built around floor space, soft acidic water and dense plants.

## Tank Size

Buy for the base. The tank for a school of 10 has a floor of at least 24 x 15 inches (60 x 38 cm), which works out to roughly 18 to 20 gallons. <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eSbKl2" product="20 gallon long tank">A 20-gallon long</AffiliateLink> covers the same floor area as that 24 x 15 base, so it fits the school too.

A 10-gallon tank is sometimes sold as enough for these fish, and it is the size for a group of six. Six is the least that works, and 10 is the school to buy. A school of that size spends its day in the middle of the tank, and the larger base leaves that water open instead of crowding the fish against the glass and the plants.

Keep the tank calm. Peaceful companions of similar size suit it, and anything big enough to swallow a 1.6-inch fish does not. The [stocking and tankmates guide](/blog/aquarium-stocking-and-tankmates-guide/) lists the checks two species pass before sharing a tank.

## Temperature

75 to 82°F (24 to 28°C) is the range, held by <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/4eQY1L5" product="Submersible aquarium heater">a submersible heater</AffiliateLink>. Set the heater inside the band, since 82°F is the top of it and nothing is gained by running closer.

That is the same band as an [ember tetra tank](/blog/ember-tetra-tank-setup-guide/). A [neon tetra tank](/blog/neon-tetra-tank-setup-guide/) runs cooler, usually at 72 to 78°F, so the two share only the middle of each range.

## Water Chemistry

The target is a pH of 5.5 to 7.5 and a general hardness of 2 to 15 dGH. The fish color up best at the acidic end of the pH range, so a tank that lands near the middle or below is the better outcome than one pushed toward 7.5.

Test the tap water before the tank is filled. Water outside those numbers moves by [adjusting GH and KH together](/blog/freshwater-ph-gh-kh-guide/), which carries the pH with it; a pH-down product on its own drags the number briefly and then lets it drift back. Some keepers add a small bag of aquarium-safe peat to the filter to bring tannins into the water, a nod to the stained streams the species comes from. Every fill and water change is treated with a <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3Rg5gTT" product="Water conditioner">water conditioner</AffiliateLink> that removes chlorine and chloramine.

## Filtration

A <AffiliateLink href="https://amzn.to/3T8xyAd" product="Sponge filter">sponge filter</AffiliateLink> sized for the tank is the simple choice. It does its work as a home for the bacteria that process waste, and its output leaves the open water in the middle of the tank calm. A hang-on-back works as well. [How much of a filter's job is biological](/blog/aquarium-filtration-guide/) is what to check when choosing a size.

## Plants, Sand and Open Water

Plant the tank with a wide variety of plants, and favor fine-leaved species at several heights so the school has cover at every level. Leave a clear space in the center of the tank, because that open water is where the school swims. Floating plants add surface cover on top, and fine sand makes a good floor.

<FunFact>
The 1917 redescription of the glowlight tetra describes its stripe as a broad band without chromatophores, cherry red in life. The species name agrees: erythrozonus means red-banded.
</FunFact>

## Cycle the Tank Before the School Goes In

The tank is [cycled before any fish are added](/blog/aquarium-cycling-guide/): ammonia and nitrite both read zero, and nitrate has started to build. The whole school can go in together once the readings hold steady, with the water tested daily until they settle.

New fish spend 30 days in [a bare quarantine tank](/blog/fish-quarantine-and-treatment-guide/) before they join a display tank that already holds fish.

The [glowlight tetra encyclopedia profile](/encyclopedia/animal/glowlight-tetra/) has more on the species itself.
