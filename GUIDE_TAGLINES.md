# Guide Taglines

Every care guide tagline, in `allGuides` order (amphibians, birds, cats, dogs,
fish, geckos, invertebrates, lizards, small mammals, snakes, turtles). The
tagline is the italic line under the animal name on a guide hub. Source:
`src/lib/data/guides/*.js`, field `tagline`.

A tagline is a greeting, not a citation. It should be witty, warm, and true on
its face. If a line needs research to defend, it belongs in the funFact or the
deep dive, not here. No hard numbers, no superlatives, no rankings, no dashes.
