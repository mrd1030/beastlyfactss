# Price every item: one species, site and book together

Owner rules (firm):
- Every item a keeper buys gets a price estimate. Ranges round OUTWARD to $5: low end down, high end up ($12 to $16 becomes $10 to $20; $8 to $12 becomes $5 to $15). Never $0 and never "$0 to $5": an item under $5 reads "about $5". A single-figure estimate rounds to the nearest $5 and keeps "about".
- Totals are the exact sum of the rounded rows (lows summed, highs summed). Every total the cost guide states (H2, seoTitle, seoDescription, description, FAQ, body lines, closing) matches.
- Every item row in the cost guide tables carries an <AffiliateLink> to the exact catalog product in src\lib\data\affiliateProducts.js (copy link and product name from the catalog; never from memory) when a fitting product exists (right species and size). Food and consumables may stay unlinked if no product fits. One link per product per article (check-affiliate-mdx). Any item with no fitting product is listed in your report as "No gear available: <item>". STRICT MATCH, owner's rule: a link must be the actual item the row describes, at the right spec (size, wattage, gallons, species). No "close enough" (a different wattage, a bigger tank, a reptile product for a bird). If you are unsure a product fits, do not link it: list it as "Unsure: <item> / <product> / why". Prefer the cheapest catalog product that genuinely fits when a row names no brand.
- The hub (src\lib\data\guides\<group>.js) stays LINKS ONLY: its buyList shows no prices. Make each buyList item link (its text must exactly match a product's `covers` string; reword the item to an existing covers string, or add the item's exact text to the fitting product's `covers` array). Update the hub's Budget row and the cost guide route line to the new totals. Cross-check product by product: every product linked in a cost guide gear row must also appear as its own item on the hub buyList, and every hub buyList product must be in a cost row (food, electricity and vet services are cost rows only).
- The care package's budget page and profile cost lines carry the same rounded estimates and totals as the cost guide.

## Where prices come from (in this order; never invent, never Amazon)
1. The price already in the cost guide row, rounded outward.
2. The catalog `price` field of the linked product, rounded outward.
3. A retailer check (PetSmart first, then Petco or Chewy) with WebFetch or WebSearch; record the URL and the price seen in your report.
If none works, leave the item unpriced and report it; do not guess.
OWNER RULE: a row's range must always cover the catalog price of every product it links (raise the high end, rounded outward, when the linked product costs more; for a row linking several products, the high end covers their combined price). Supplements: the first supply (each linked supplement product) is a SETUP row covering the products' combined price; the monthly row is unlinked "Supplement top-ups" at the ongoing amount (the bearded dragon and leopard gecko do this). If a budget page overflows, split it into a setup page and a monthly page rather than cutting. Gear that has to fit the enclosure (UVB fixtures and tubes, heat mats, the enclosure or tank itself, any sized lighting) gets a short sizing note in its row, e.g. "check the length against your enclosure" or "check the dimensions before you buy".

## Steps
1. Build ONE master item list for the species: union of the cost guide table rows, the hub buyList, the book's budget page lines, and every item the book's setup checklist and shopping list name (a thing to buy, not a task); merge duplicates (same thing, different words). Decide for each: setup or ongoing, and which table it belongs in.
2. Price, round, link each item per the rules.
3. Rewrite the cost guide tables (keep existing table structure and headings; add rows for items that were missing). Do not add a "Prices last checked" or "as of" line (owner, 2026-10-06). Set lastUpdated "2026-10-03".
4. Update the hub (links, Budget row, route line, firstWeek rows, quick facts, FAQs, and the hub's seoTitle/seoDescription/description wherever they state a price or total). Every metadata field that states a cost changes too: the cost guide's title, seoTitle, seoDescription, description, excerpt, FAQ answers and image alt text. Grep content\guides and src\lib\data for the old totals and update any other guide quoting them. List every changed location in the report.
5. Update the book's budget page and profile cost lines to match; if the book is a released edition, bump the minor edition (4.0 to 4.1, 3.0 to 3.1) in every place the build sets it (build.py EDITION, README render command) and add a version history entry: "N.N, Oct 2026. Costs: every item priced and rounded to the nearest $5; setup $X to $Y, <ongoing> $A to $B. Other changes are wording only." Unreleased editions (the owner will say which) keep their number and fold the new totals into the existing Costs line.
6. Checks: node scripts/sync-articles.js; check-internal-links, check-related-articles, check-voice --strict, check-seo-tags, check-affiliate-mdx, check-cost-coverage; the three hub checks with the Windows workaround (copy scripts\<name>.mjs to scripts\_t.mjs, replace `await import(path.resolve('src/lib/data/guides', f))` with `await import(require_url(path.resolve('src/lib/data/guides', f)))`, prepend `import { pathToFileURL as require_url } from 'url';`, run, delete). Then restore public\articles.json and src\lib\generated\* (git checkout of those paths only).
7. Book: python build.py in <id>-src; from content\CAREPACKAGE Guides\source with env CHROME_BIN="C:/Program Files/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe": node _render.mjs <id> "<Name>" <edition> --measure (every page at least 15px free; add a page rather than cut), then without --measure.

House rules: docs\RULES.md; no em or en dashes; US spelling; no outside promotion in prose (product names live in links and the catalog, not in sentences). No commits or pushes; git only for the restore in step 6.

## Report (under 400 words)
The master list as a short table: item | old price (cost guide / book) | new rounded price | price source | linked product or "No gear available". Then old and new totals, the edition change, checks, measure line.
