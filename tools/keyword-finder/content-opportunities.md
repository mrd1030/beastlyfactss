## 5) Surprising / Unusual — Worth Flagging on Its Own

- **Fire-Bellied Toad "near me" spike (15 hits) is the single highest buying-intent suggestion in the entire dataset** — unusual for a small, cheap, obscure amphibian. Worth a closer look; may reflect a specific regional breeder/availability trend. At minimum, add a where-to-buy/sourcing section to the existing guide.
- **Niche/exotic species are punching far above their weight class.** Giant African Millipede, Ackie Monitor, Leaf-Tailed Gecko, and Argentine Tegu all show concentrated, high-value buying and safety signal despite being far more obscure than mainstream reptiles — this is the clearest actionable pattern in the whole dataset.
- **Conversely, several of the most popular pet species are "quiet" once isolated** — Bearded Dragon, Leopard Gecko, Crested Gecko, Ball Python, Boa Constrictor, and Axolotl each top out at only 2 hits on their best individual query. Likely means the site already covers their basics well; don't chase new content here based on this dataset.
- **Swiss guinea pig law** — a real, widely-cited animal-welfare rule (guinea pigs must be kept in pairs) surfaces from `is it legal to own one guinea pig in switzerland` queries. Small but fun single-fact hook, low effort.
- **Milk Snake is the only species in the dataset where "where to buy" phrasing itself reaches the 2-hit qualifying bar** — if writing "where to buy" style copy anywhere, this is the most literal match.
- **Petco-specific pricing queries** for Russian Tortoise (`russian tortoise cost petco`) and Savannah Monitor (`savannah monitor price at petco`) — possible retailer-affiliate angle if the site has or wants a Petco relationship.
- **Hermit Crab** — `how much does it cost to own a hermit crab` (2 hits) is phrased as total-cost-of-ownership, not purchase price; frame accordingly.
- **Data-quality flags — do not chase these, they're keyword-tool noise, not pet-content demand:**
  - **Persian** — top "buying" and "comparison" signal is almost entirely Persian food/culture/rug collisions (restaurants, bakeries, ice cream, rugs), not the cat breed. Real cat-buying queries exist but never clear 2 hits.
  - **Canary** → top hit is "canary islands" (travel destination, not the bird).
  - **Oscar** (fish) → top hits are "oscar isaac" (actor) and "oscar the grouch" — no usable fish signal.
  - **Goldfish** → top hit "goldfish plant" is almost certainly the houseplant, not the fish.
  - **Chinchilla** → top raw hit "chinchilla coat" (3) is likely the fur/fashion sense; use "chinchilla cage"/"chinchilla cost" instead (also 3 hits, genuinely pet-relevant).
  - **Cat/Dog generic seeds** → dominated by local-business noise (cat cafes, vet/groomer "near me") and substring collisions (Caterpillar stock price, catalytic converter cost, cataract surgery) — no real content signal.
  - **Bengal, Oscar, Koi** in the comparisons lens collide with the Cincinnati Bengals NFL team, the Academy Awards, and fintech/anime terms respectively — exclude these "vs" rows entirely from comparison-content planning.
