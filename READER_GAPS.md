# Reader gaps by guide type

Built 2026-09-24 from docs/READER_REVIEWS.md, docs/READER_LOG.md and SHORT_ARTICLES.md. 83 species were reader-tested (2026-09-08 to 2026-09-16). Each gap below uses the latest pass and fix note for that species, and the ones marked "verified" were checked by grepping the live article in content/guides.

Four things change the picture since the tests ran:

- **Hubs.** Almost every hub was rebuilt as a router, so the most common complaint (hub numbers fighting the deep dives) is closed across the board. It is left out of the tables below.
- **New feeding guides.** 25 feeding guides were written after their species was tested, so no reader has seen them. They are listed under Feeding.
- **New legal guides.** 7 legal guides were written after their species was tested and are also unread. They are listed under Legal.
- **New shared guides (2026-09-16).** Six shared guides now answer several of the most common "not covered anywhere" items: aquarium-water-changes, aquarium-stocking-and-tankmates, aquarium-ich-treatment, shrimp-molting, tortoise-soaking, and invertebrate-quarantine-cleaning-and-escapes. They are wired into the matching species' sidebars. Where the species page itself is still silent, the tables say "shared guide covers it".

Grades are the reader's latest grade for that page. "n/g" means the page was never graded one by one: cockatoo, lovebird and tarantula got single-pass summaries, and the raw output for green anole, leaf-tailed gecko and mouse was lost. "untested" means no reader has seen the page.

## Handling

### What readers mainly said

1. **Thin on actual technique** (~15 species): no pick-up method, no session length, no first week. "No actual technique: no pick-up method, no session length, no settling-in week." (milk snake)
2. **Padded with another species or repeated material** (~8): fire skink, rosy boa, jumping spider, veiled chameleon, oscar, corydoras, tiger salamander. "Mostly a blue-tongue comparison with little fire skink method." (fire skink)
3. **Best page in the set when it gives timings and stop signals** (~20 rated A- or A): bearded dragon, leopard gecko, blue-tongue skink, budgie, rat, uromastyx. "Wait 7 to 14 days, scoop from below, the stop-signal list." (bearded dragon)
4. **Handling policy contradicts another page** (~7): tarantula hub (fixed), tiger salamander, hognose, stick insect, mourning gecko, swordtail (fixed). "a hub that says ten minutes of bare-hand contact and an article that says gloves-only cannot both be house policy." (tiger salamander)
5. **Handling guide doing another job** (2): quaker (legal plus temperament) and corydoras (a duplicate of feeding). "nothing in it teaches handling." (quaker)

## Cost

### What readers mainly said

1. **The page disagreed with itself** (~20 species): headline against its own table, or FAQ against body. Almost all fixed. "Captive-bred individuals cost more but make genuinely better pets" then "A wild-caught animal costs about the same as a captive-bred one" (fire skink, now fixed). Conure is still open.
2. **Leaves out items that other pages call required** (~12): thermostat, hygrometer, scale, UV fixture, quarantine tank, test kit. "a reader buying from the list ends up unable to measure the species' headline requirement." (fat-tail, hygrometer)
3. **No vet or emergency figure** (~7): axolotl, sulcata, rabbit RHDV2, cockatoo PBFD screening, hedgehog spay, rat and guinea pig neuter. "what a vet visit actually costs (the cost guide only prices the animal, the tank, and food)" (axolotl)
4. **Prices a box that is not the box you need** (3): tegu, savannah monitor, green iguana, all honest about it. "the warning that the priced enclosure is not the enclosure I need" (tegu)
5. **Praised when the arithmetic can be checked** (~15 rated A- or A). "Real arithmetic I can check" (box turtle). "The page I would screenshot" (green iguana)
6. **Linked another species' cost page instead of its own sibling** (~5, fixed). "It links a hedgehog cost page instead." (sugar glider)

## Health issues

1. **Symptom-to-action pages with a vet-now list are the most trusted pages** (~30 rated A- or A). "Symptom-to-action for eleven conditions plus a vet-now list." (bearded dragon)
2. **Thin, with no numbers** (~12): tiger salamander, stick insect, savannah monitor, tegu, fire skink, kingsnake, jumping spider, blue-tongue skink, green iguana. "it is 900 words of prevention with no numbers in it." (stick insect)
3. **Names the treatment but stops before the number** (~10): ich temperature, metronidazole dose, euthanasia method, quarantine length, mite procedure. "ich section stops short of a number" (bristlenose)
4. **No Sources block on a medical page** (4): box turtle, canary, neon (now fixed), leaf-tailed gecko (now fixed). "No sources at all, which for a medical page is a problem." (box turtle)

## Hub and encyclopedia (brief)

- **Hub.** Before the router rebuild, the hub was the main source of contradictions, graded C to D+ ("The hub reads like it was written from a different sourceset than the deep dives and never reconciled"). After the rebuild, readers graded hubs A- to A (rabbit, ball python, box turtle). The rebuild also removed hub-only content that no deep dive carried, which opened some gaps: the diet sections that led to new feeding guides, fish water-change lines that led to the shared guide, blue-tongue skink adult weight, and hedgehog bathing and spay.
- **Encyclopedia.** Readers mostly scored it "nothing to act on" (C to B). Recurring defects were a captive figure sitting in the wildLifespan field, a conservation field contradicting the entry's own history, and adult sizes out of line with the deep dives. Most were fixed field by field. The overview prose was left as is in several places: green iguana, rosy boa, cardinal wild lifespan.
