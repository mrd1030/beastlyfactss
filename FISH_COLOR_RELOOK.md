# Fish color batch: relook list

Owner, 2026-11-04: the fish color pass (color passages and FAQs added to every
fish feeding guide, plus the new fish color guide) is checked guide by guide
as it lands, and anything that needs another edit goes here. Nothing on this
list is being fixed now; go over it together after the batch is done, then
one edit pass. Delete an item once it is fixed or dismissed.

Source counts include the new color source. Links are in-body internal links
(the color pass added none, as asked).

## Across the batch

- **Research narration in FAQs.** Several new FAQs talk about the study
  instead of stating the fact: "The one angelfish trial ran for four weeks",
  "The trial that measured it ran for 10 weeks" (discus). RULES.md: give the
  number, not the provenance. Reword to "Expect weeks: in trials the change
  showed within about four weeks."
- **Old hobby sources still in the Sources blocks.** Not added by this pass,
  but they break the no hobby sites rule (owner, 2026-11-01): Aquarium Co-Op
  on angelfish, betta, bristlenose, corydoras, discus, ember, oscar and zebra
  danio feeding; Fishkeeping World and Aqua-Fish.net on angelfish. Handle
  under the fish guides regrade (research/FISH_GUIDES_REGRADE_2026-11-01.md),
  keeping any that the 3 source floor needs.

  angelfish trial" is narration (see above).

  astaxanthin alga looked more intensely colored, and growth did not change".
