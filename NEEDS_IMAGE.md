## 2026-08-17 run: 5 facts drafted, awaiting images

Ready for the site owner to generate photos and add to facts.js.

1. **Kakapo** (Birds) - A Parrot That Forgot to Fly. The kakapo is the world's only flightless parrot and the heaviest parrot species alive, with males weighing up to 4 kg. It's also nocturnal, unusual among parrots, and during mating season males make a deep booming call that can carry for miles through the forest. Kakapos are exceptionally long-lived, with individuals recorded living past 80 years. Visual hook: a moss-green, owl-faced kakapo perched low in dense forest undergrowth at night.
2. **Maine Coon** (Dogs & Cats) - Gentle Giant of Cats. Maine Coons are the largest domesticated cat breed, with males often weighing 15 to 25 pounds and measuring 40-plus inches from nose to tail. The longest Maine Coon on record, a cat named Stewie, measured 48.5 inches. Unlike most cats, they can take up to five years to reach their full adult size. Visual hook: a huge fluffy Maine Coon cat stretched out at full length beside a normal sized house cat for scale.
3. **Okapi** (Mammals) - The Giraffe's Only Cousin. The okapi is the sole living relative of the giraffe, despite the zebra like stripes on its legs and rear. Its tongue can stretch 14 to 18 inches long, long enough to lick its own eyes and ears clean. It lives only in the rainforests of the Democratic Republic of Congo, where its stripes work as camouflage in dappled light. Visual hook: an okapi's zebra striped hindquarters and its long dark tongue curling up toward its own eye in dense rainforest.
4. **Nudibranch** (Ocean) - Stolen Stingers. Some nudibranchs feed on stinging jellyfish and anemones, then steal the prey's undischarged stinging cells instead of digesting them. The cells pass intact through the nudibranch's gut into finger like projections on its back called cerata, where the slug redeploys them as its own defense. Scientists are still working out exactly how it keeps the stolen stingers from firing along the way. Visual hook: a brightly colored nudibranch with feathery cerata along its back, crawling near the anemone it fed on.
5. **Gila Monster** (Reptiles) - From Venom to Ozempic. The Gila monster is the only venomous lizard native to the United States, but its slow metabolism lets it get by on just a few meals a year, living off fat stored in its tail between hunts. A protein isolated from its venom, exendin-4, became the basis for early diabetes drugs and later helped inspire the GLP-1 medications behind Ozempic. Visual hook: a black and orange beaded Gila monster lizard emerging slowly from a desert burrow.
