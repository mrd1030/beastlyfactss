## Substrate section pass, 2026-11-01 (tank setup guide)

Added to "Substrate: The Decision That Matters Most", from sources already in the guide's block and re-opened this session: the reason for the 0.6 inch depth, finding food that has worked deep into the bed (Ian Fuller, Corydoras World: "this allows the catfishes to reach through to the base glass of the tank" to "find all the particles of food that have penetrated deep into the substrate"); sharp sand or gravel damaging barbels and leading to bacterial or fungal infection that may be fatal (Fuller); panda habitat "often flowing over sandy substrates", "Use a substrate of river sand", "don't use undergravel filtration and ensure the substrate is kept scrupulously clean", barbel loss over "poorly-maintained or dirty substrates", and breeding groups "less susceptible to disease when kept over a thin layer of sand, rather than in a bare-bottomed setup" (Seriously Fish, Corydoras panda). Cut as a repeat of the shared guide: "Fine substrates in general should go no deeper than 1 inch." (the shared aquarium substrate guide carries the 1 inch figure for fine sand, sourced to Matt Clarke). No new sources; no figures changed.
