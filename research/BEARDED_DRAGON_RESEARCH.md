`content/guides/bearded-dragon-feeding-guide.mdx`.

## 2026-11-03: feeding guide source cleanup

- Removed Hepper from the feeding guide's Sources block. Hepper is a pet product
  retailer's blog, which the no hobby sites rule (owner, 2026-11-01) keeps out of
  Sources. It was cited for "How Long Can a Bearded Dragon Go Without Eating" and
  "Bearded Dragon Brumation".
- Added two sources already verified for the safe foods guide, each backing a claim
  the feeding guide makes in its Foods to Avoid section: Merck Veterinary Manual,
  Avocado (Persea spp) Toxicosis in Animals (persin), and Knight and colleagues
  1999, Firefly Toxicosis in Lizards (fireflies). The guide now has 4 sources.
- Rewrote the inline source talk in the body and FAQs ("per veterinary sources",
  "Enthusiast sites cite", "a real tension in the sourcing", "common guidance",
  "Sources differ on the exact percentages") as plain statements, per RULES.md
  "The source goes in the block, not the sentence".
- Checked against the live pages on 2026-11-03: VCA "Brumation in Bearded
  Dragons" and VCA "Bearded Dragons: Feeding". The NC State page ("How to Feed
  Your Bearded Dragon") returned 503 to the cloud fetch, so its claims were not
  rechecked.

## Kept without a source (owner review)

- "A healthy adult can physically go 1 to 2 weeks without food" (FAQ and When to
  Worry): only source was Hepper. VCA Feeding and VCA Brumation do not state a
  fasting period. [needs a vet source or owner call]
- Brumation figures in Why Bearded Dragons Stop Eating and When to Worry: "typically
  starting between 1 and 3 years old", "commonly lasting 1 to 3 months", "consult a
  vet if it extends past roughly 3.5 months or the dragon loses more than about 10%
  of its starting body weight", "appetite should return within 2 to 3 weeks". Only
  source was Hepper; VCA Brumation states none of these figures. The site's
  brumation guide may carry its own sources for some of them. [needs a vet source
  or owner call]
- Hatchlings "3 to 5 feedings a day": the old text gave this as an enthusiast site
  figure next to the vet figure of 2 to 3. Taken out of the body with the source
  talk; the guide now says 2 to 3. [owner call: leave out or restore]

## Possible contradictions found (not changed)

- Fruit "capped around 5 to 10% of the diet": VCA Feeding appears to say fruit
  should be 10% to 20% of the plants fed, not of the whole diet. This came from a
  summary of the page's lower half, not its verbatim text, so recheck on the live
  page before changing the guide.
- Feeding schedule tiers: VCA Feeding gives "once or twice daily" for all young
  dragons and "every 24 to 72 hours" for adults, with no separate hatchling or 1 to
  4 month tiers. The tiers may come from NC State, which could not be rechecked.
