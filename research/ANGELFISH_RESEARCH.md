## Substrate section, 2026-11-01

Section kept and grown from 23 to 98 words. Pages opened 2026-11-01:
- Seriously Fish: Pterophyllum scalare (SB49, already in the block). Says nothing on substrate type. "Angels spawn on vertical surfaces such as slate, broad-leaved plants or even the aquarium glass." Hardness 0 to 15 dGH is the guide's existing figure from this page.
- Matt Clarke, "Frequently asked questions on aquarium substrates," Practical Fishkeeping (SB1, named author; added to the block, now 4), https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/features/frequently-asked-questions-on-aquarium-substrates/. Extracts: "Inert substrates are of particular importance to those keeping fishes that like soft water, such as Discus"; "Even standard pea gravel contains sufficient calcium to boost the pH and hardness"; "Coral sand and gravel is calcareous, so you should only use it in situations where you want the pH and hardness to be kept high." Applying the inert rule to angelfish is my inference from the guide's soft-water preference and 0 to 15 dGH.

Left out: "can be kept in a wide variety of setups, such as bare tanks, community tanks, and planted tanks" (SB64, Aquarium Co-Op, hobby site; no allowed source). No bare-tank line was written.
