# Easy-to-generate enrichment image prompts

Copy-paste ready. Every prompt below is lifted verbatim from IMAGE_PROMPTS.md,
grouped so you can work down a category without switching subject type.

**All are 3:2 at 1168x784.** Deliberately excluded, because you said they are
generating badly: the five remaining snakes, the four remaining geckos, and the
six remaining lizards (fire skink, green anole, green iguana, Jackson's chameleon,
uromastyx, veiled chameleon). Those 15 are listed at the bottom for when you have
time, and none of them block anything the others unblock.

# Fact photos, `public/assets/facts/`, 1168x784 (22)

Every fact on the site has its own photo except these. Ids 297 to 314 are a
contiguous block that got skipped (everything either side, 1 to 296 and 315 to
324, is done), and 325 to 328 are the new bald eagle set.

**All are 3:2 at 1168x784.** Never upscale: a frame that arrives at 1264x848 gets
resized down to 1168x784 and re-encoded through mozjpeg at quality 80, and one
that arrives smaller is installed at its native size instead of being enlarged.
A 1600x1067 pass was generated once and thrown away for exactly that reason, so
do not target it. One photo per fact, never shared between two facts
and never the same file as a Beastfile hero. The build stamps its own
"beastlyfacts.com" pill on these, so no prompt asks for text: if a frame comes
back with lettering on a prop, regenerate it.

Filename is given above each block. After installing, register the id in
FACT_IMAGES in BOTH `src/lib/data/factImages.js` and `public/_worker.js`.

## Bald eagle (4) — all four must differ from each other and from the Beastfile pair

### bald-eagle.jpg — id 325, the biggest nest in the tree
